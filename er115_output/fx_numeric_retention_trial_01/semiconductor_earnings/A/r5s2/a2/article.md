# Who Is the Star of AI? Let’s Shine a Spotlight on Those Who Connect Things, Too

If AI news became a movie, a shiny computer chip would be at the poster’s center. It would do hard math with ease. It might seem to say, “Leave the future to me.” But when I read about Broadcom, I looked at the one beside it. Wait a minute. Network, you get a lot of screen time too, don’t you?

The company’s CEO said demand is still very strong for custom AI chips and networking products. That means people want AI chips made for certain uses, and products linked to networks.

It would be a shame to leave saying, “Of course, AI chips are popular,” and go home. Wouldn’t that be like two performers on stage, but only one gets a bunch of flowers?

Of course, we should not miss the impressive numbers. Broadcom reported $16.7 billion in AI chip sales for this quarter. The CEO said the company expects $21.7 billion in sales next quarter.

Wow, the sequel is even bigger. But we should not mistake the movie preview for the movie. The $16.7 billion is what the company actually made this quarter. The $21.7 billion is what it expects next quarter, not money from sales already made.

Also, neither number is for all the company’s sales. Both numbers are for AI chip sales. When numbers are big, it is easy to clap and say only, “Wow!” But we should read the subtitles and see exactly what they mean.

Now that the numbers have caught our attention, let’s return to the CEO’s words. The CEO named not only custom AI chips, but “networking” too.

That does not mean we should make this a surprise ending: “The network was the real star all along!” This news alone does not show how much AI chip sales came from network products. We know the performers’ names, but not how long they are on stage.

Even so, we can change where we, the audience, look. When we hear AI, we can look beyond what makes answers. We can also look at the things around it that connect things. We do not need to lose sight of the main star. We only need to pull the camera back a little.

$16.7 billion, and next, $21.7 billion. After seeing how big these numbers are, let’s notice the performer beside them too. What is happening at the center of the spotlight? And what is in high demand beside it? AI news is much more interesting when we look at the supporting cast too.

## In one line
Broadcom’s CEO says demand is very strong for custom AI chips and networking. The article asks us to notice networks too, but gives no share of AI chip sales.
