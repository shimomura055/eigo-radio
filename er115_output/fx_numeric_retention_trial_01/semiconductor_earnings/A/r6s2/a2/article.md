# Is AI’s “Brain” Its Only Star? Let’s Cheer for the Work of “Connecting” Behind the Scenes

AI news today is once again a show of a star’s latest tricks. “It’s gotten smarter again!” “Can it do that, too?” The audience is cheering. But this time, let’s move the spotlight a little to the side. I’d like to notice the work of “connecting,” which was named along with the star.

The starting point was Broadcom’s earnings report. Sales of AI semiconductors came to $16.7 billion. The CEO said this was 221% higher than in the same period a year earlier. That number is too big to just read and move on. It makes you want to clap and say, “AI is booming!” But there is more to see.

The CEO said demand was still very strong for “custom AI accelerators” and “networking.” When two technical terms come together, it can feel like the audience has been left behind. For now, let’s keep two ideas in mind: “for AI” and “equipment for connecting things.”

The second idea catches my interest: “connecting.” If AI is the star of a live show, we usually watch its amazing performance. But in the earnings report, we also hear a voice from backstage: “There is strong demand over here, too!” Oh, so things are busy over there as well.

Of course, “connecting” sounds plain. Unlike a new feature, it does not easily make you say, “Wow!” at first look. Even so, the CEO named it as an area with strong demand. If we follow only the exciting answers, we may miss this part of the show.

But it is too soon to write a headline in our minds: “Connecting technology is the real reason sales jumped!” This report does not tell us how much each area sold. And “very strong demand” is the CEO’s own view. Let’s not give the lead actor award in all the excitement just yet. First, let’s separate what we know from what we don’t.

The company also expects its total sales to go up next quarter. But this is a forecast about the future. Unlike results that have already come in, the actual results may be different. “Sales have already happened” and “sales may rise from here” are different things. The more excited we get about strong numbers, the more we need to remember this difference.

The next time a new AI trick surprises you, look at the side that supports it, too. Along with asking, “What can it do?” ask, “What is in demand in businesses related to AI?” Starting with $16.7 billion, this time we’re looking at “connecting.” When we look backstage as well as at the stars, the earnings report can look a little different, like a show.

## In one line
Broadcom reported $16.7 billion in AI semiconductor sales, while its CEO said demand stayed very strong for custom AI accelerators and networking. This puts networking’s behind-the-scenes role in focus, though the report does not say how much money came from it.
