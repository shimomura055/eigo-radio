# S-2 Selection -- 03_japanese_lit

**Selected**: 注文の多い料理店 ("The Restaurant of Many Orders" / "Two Hunters at
the Strange Restaurant"), Kenji Miyazawa, Aozora Bunko card1927.html

**Why this one (1-2 lines)**: Of the 4 S-1 candidates (all well-known Aozora
authors), this is the most event-driven and plot-shaped -- a compact sequence of
escalating instructions building tension toward a reveal -- which fits an A2
short-story shape much better than the purely atmospheric Kajii candidate, and
gives a clearer "escalation" structure than the moral-dilemma-in-one-scene shape
of the Akutagawa/Dazai candidates.

See `rights_check.md` for the rights verification detail (including an important
caveat about this specific card's older page template).
