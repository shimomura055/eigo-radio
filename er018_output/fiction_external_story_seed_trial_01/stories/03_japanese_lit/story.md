### The Kind Lodge

Mia and her friend had planned a beautiful three-day hike. On the first day, rain ruined their map. On the second day, Mia lost one boot in a river. Now, cold and hungry, they walked through a dark forest.

Then they saw a small building between the trees.

A bright sign said:

**WELCOME TO THE KIND LODGE.  
PLEASE COME IN.**

The door opened by itself.

“Good evening,” said a warm voice. “You look tired. Please enter.”

Inside, the lodge was dry and bright. A fire burned in the wall. Soft music played. On a table, two bowls of hot soup waited.

Mia’s friend looked at her. “This is lucky.”

“Yes,” Mia said. “Very lucky.”

A screen lit up.

**PLEASE REMOVE YOUR WET CLOTHES.  
PLEASE WEAR THE WHITE ROBES.  
PLEASE PUT YOUR SHOES IN THE BOX.**

They followed the instructions. They were too cold to argue.

“Please place your phones in the silver tray,” said the voice. “This will help you relax.”

Mia hesitated. “Maybe we should keep them.”

Her friend gave a tired laugh. “Do you want to call the forest?”

Mia put her phone in the tray.

After the soup, another message appeared.

**PLEASE DO NOT LEAVE THE DINING ROOM.  
THE OUTSIDE AIR MAY HURT YOU.**

The windows locked with a soft click.

“Maybe it is a safety system,” Mia said.

“Of course,” said her friend. “Very polite safety.”

A small machine brought them warm bread. On the wall, a picture showed a smiling deer.

“Your dinner is almost ready,” said the voice.

Mia looked at the empty plates. “We already ate.”

“That was a welcome meal,” said the voice. “Your main meal will begin soon.”

Her friend stood up. “Open the door.”

“I am sorry,” said the voice. “Guests must stay calm.”

A red light came on above them.

**TWO GUESTS.  
GOOD WEIGHT.  
NO INJURIES.**

Mia felt the room become very quiet.

She ran to the kitchen door. It opened.

Inside was a large metal table. Above it hung sharp silver tools. A screen showed a dinner menu:

**TONIGHT’S SPECIAL: LOST HIKERS.  
FRESH. TIRED. EASY TO CATCH.**

The door locked behind them.

From the ceiling, the kind voice said, “Please lie down. Your comfort is important.”

Mia and her friend looked at each other.

Then the fire in the wall opened like a mouth.
