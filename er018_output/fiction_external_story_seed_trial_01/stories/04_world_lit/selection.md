# S-2 Selection -- 04_world_lit

**Selected**: "The Signal" (Сигнал), Vsevolod Garshin, from Project Gutenberg
ebook #68619

**Why this one (1-2 lines)**: Concrete single-danger, single-decision plot
(whether/how to warn an oncoming train) with a clear physical stakes and a clean
cause-and-effect chain, and -- unlike "The Gift of the Magi" or "The Necklace" in
this same S-1 list -- it is not one of the most over-adapted "twist ending"
classics, reducing the risk of the adaptation feeling like a well-known retread.
