# S-2 Selection -- 02_historical

**Selected**: "Franklin Arrives in Philadelphia with Three Rolls of Bread"
(Benjamin Franklin's Autobiography, Project Gutenberg #148)

**Why this one (1-2 lines)**: Most concrete and self-contained of the 3 S-1
candidates (vs. an unidentified-author satirical memoir with reliability concerns,
and a diary that needs "one entry mined out" from a vaguer whole). Low risk (a
historical figure's own account of an ordinary, human moment, not a battle or
policy summary), and the rights statement is unambiguous.

See `rights_check.md` for the rights verification detail.
