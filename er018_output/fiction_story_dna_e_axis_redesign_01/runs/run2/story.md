### The Memory Test

At three o’clock, the teacher gave the class a strange test.

“Write what happened in Room 17 on Friday,” the teacher said.

Room 17 had been locked since that day. No one was allowed near it.

Mina looked at the first question.

**What happened at 4:05?**

She wrote:

*The door was locked. We heard the bell. Nobody went inside.*

When the teacher collected the papers, Mina saw a few answers. They were all the same. Every student had written the same three sentences, with almost the same words.

Mina felt cold.

She did not remember writing them.

Then a thin piece of paper fell from her test book. It was old and folded many times.

The handwriting was hers.

At the top, there was a date: **Friday, 3:50**.

Mina read.

*We went into Room 17.*

*There was a girl under the teacher’s desk. She was not from our school. She had the key in her hand.*

*She said, “If you remember me, I can leave.”*

Mina’s hand began to shake.

The next line was darker. It looked as if she had pressed the pencil very hard.

*I told everyone to look at the wall. I told them to say, “The door was locked. Nobody went inside.” I made them say it again and again.*

*Then I said it too.*

Mina stopped breathing.

She remembered.

The class had stood in Room 17. The strange girl had smiled at them. The key had been warm in Mina’s hand. The girl had whispered Mina’s name.

Mina had been afraid. She had told everyone to forget. Somehow, her words had entered their minds. They had forgotten the girl.

Mina had made herself forget, too.

She looked again at the test. The questions were not normal questions.

**Why was the key warm?**

**Who was under the desk?**

**What did the girl say to you?**

The teacher had found the old paper. This test was made to break Mina’s false memory.

The teacher came and stood beside her desk.

“You remember now,” the teacher said quietly.

Mina looked at Room 17.

The lock began to turn.

From inside the room, a girl whispered, “Mina?”

Mina had forgotten one thing.

She had never told the girl to stay silent.
