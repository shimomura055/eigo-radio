# A New Bus Route Opens Near the Library

Starting next Monday, the city will open a new bus route between the train station and the library. The bus stop is on Park Street, next to the bakery. A bus will come every fifteen minutes from six in the morning until nine at night.

The city says the route will help students and older people. Many students go to the library after school. The library is on the second floor of the community center, and it has a quiet room for study. Until now, the nearest bus stop was a twenty-minute walk away.

Each Thursday, there will be a weekly meeting at the community center. Riders can tell the city what they think about the new route. The first meeting is at seven in the evening. A glass of water and a small snack will be free for everyone.

The ticket costs two dollars. A monthly pass costs thirty dollars, and students can buy a pass for fifteen dollars. People can buy tickets from the driver or from a machine at the station.

The city also wants to know how many people use the new bus. For the first month, drivers will count riders at each stop. The city will share the numbers on its website. If fewer than fifty people use the bus each day, the city may change the times.

For now, many residents say they are happy. One woman said she will take the bus every morning to work. A young student said he is excited to read books after school without a long walk.

## In one line
A new bus route will connect the train station with the library every fifteen minutes.
