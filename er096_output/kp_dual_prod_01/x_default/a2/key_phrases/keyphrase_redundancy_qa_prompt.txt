以下は、同じ記事から選ばれた5件のKey Phrase(英語学習ポッドキャストの
リスニング教材)です。5件から作れる全てのペア(10組)について、
それぞれ以下4つの観点で意味重複を判定してください(それぞれPASS=重複なし/
FAIL=重複あり)。

- meaning_overlap: 2つのkey_phraseが表す意味内容が、実質的に同じか(例:
  「注意を引く」と「注意の一部を奪う」は表現は違っても同じ「注意を奪われる」
  という概念を指しており、meaning_overlap=FAILとすべきです)
- usage_context_overlap: 2つの表現が使われる典型的な場面・文脈が、実質的に
  同じか
- grammatical_teaching_value_overlap: 2つの表現が学習者に教える文法・構文上の
  価値(例: 句動詞の型、比喩の型、コロケーションの型)が、実質的に同じか
- conceptual_role_overlap: 記事の中でこの2つの表現が担っている概念・役割が、
  実質的に同じか

is_near_duplicateは、上記4観点のうち2つ以上がFAILの場合、または1つでも
明確にFAILで「単なる言い換え、または同じ内容を別の比喩・表現で表しただけ」
と判断できる場合にtrueにしてください。reasoningには判定理由を1〜2文の
日本語で書いてください。

単に同じ記事・同じ話題について書かれているために表面的な語彙(記事固有の
固有名詞やトピック語)が共通することは、重複とはみなさないでください。
判定対象はあくまで、Key Phraseそのものが教える意味・用法・学習価値です。

pairsには、必ず10組全ての組み合わせを含めてください(rank_a < rank_b)。

【記事本文(参考、文脈確認用)】
# “Can’t Wait for the Next Episode”? Make One Yourself? “byUs” Lets You Make Stories with Characters You Love

“Wait, it ends here!?” Have you ever shouted that when a manga ends at an exciting moment? It says the story will continue next time. What will that character say next? What will happen to the two of them? It may be a shame to keep those ideas only in your head.

The company that runs LINE Manga has announced a new service. It is called “byUs,” an AI chat service for stories. It is pronounced “bias.” You can talk with characters you love. You can also make your own stories based on their works. You can leave the reader’s seat and step onto the stage of making stories.

If you thought, “Can’t you already talk with characters?” you noticed something important. LINE Manga already has a service called “Character Chat.” The company says byUs lets you do more than chat. You can also make stories more freely. So, you can ask not only, “What should we talk about next?” but also, “What should happen next?”

For example, you can continue an official story on the service. Or you can make your own story as a fan. “What if that person came here?” “If it were me, I’d want to see this happen.” The ideas that grew as you turned each page can now start your own stories. You may wait to see what happens next. Or you may use that time to think up what happens next.

You can also publish your fan stories, if you want to. You can keep them private, or let someone else read them. But things change when you make a story public. “Will people understand this story?” “I want to think a little more about what happens next.” Before you know it, you may think like a story-maker, though you began as a reader.

Of course, you cannot use just anything you like. Only works and characters approved by their original creators are allowed. The Help page also says the service has permission to use the original works it features. It has this permission through official agreements with their creators. This is the base that lets you enjoy asking, “What if?”

The service first offered two works and four characters in Japan. Here, “taking part in a story” means moving the story forward through conversations. It also means making a story as a fan. Reading a work is fun. Now, it is also fun to think up what happens next.

Until now, you could only say, “If it were me, I’d do it this way,” to yourself. With byUs, those words can help you start making a story. What will you say to a character you love? What will happen next in the story? So, where will your “next part” begin?

## In one line
LINE Manga has launched byUs, an AI service for chatting with approved characters and making stories from their works. Readers can imagine what happens next and make fan stories to keep private or share.
