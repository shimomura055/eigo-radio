# “Can’t Wait for the Next Episode”? Make One Yourself? “byUs” Lets You Shape Stories with Characters You Love

“Wait, it ends here!?” Have you ever blurted that out when a manga ends at an exciting moment and says the story will continue next time? What will that character say next? What will happen to the two of them? It might be a shame to let those ideas stay only in your head.

The company that runs LINE Manga has announced the launch of “byUs,” an AI story chat service. It is pronounced “bias.” You can enjoy talking with your favorite characters and make your own stories based on the works. You can take a step from the reader’s seat onto the stage of story-making.

If you thought, “Can’t you already talk with characters?” you’ve noticed something important. LINE Manga already has “Character Chat.” The company says the difference is that, as well as having conversations, you can make stories more freely. In other words, you can enjoy not only asking, “What should we talk about next?” but also, “What should happen next?”

For example, you can continue an official story in the service. Or you can make your own story as a fan. “What if that person showed up here?” “If it were me, I’d want to see this happen.” The ideas that grew with every turn of the page can now become a starting point for your own stories. The time spent waiting for what happens next may become time spent thinking up what happens next.

What’s more, you can publish the fan stories you make if you want to. You can enjoy them privately, or let someone else read them. But things change when you make a story public. “Will people understand this plot?” “I want to work on what happens next a little more.” You may find yourself thinking like a story-maker before you know it, even though you started out as a reader.

Of course, you can’t bring in just anything you like. Only works and characters approved by their original creators can be used. The Help page also explains that the service has permission to use the original works it features through formal agreements with their creators. This is the foundation that lets you enjoy freely imagining “what if.”

The service first offered two works and four characters in Japan. “Taking part in a story” here means moving the story forward through conversation or making a story as a fan. The fun of reading a work is joined by the fun of thinking up what happens next.

Until now, “If it were me, I’d do it this way” was something you could only say to yourself. With byUs, those words become a way into making a story. What will you say to a character you love? What will happen next in the story? So, where will your “next part” begin?

## In one line
LINE Manga has launched byUs, an AI service that lets readers chat with approved characters and create stories based on their works. It gives readers a way to imagine what happens next and make fan stories they can keep private or share.
