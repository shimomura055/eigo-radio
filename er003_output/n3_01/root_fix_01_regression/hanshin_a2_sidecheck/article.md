# One Swing Opens the Door, and Hanshin Never Lets It Close

On August 16, 2026, the Hanshin Tigers faced the Hiroshima Toyo Carp at Mazda Stadium.

Hanshin did not wait long to take control.

In the top of the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. It gave Hanshin a 2-0 lead before Hiroshima had its first turn at bat.

One swing had changed the shape of the game.

Hanshin starter Takuto Ihara then protected that early lead. He pitched five innings and gave up only two hits and one run. Hiroshima could not build a long attack against him.

The Carp scored their only run in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, but Hiroshima could not add another run after that.

Hanshin was not finished.

The Tigers added more runs in the seventh inning. They scored again in the eighth and moved far ahead. What had once been a small lead became a clear and comfortable victory.

The final score was:

**Hanshin 8, Hiroshima 1**

Hanshin took control with Sato’s early home run. Ihara kept the game steady from the mound, and the offense finished the job with late runs.

### ⭐ Ihara Made the First Swing Matter

An early home run does not always lead to a win. The other team can answer quickly. Ihara did not allow that to happen. His five strong innings turned Sato’s first-inning home run into a lead that Hanshin could keep and build on.

### ⭐ Hanshin’s Attack Stayed Active Late

Hanshin did not simply protect a small lead. Seishiro Sakamoto brought in runs in both the seventh and eighth innings. Torai Fushimi also came from the bench and drove in a run in the seventh. The game started with power, but late hitting made the result clear.

## In one line…

One big swing opened the game, five steady innings held it in place, and late hits turned Hanshin’s early lead into an **8-1 win**.
