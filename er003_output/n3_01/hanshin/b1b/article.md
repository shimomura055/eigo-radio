# A Fast Start, a Calm Finish: Hanshin Cruise Past Hiroshima 8–1

Hanshin did not need much time to take control at Mazda Stadium.

On August 16, 2026, the Tigers faced the Hiroshima Toyo Carp. In the first inning, Teruaki Sato stepped up and sent a two-run home run to center field.

With one swing, Hanshin had an early 2–0 lead. More importantly, the team had already decided the direction of the game.

Starting pitcher Takato Ihara then protected that advantage.

Ihara allowed only two hits and one run over five innings. He did not give Hiroshima many chances to build an attack. His steady work allowed Hanshin to play from ahead without losing control.

Hiroshima finally scored in the fifth inning. E. Montero hit a solo home run, cutting into the lead.

But that was the Carp’s only run of the night.

Hanshin stayed patient and waited for another chance to strike. That chance came late in the game. The Tigers added runs in both the seventh and eighth innings, turning a close contest into a comfortable victory.

By the end, there was no late drama and no need for a difficult escape.

The final score was clear:

**Hanshin Tigers 8, Hiroshima Toyo Carp 1**

It was a complete win built on a simple plan: score early, pitch well, and finish strongly.

### ⭐ Sato’s Home Run Changed the Shape of the Game

A first-inning home run does more than add runs to the scoreboard. It lets the starting pitcher work with a lead from the beginning. Sato’s two-run shot gave Ihara room to stay calm and focus on getting outs.

### ⭐ The Late Runs Showed Hanshin’s Full Strength

Hanshin did not depend only on its early lead. Seishiro Sakamoto delivered key hits, while pinch hitter Torai Fushimi also added an RBI hit. Their work helped turn a controlled performance into a one-sided result.

## In One Line…

**Hanshin opened the door with Sato’s powerful swing, and Ihara made sure Hiroshima could never walk back into the game.**
