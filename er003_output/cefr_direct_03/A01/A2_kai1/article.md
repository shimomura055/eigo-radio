# Argentina Strike Late to Break England’s World Cup Dream

England were only a few minutes away from the World Cup final.

Then Argentina changed everything.

The two teams met in the 2026 FIFA World Cup semi-final on July 15. The match started at 3 p.m. local time at Atlanta Stadium.

The first half ended without a goal. Both teams went into half-time with the score at 0-0.

England made the first move in the 55th minute. Morgan Rogers sent a low cross from the right side. Anthony Gordon met the ball at the far side and scored.

England led 1-0.

For the next 30 minutes, England held on to that lead. A place in the final was getting closer.

But Argentina did not stop.

In the 85th minute, Argentina used a short corner. Lionel Messi passed the ball across to Enzo Fernández. The midfielder shot from outside the penalty area and scored with his right foot.

It was now 1-1.

Then came the final blow.

In second-half stoppage time, at 90+2, Alexis Mac Allister hit the post. Messi collected the ball and sent a right-footed cross to the far side.

Lautaro Martínez rose and headed the ball into the goal. He had entered the match as a substitute in the 81st minute.

Argentina had turned the match around.

The final score was:

**England 1–2 Argentina**

Argentina moved on to face Spain in the World Cup final. England will play France in the third-place match.

### ⭐ Messi Won the Match Without Scoring

Messi did not score, but he created both Argentina goals. At 39 years and 21 days old, he also became the oldest outfield player in a World Cup semi-final. FIFA named him Player of the Match.

### ⭐ The Numbers Show Argentina’s Pressure

The score was close, but the match numbers show a wider difference. Argentina had 15 shots, while England had five. FIFA’s possession figures were 56.5 percent for Argentina and 35.2 percent for England. Another 8.2 percent was listed as “in contest.”

## In one line…

**England could almost see the final, but Messi opened the door—and Lautaro sent Argentina through it.**
