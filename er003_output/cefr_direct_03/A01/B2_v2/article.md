# 🏆 England Came Close—Then Messi Opened the Door for Argentina

For most of the afternoon in Atlanta, England appeared to have found a path to the World Cup final.

The 2026 semifinal kicked off at 3 p.m. local time on July 15. England and Argentina entered half-time at 0-0, with neither side able to break through.

Then, in the 55th minute, England struck.

Morgan Rogers sent a low cross from the right, and Anthony Gordon met it at the far side to give England a 1-0 lead.

For a while, the message seemed clear:

“Protect this lead, and the final is yours.”

But Argentina kept pushing—and Lionel Messi kept looking for an opening.

In the 85th minute, a short corner reached Messi. He moved the ball across to Enzo Fernández, who fired a right-footed shot from outside the penalty area. The score was suddenly 1-1.

Extra time appeared to be waiting.

Argentina had other plans.

In second-half stoppage time, Alexis Mac Allister hit the post. Messi recovered the ball and delivered a right-footed cross toward the far side. Lautaro Martínez, who had entered as a substitute in the 81st minute, rose to meet it with his head.

The clock showed 90+2.

The scoreboard showed:

**England 1–2 Argentina**

In a matter of minutes, England’s place in the final disappeared. Argentina completed the comeback and advanced to face Spain, while England moved into the third-place match against France.

### ⭐ Messi decided the game without scoring

Messi did not put the ball in the net, but he assisted both Argentine goals and was named Player of the Match. At 39 years and 21 days, he also became the oldest outfield player ever to appear in a World Cup semifinal.

### ⭐ The late comeback had been building

Argentina finished with 15 shots, while England managed five. The expected-goals figures also favored Argentina, 1.47 to 0.46. Those numbers do not guarantee a result, but they suggest that Argentina’s late pressure was more than a sudden burst of luck.

## In one line…

England could almost touch the final, but Messi found two late openings—and Argentina walked through the door.
