# One Lead, Two Late Blows: Argentina Break England’s Heart

England were only minutes away from the World Cup final.

Then Argentina changed the entire story.

The 2026 FIFA World Cup semi-final, Match 102, kicked off at 3 p.m. local time on July 15 at Atlanta Stadium. England faced Argentina, with a place in the final at stake.

The first half ended without a goal. Both teams went into the break with the score at 0-0.

The game finally opened up in the 55th minute. Morgan Rogers sent a low cross from the right, and Anthony Gordon met it at the far side. England were ahead 1-0, and the final suddenly looked close.

For the next half-hour, that single goal separated the teams.

But in the 85th minute, Argentina found a way back. After a short corner, Lionel Messi moved the ball across to Enzo Fernández. The midfielder struck with his right foot from outside the penalty area and made it 1-1.

The biggest moment was still to come.

In second-half added time, Alexis Mac Allister hit the post. Messi recovered the ball and delivered a right-footed cross toward the far side. Lautaro Martínez, who had entered as a substitute in the 81st minute, rose to meet it with his head.

The ball went in at 90+2.

**England 1–2 Argentina.**

Argentina had turned the semi-final around with two late goals. They moved into the final against Spain, while England were sent to the third-place match against France.

### Messi did not score, but he wrote the ending

Both Argentina goals came from Messi’s passes. At 39 years and 21 days, he became the oldest field player to appear in a World Cup semi-final. FIFA also named him the official Player of the Match.

### The numbers show Argentina’s growing pressure

Argentina took 15 shots during the match, while England managed five. Argentina also had more of the ball and spent more time finding space in the final third. The late comeback was dramatic, but it did not appear from nowhere.

## In one line…

England could see the final, but Messi found two late openings—and Argentina walked through the last one.
