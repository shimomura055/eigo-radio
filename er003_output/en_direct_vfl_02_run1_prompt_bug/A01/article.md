# 🏆 “So Close to the Final—Then Messi Turned the Semifinal on Its Head”

For England, the World Cup final seemed close enough to touch.

For Argentina, the match was never over.

At Atlanta Stadium, in front of 68,239 spectators, the two giants finished a tense first half locked at **0-0**. Neither side could find the breakthrough, but the semifinal exploded into life after the interval.

In the 55th minute, Morgan Rogers drove a low cross in from the right. Anthony Gordon arrived at the far post and finished it.

**England 1-0 Argentina.**

For a moment, England had one foot in the final.

But Argentina began to push forward with greater force. Jordan Pickford denied a header from Nico González, while Alexis Mac Allister later struck the post with another headed effort.

Still, England held on.

Then came the 85th minute.

From a short-corner sequence, Lionel Messi found Enzo Fernández, who struck from outside the penalty area to make it **1-1**.

And Argentina were not finished.

In the 90+2nd minute, Mac Allister hit the post again. Argentina recovered the ball, Messi delivered a right-footed cross, and substitute Lautaro Martínez rose to head home the winner.

**England 1-2 Argentina.**

No extra time. No penalties. Just a devastating late reversal.

## Semifinal Talking Points

### ⭐ Messi Won the Match Without Scoring

Messi did not put his own name on the scoresheet, but he assisted both Argentine goals and was named FIFA’s official Player of the Match.

At 39 years and 21 days, he also became the oldest field player to appear in a World Cup semifinal. On the biggest stage, his influence arrived through timing, vision and two decisive passes.

### ⭐ England’s Lead Became a Trap

The statistics reflected Argentina’s growing control: 63 percent possession, 14 shots to England’s five, and six corners to one.

Afterward, England coach Thomas Tuchel said his team had become too passive after scoring, failed to regain possession and allowed too many crosses, chances and shots.

The lead offered protection—but not escape.

## In One Line…

**“England saw the door to the final open, but Messi supplied the keys for Argentina to walk through it.”**

Argentina advanced to face Spain in the final. England were left to regroup for the third-place match against France.
