At midnight, a phone may still be next to a teenager’s bed.

But under a new UK government plan, the phone could become much quieter.

The government is planning new social media settings for 16- and 17-year-olds. From midnight to 6 a.m., covered social media services would use nighttime limits by default. Alerts and push notifications would be muted during these six hours.

The idea is simple:

“Give the night some quiet time.”

This would not be a strict social media ban. The settings would turn on automatically, but young users could change them.

This difference is important.

The plan would not make social media completely impossible to use at night. Instead, quiet would become the normal setting. A young person would need to choose to change it.

The rules are also not active yet.

As of August 12, 2026, they were still only a government plan. Parliament must approve the new rules before they can begin. The government expects the changes to start around spring 2027.

So phones will not suddenly become silent tonight.

But the UK is preparing to ask an important question: should social media keep calling young people back after midnight?
