At midnight, a teenager’s phone may still be beside the bed.

But under a new UK government plan, it may become much quieter.

The government plans to introduce nighttime social media settings for 16- and 17-year-olds. From midnight to 6 a.m., covered services would use nighttime restrictions by default. These would include muting alerts and push notifications.

The message is simple:

“Night should be a time to switch off.”

However, this would not be a complete social media ban. The settings would turn on automatically, but young users could change them.

In other words, the government does not plan to make teenagers’ phones unusable at night. It wants quiet settings to become the normal starting point, instead of allowing notifications to keep calling users back.

The plan is also not in force yet.

As of August 12, 2026, it still needed approval from Parliament. The government expects the changes to begin around spring 2027.

So nothing changes tonight. But the UK is preparing to make nighttime social media a little less difficult to ignore.
