## B1 Key Words / Phrases (A01)

1. stoppage time — アディショナルタイム
   source: Then stoppage time began.
2. take a player off — 選手を交代で下げる
   source: England took off players including Rice and made its defense stronger.
3. shot on target — 枠内シュート
   source: Neither team had a shot on target before the break.
4. close the door to — ～への道を閉ざす
   source: England tried to close the door to the final.
5. narrow lead — わずかなリード
   source: The team wanted to protect its narrow lead.
