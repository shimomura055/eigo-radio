## B1 Key Words / Phrases (A01)

1. opt out — 参加・適用を断る
   source: Teenagers aged 16 and 17 could change the settings and opt out.
2. covered apps — 規制対象のアプリ
   source: During these hours, covered apps would be unavailable under the standard settings.
3. the urge to — ～したい衝動
   source: The government hopes this small extra step will slow the urge to watch “just one more” video.
4. personalized feed — 個人向けに選ばれた投稿欄
   source: Personalized feeds would also stop during this period.
5. digital switch-off period — デジタル利用の停止時間帯
   source: On July 15, the British government announced a planned digital switch-off period for 16- and 17-year-olds.
