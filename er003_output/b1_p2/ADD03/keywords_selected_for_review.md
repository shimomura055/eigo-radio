## B1 Key Words / Phrases (A01)

1. blockade — 海上封鎖
   source: A blockade against ships linked to Iran was still in place.
2. be in place — 実施中である、効力を保つ
   source: A blockade against ships linked to Iran was still in place.
3. freedom of navigation — 航行の自由
   source: It also went directly against the basic principle of freedom of navigation.
4. tollbooth — 料金所
   source: Imagine a giant tollbooth suddenly appearing in the Strait of Hormuz.
5. smell of gunpowder — 火薬のにおい、戦争の気配
   source: “The tollbooth sign is gone, but the smell of gunpowder still hangs over the strait.”
