## B1 Key Words / Phrases (A01)

1. Strait of Hormuz — ホルムズ海峡
   source: He wanted a 20 percent fee on all cargo using the Strait of Hormuz.
2. blockade — 封鎖、通行遮断
   source: A blockade against Iran-linked ships was still in place.
3. drop the fee — 料金案を取り下げる
   source: Trump dropped the fee one day later.
4. Brent crude oil — ブレント原油
   source: The day before, Brent crude oil rose by about 10 percent.
5. be in place — 実施中である、存続している
   source: A blockade against Iran-linked ships was still in place.
