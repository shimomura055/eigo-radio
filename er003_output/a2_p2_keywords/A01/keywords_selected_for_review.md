## B1 Key Words / Phrases (A01)

1. shot on target — 枠内シュート
   source: Neither team had a shot on target before half-time.
2. go ahead — リードする
   source: England went ahead in the 55th minute.
3. come on — 交代出場する
   source: Striker Lautaro Martínez came on in his place.
4. go out — 敗退する
   source: England went out after coming very close to the final.
5. turn the game around — 試合を逆転する
   source: Argentina turned the game around in seven minutes and ended England’s dream.
