以下は、同じ記事から選ばれた9件のKey Phrase(英語学習ポッドキャストの
リスニング教材)です。9件から作れる全てのペア(36組)について、
それぞれ以下4つの観点で意味重複を判定してください(それぞれPASS=重複なし/
FAIL=重複あり)。

- meaning_overlap: 2つのkey_phraseが表す意味内容が、実質的に同じか(例:
  「注意を引く」と「注意の一部を奪う」は表現は違っても同じ「注意を奪われる」
  という概念を指しており、meaning_overlap=FAILとすべきです)
- usage_context_overlap: 2つの表現が使われる典型的な場面・文脈が、実質的に
  同じか
- grammatical_teaching_value_overlap: 2つの表現が学習者に教える文法・構文上の
  価値(例: 句動詞の型、比喩の型、コロケーションの型)が、実質的に同じか
- conceptual_role_overlap: 記事の中でこの2つの表現が担っている概念・役割が、
  実質的に同じか

is_near_duplicateは、上記4観点のうち2つ以上がFAILの場合、または1つでも
明確にFAILで「単なる言い換え、または同じ内容を別の比喩・表現で表しただけ」
と判断できる場合にtrueにしてください。reasoningには判定理由を1〜2文の
日本語で書いてください。

単に同じ記事・同じ話題について書かれているために表面的な語彙(記事固有の
固有名詞やトピック語)が共通することは、重複とはみなさないでください。
判定対象はあくまで、Key Phraseそのものが教える意味・用法・学習価値です。

pairsには、必ず36組全ての組み合わせを含めてください(rank_a < rank_b)。

【記事本文(参考、文脈確認用)】
You think I am mad, but I am only nervous. Illness made my senses stronger, especially my hearing. I hear everything in heaven and on earth. Listen, and judge how calmly I tell you this.

I loved the old man who lived with me. He never hurt me, and I wanted none of his money. But a cloudy film covered his pale blue eye. That vulture’s eye made my blood turn cold. I had to get free of it.

At midnight for seven nights, I slowly opened his door. You should have seen my patience—he never even moved! Then I let one thin ray escape from my lantern.

But I always found that eye shut.

I could not act: I hated his eye, not him. Every morning, I kindly asked about his night’s sleep.

On night eight, I almost laughed at my own cleverness. Then my thumb slipped on the catch of my lantern.

“Who is in here?” he shouted.

For an hour, he sat listening while I stood completely still. Finally, he groaned softly in fear, not pain. I knew that sound well.

I opened the lantern’s cover, sending light onto his eye.

It was completely open now.

I heard low, quick beats, like a watch wrapped in cotton. His heart grew faster and louder—what if a neighbour heard?

I rushed at him, and he gave one scream. I pulled him down and covered him with the heavy bed. Finally his heart stopped, and I could feel no pulse.

I hid his body under three floorboards, then replaced them exactly. You could search all night and find no mark.

At four that morning, three police officers arrived and knocked. A neighbour had heard a scream from the house.

I welcomed them and blamed my scream on a bad dream. I said the old man was away in the country. After showing every room, I brought chairs into his bedroom. You see, I sat right above the body without fear.

They talked in a friendly way while I smiled.

My head started hurting, and I heard a ringing sound. No—it was that low, quick beating once again.

I raised my voice, but the beating grew louder too.

I walked about and scraped my chair across the floorboards. They went on talking and smiling as before.

The sound kept growing louder and louder!

Could you bear that? They heard and knew—they were laughing at me! I could not stand any more.

“I killed him!” I screamed. “Pull up the floorboards here! That is the beating of his horrible heart!”
