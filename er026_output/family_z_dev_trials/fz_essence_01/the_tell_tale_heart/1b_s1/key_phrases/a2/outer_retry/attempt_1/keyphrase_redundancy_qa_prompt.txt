以下は、同じ記事から選ばれた3件のKey Phrase(英語学習ポッドキャストの
リスニング教材)です。3件から作れる全てのペア(3組)について、
それぞれ以下4つの観点で意味重複を判定してください(それぞれPASS=重複なし/
FAIL=重複あり)。

- meaning_overlap: 2つのkey_phraseが表す意味内容が、実質的に同じか(例:
  「注意を引く」と「注意の一部を奪う」は表現は違っても同じ「注意を奪われる」
  という概念を指しており、meaning_overlap=FAILとすべきです)
- usage_context_overlap: 2つの表現が使われる典型的な場面・文脈が、実質的に
  同じか
- grammatical_teaching_value_overlap: 2つの表現が学習者に教える文法・構文上の
  価値(例: 句動詞の型、比喩の型、コロケーションの型)が、実質的に同じか
- conceptual_role_overlap: 記事の中でこの2つの表現が担っている概念・役割が、
  実質的に同じか

is_near_duplicateは、上記4観点のうち2つ以上がFAILの場合、または1つでも
明確にFAILで「単なる言い換え、または同じ内容を別の比喩・表現で表しただけ」
と判断できる場合にtrueにしてください。reasoningには判定理由を1〜2文の
日本語で書いてください。

単に同じ記事・同じ話題について書かれているために表面的な語彙(記事固有の
固有名詞やトピック語)が共通することは、重複とはみなさないでください。
判定対象はあくまで、Key Phraseそのものが教える意味・用法・学習価値です。

pairsには、必ず3組全ての組み合わせを含めてください(rank_a < rank_b)。

【記事本文(参考、文脈確認用)】
You think I am mad, but my mind is well. My illness made my senses stronger, especially my hearing. I hear everything in heaven and on earth. Listen, and tell me how calmly I explain it.

I loved the old man who shared my house. He never hurt me, and I wanted none of his money. Only his pale blue eye with its cloudy covering troubled me. It looked like a vulture's eye, you understand? I had to get rid of that eye.

At midnight, for seven nights, I silently opened his door. I shone a thin line of light from my lantern. His eye stayed shut, so I could do nothing. You see, I hated his eye, not him.

On night eight, I smiled at how patiently I worked. Then my thumb slipped against the lantern's catch.

He sat up in bed and called, “Who is there?”

You must admire how I stood still for an hour. He stayed awake, listening, then gave a low, frightened groan. I knew that sound from my own nights without sleep.

Finally, I let a little light out of the lantern. It fell straight onto his eye.

That eye was now completely open.

I heard his heart beating softly, but very quickly. It sounded like a watch covered in cotton. It grew faster and louder; a neighbour might hear!

I rushed inside, and he gave one sharp scream. I dragged him down and pulled the heavy bed over him. When his heart stopped, I checked: he was dead.

I hid his body under three boards in the floor. I put them back perfectly, leaving no marks or stains. You wouldn't expect a madman to work so neatly!

Three police officers came knocking at four in the morning. A neighbour had heard a scream, but I welcomed them. I said a bad dream had made me cry out. The old man was visiting the country, I told them.

After showing them every room, I put chairs in his bedroom. I sat right above the body; you see my courage?

As they chatted, a ringing sound began in my ears. Then, below their voices, I heard that soft, quick beating.

I spoke louder, stood up, and walked around the room. I dragged my chair across the boards, but the beating grew louder.

They kept smiling and talking, as if they heard nothing. But they must hear it; they knew what I'd done! Their smiles were laughing at me. How could you expect me to bear it?

“I killed him!” I screamed. “Pull up the floorboards, here under my chair! That is the sound of his horrible heart beating!”
