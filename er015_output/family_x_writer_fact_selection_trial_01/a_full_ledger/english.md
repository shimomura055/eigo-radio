# We Thought It Was an AI Voice. Behind the Scenes, a Human Was Speaking

Here is a small mystery.

You ask an AI that can make phone calls to book a hair appointment, ask a store if it has something in stock, or get price estimates from companies. The AI makes the calls for you.

That was the idea behind Muse, Meta’s personal AI agent, announced in the United States on September 8, 2026. Muse can also send email, book trips, and use websites. It sounds as if a skilled secretary has moved into your smartphone.

But that secretary had something happening behind the scenes.

During Meta’s internal tests, some calls received by Muse were handed over to trained human staff. The staff actually called the stores and companies. When the calls ended, they sent the users a record and a summary of the conversation. The AI was onstage, but humans were waiting in the wings. Behind the AI phone service, there were human helpers.

The feature was tested with Meta employees starting in August 2026. By the middle of September, about half of the employees could use it. In some tests, calls handled by humans appeared to have a possible success rate of 95 to 98 percent.

Why hand the calls to humans? Sometimes the person on the other end realized that the caller was an AI and hung up. The AI might be able to make the call, but if the other person did not want to talk to an AI, the game was over. The call had to be passed to a human voice.

But this created a new mystery: Who, exactly, was hearing what the user said?

Some employees worried that personal information and important details from their calls might be heard by human workers without their knowledge. In fact, one call record included an inappropriate remark about race by a human worker.

A Meta executive admitted that it was a problem for humans to start making calls without enough explanation, and the company temporarily rolled the feature back. Meta has not yet decided when or how calls handled by humans will be offered to the public. At least for now, reports clearly confirm mainly the internal tests.

When we hear about AI advancing, we often imagine a future without humans. But Muse’s experiment showed something slightly different: humans may not disappear. They may be working hard behind a service wearing an AI costume. After convenience, the next question may not be how naturally AI can speak, but whether the person inside the voice clearly says who they are.

## In one line

Muse looked like an AI phone service, but behind some calls were human workers—and that raised questions about privacy and honesty.
