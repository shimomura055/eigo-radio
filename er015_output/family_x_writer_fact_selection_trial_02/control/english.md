# I Asked AI to Make a Call—and a Human Came Out from Behind the Stage

“Let AI handle this call.” If you imagine a scene from the future, you might expect AI to take care of everything. But the next moment, a human staff member steps out from behind the stage. Meta had been testing a slightly strange system like this inside the company.

Meta’s personal AI service, “Muse,” has a feature that can make phone calls for users. But in one experiment, contracted human staff handled some of those calls behind the scenes. Meta called the system “human concierge.”

It was like a stage show with AI as the star. From the caller’s side, the AI service appeared to handle the call. But when the situation became difficult, a human staff member waiting behind the stage quietly stepped out and made the call instead. To the audience, it looked like an AI show. Behind the stage, however, a human was speaking the lines.

You could also see this as a way for humans to support work that AI still cannot do well on its own. Inside a service advertised as AI, old-fashioned phone support was hidden. That makes the system feel surprisingly familiar, even though it was presented as a story about the future.

But then privacy stopped the show. Meta employees raised concerns that the content of the calls could be passed outside the company. Phone calls can include not only reservations and questions, but also personal matters. If people do not know who is listening, or how much of the conversation is being seen, it is natural for them to feel worried.

According to internal posts reviewed by Reuters, a Meta executive said the company had stopped the feature for the time being.

The interesting point in this story is not whether AI has surpassed humans. It is that humans were working behind the stage to create the magic of AI—and that the company then faced a question about how to tell users what was happening backstage.

As people have more chances to leave phone calls and bookings to AI, convenience will not be the only thing that matters. Knowing who is actually handling the task may also become an important part of trust in the service.

## In one line

The AI show depended on human help backstage, and privacy concerns brought the performance to a stop.
