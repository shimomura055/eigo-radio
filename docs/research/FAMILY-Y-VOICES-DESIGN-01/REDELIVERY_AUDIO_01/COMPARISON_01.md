## パート1: リード(語り手・Aoede)

### 日本語(R0無変更+補完)

留守で受け取れなかった荷物を、もう一度届けてもらう。その再配達に、いつもの配送料とは別に受け取る側がお金を払うとしたら、どうだろう。ここに、立場も暮らしも違う三人の声を並べる。これから聞くのは、実在の特定の人の話ではない。ネットなどで公開されている複数の意見をもとに、組み立てた声だ。

### Advanced(忠実英訳)

If you miss a package because you’re not home, you can have it delivered again. What if the person receiving it had to pay for that redelivery, on top of the usual delivery fee? Here are the voices of three people with different lives and points of view. These are not the stories of specific real people. They are voices created from several opinions shared online and elsewhere.

### Standard(A2簡易化)

If you’re not home when a package comes, you can have it delivered again. What if the person getting it had to pay for this, as well as the usual delivery fee? Here are the views of three people with different lives and points of view. These are not stories about specific real people. They are voices made from several opinions shared online and in other places.

## パート2: Voice 1 現役の宅配ドライバー(Algieba)

### 日本語(R0無変更+補完)

不在票を入れて帰るときは、私もやはり少し気持ちが沈む。でも、そのまま次の家へ向かう。再配達はできれば減ってほしいし、きついのも本当だ。それでも、その費用をお客さんに払ってもらえばいい、とは割り切れないんだ。私は、受け取れなかったお客さんごとに、その再配達にかかった分を請求するより、荷物を運ぶための料金全体で受け止めるほうが、筋が通るんじゃないかと思う。そのためには、通販の送料無料も、そのままでいいのか、会社の側で考えてもらいたい。送料を見直すなら、その分がちゃんと運ぶ人に回るように、料金の組み方まで見直してほしい。

### Advanced(忠実英訳)

When I leave a delivery notice and head off, I do feel a little down. But then I go on to the next house. I wish there were fewer redeliveries, if possible, and it’s true that they’re hard on us. Even so, I can’t just say that customers should pay for them. I think it makes more sense to cover the cost through the overall fee for delivering packages than to charge each customer who missed a delivery for that redelivery. To do that, I’d also like companies to think about whether free shipping on online orders should stay as it is. If they review shipping fees, I want them to review how the fees are set, too, so that the money actually goes to the people delivering the packages.

### Standard(A2簡易化)

When I leave a delivery notice and head off, I do feel a little down. But then I go on to the next house. I wish there were fewer redeliveries, if possible. They really are hard on us. Even so, I can’t just say customers should pay for them. I think it makes more sense to put the cost into the overall fee for package delivery. That is better than charging each customer who missed a delivery for redelivery. I’d also like companies to think about free shipping on online orders. Should it stay as it is? If they look again at shipping fees, I want them to look at how the fees are set, too. I want the money to really go to the people delivering the packages.

## パート3: Voice 2 共働きの受取人(Zephyr)

### 日本語(R0無変更+補完)

再配達でお金を払うとなると、どうも引っかかるんだよね。わたしも夫も仕事で家を空けるから、指定できる荷物は確実に家にいる日と時間にしてる。でも、午前の時間帯を指定したのに来なくて、用事を済ませに出て、戻ったら不在票が入っていたことがある。時間指定できない荷物も多くて、いつ届くか事前に分からない。お金を取る前に、指定した時間に来ることと、届く日時を知らせることを、まず何とかしてほしい。配達員さんも人手が足りなくて大変なんだろうし、仕方ないのかなとも思う。でも、そこでわたしが払うのは納得できない。そう思うわたしが悪いの？

### Advanced(忠実英訳)

It just doesn’t sit right with me to pay for redelivery. My husband and I are both out of the house for work, so for packages where I can choose, I pick a day and time when I know I’ll be home. But once, I chose a morning time slot and the package didn’t come. I went out to take care of an errand, and when I got back, there was a delivery notice. Many packages don’t let you choose a delivery time, either, so you don’t know ahead of time when they’ll arrive. Before charging us, I want them to first do something about showing up at the time we chose and telling us when the package will arrive. The delivery workers are probably having a hard time because they’re short-staffed, and maybe it can’t be helped. But I can’t accept having to pay for that. Am I wrong to feel that way?

### Standard(A2簡易化)

It just doesn’t feel right to me to pay for redelivery. My husband and I are both out of the house for work. For packages where I can choose, I pick a day and time when I know I’ll be home. But once, I chose a morning delivery time slot, and the package didn’t come. I went out to do an errand. When I got back, there was a delivery notice. Many packages don’t let you choose a delivery time, either. So you don’t know ahead of time when they’ll arrive. Before they charge us, I want them to first do a better job of coming at the time we chose. I also want them to tell us when the package will arrive. The delivery workers are probably having a hard time because there are too few of them. Maybe nothing can be done about that. But I can’t accept having to pay for it. Am I wrong to feel that way?

## パート4: Voice 3 受取方法を工夫している受取人(Schedar)

### 日本語(R0無変更+補完)

僕は再配達で迷惑をかけないよう、営業所やロッカーで受け取るようにしている。自分の都合で取りに行けるのは便利なんだけど、商品によってはロッカーを選べないし、営業所に頼んでも届くのが遅れて、結局は対面で受け取ることもある。それでも、こっちは受け取り方を考えて手間をかけているのに、いつも不在で再配達を頼む人と同じ料金なのは、正直おかしいと思う。配達する人の時間を使うんだから、再配達は有料でいい。最初の再配達は無料にして、くり返し不在になる人から払ってもらう形なら納得できる。ただ、これは営業所が近くて、受け取り方を選べる僕の言い分かもしれないけど。

### Advanced(忠実英訳)

I try to pick up packages at a delivery office or a locker so I don’t cause trouble with redelivery. It’s convenient to pick them up when it suits me, but some products can’t be sent to a locker. And even if I ask for a package to be sent to a delivery office, it can arrive late, so in the end I sometimes have to receive it in person. Still, I think it’s honestly wrong that I pay the same fee as people who are always out and ask for redelivery, when I’m the one thinking about how to receive my packages and taking the trouble. Redelivery uses the delivery worker’s time, so I think it’s fine to charge for it. I’d be okay with making the first redelivery free and having people who are repeatedly out pay for it. Though maybe that’s just my view, since the delivery office is nearby and I can choose how to receive my packages.

### Standard(A2簡易化)

I try to pick up packages at a delivery office or a pickup locker. That way, I don’t cause trouble with redelivery. It’s handy to pick them up when it works for me. But some products can’t be sent to a pickup locker. Even if I ask for a package to go to a delivery office, it can arrive late. So sometimes, in the end, I have to receive it in person. Still, I honestly think it’s wrong that I pay the same fee as people who are always out and ask for redelivery. I’m the one who thinks about how to get my packages and takes the trouble. Redelivery uses the delivery worker’s time, so I think it’s fine to charge for it. I’d be okay with making the first redelivery free. People who are repeatedly out could pay for it. Maybe that’s only my view, though, because the delivery office is nearby and I can choose how to get my packages.

## パート5: Tension+Closing(語り手・Aoede)

### 日本語(R0無変更+補完)

不在票を入れる人も、戻ってそれを見る人も、ロッカーへ寄る人も、再配達は減ってほしい。運ぶ人に回るお金、午前に待つ約束、ロッカーへ行く手間の報われ方と、大事にしたいものは違う。くり返す不在の家に払ってもらうか、送料全体で受け止めるかでは、答えが分かれる。午前に届かない荷物も、ロッカーを選べない荷物も、受け取る側の工夫だけでは追いつかない。あなたは、次の荷物が届くはずの日、どこで、何をしながら待っている？

### Advanced(忠実英訳)

The person who leaves a delivery notice, the person who comes back and sees it, and the person who stops by a locker all want fewer redeliveries. What matters to them is different: money going to the people delivering packages, the promise of waiting for a morning delivery, and how the effort of going to a locker is rewarded. They disagree on whether people who are repeatedly out should pay, or whether the cost should be covered by the overall shipping fee. Packages that don’t arrive in the morning and packages that can’t be sent to lockers are problems the person receiving them can’t solve through their own efforts alone. On the day your next package is supposed to arrive, where will you be, and what will you be doing while you wait?

### Standard(A2簡易化)

The person who leaves a delivery notice, the person who comes back and sees it, and the person who stops at a pickup locker all want fewer redeliveries. They care about different things: money for package delivery workers, waiting for a promised morning delivery, and how the effort of going to a pickup locker is rewarded. They disagree about whether people who are repeatedly out should pay, or whether the cost should be covered by the overall shipping fee. Some packages do not arrive in the morning. Some cannot be sent to pickup lockers. The person receiving them cannot solve these problems alone. On the day your next package is supposed to arrive, where will you be? What will you be doing while you wait?

## In One Line

### Advanced

Three people all want fewer package redeliveries, but disagree over who should pay for them. Customers cannot always prevent them on their own, because deliveries may miss promised times or offer no locker option.

### Standard

Three people all want fewer package redeliveries, but they do not agree on who should pay. Customers cannot always prevent redeliveries on their own. Packages may not arrive at the promised time, and some cannot be sent to a pickup locker.
