What if it cost money to have a package delivered again when you were not home at the time it arrived? Charging for redelivery means the person receiving the package pays a fee on top of the usual shipping cost. How should we decide whether to charge a fee or reduce redeliveries in another way? Let’s hear from three people with different lives and points of view.

Y 導入/結び / y_intro_1

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

First to speak is a delivery driver who visits homes every day and delivers packages.

本文 / y_voice_1

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

When I leave a notice saying nobody was home and head off, I feel a little down, too. But then I go to the next house. I really would like to see fewer redeliveries, and they are hard on us, too. Even so, I can’t just say that customers should pay the cost. I think it makes more sense to cover the cost through the overall fee for carrying packages than to charge each customer who wasn’t home for the cost of that redelivery. To do that, I want the company to think about whether free shipping for online orders should stay as it is. If they review shipping fees, I want them to review how the fees are set, too, so that the money really reaches the people who deliver the packages.

Y 導入/結び / y_intro_2

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

Next is someone who receives packages and is often away from home because both she and her husband work.

本文 / y_voice_2

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

Having to pay for redelivery just doesn’t sit right with me. My husband and I are both away at work, so for packages where we can choose a delivery time, we choose a day and time when we know we’ll be home. But once, I chose a morning time slot and the package didn’t come. I went out to take care of an errand, and when I got back, there was a missed-delivery notice. There are also many packages where you can’t choose a time, and you don’t know beforehand when they’ll arrive. Before charging us, I want them to first do something about arriving at the time we chose and telling us when the package will arrive. The delivery workers must have a hard time because there aren’t enough of them, and I guess it can’t be helped. But I can’t accept having to pay for that. Am I wrong to feel this way?

本文 / y_voice_3

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

I try to pick up packages at a delivery office or locker so I don’t cause trouble with redelivery. It’s convenient to pick them up when it suits me, but for some items, I can’t choose a locker. Still, I take the trouble to think about how to receive my packages, so honestly, I think it’s wrong that I pay the same fee as people who are always away and ask for redelivery. Redelivery takes up the delivery worker’s time, so it’s fine to charge for it. I’d be happy if the first redelivery were free, and people who are repeatedly away had to pay. Y 導入/結び / y_closing

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

A missed-delivery notice was left at the home of someone who had chosen a morning time slot, too. For people who can choose how to receive packages, a redelivery fee can be a signal that encourages them to make an effort. But having the person receiving the package pay won’t change late deliveries or how arrival times are shared. The person receiving the package can’t change how much shipping costs or how much of it goes to the people who deliver the packages, either. When you couldn’t be home and had a problem, could you choose how to receive your package? Did you know when it would arrive? Is that a problem that can be changed by charging the person receiving the package?
