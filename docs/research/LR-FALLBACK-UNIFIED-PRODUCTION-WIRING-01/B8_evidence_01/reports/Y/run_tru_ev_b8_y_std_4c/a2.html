What if you had to pay when a package came again because you were not home? A redelivery fee is extra money paid by the person getting the package. It is added to the usual delivery cost. How should we decide whether to charge this fee or cut repeat deliveries another way? Let’s hear from three people with different lives and views.

Next, hear from a woman who gets packages and is often away. She and her husband both work.

本文 / y_voice_2

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

Having to pay for a redelivery just doesn’t feel right to me. My husband and I are both away at work. So, when we can choose a delivery time, we choose a day and time when we know we’ll be home. But once, I chose a morning time, and the package did not come. I went out to do an errand. When I came back, there was a note saying they had tried to deliver it. There are also many packages where we cannot choose a time. We do not know when they will come, either. Before they charge us, I want them to fix two things: come at the time we chose and tell us when the package will arrive. Delivery workers must have a hard time because there are not enough of them. I guess it can’t be helped. But I can’t accept having to pay for that. Am I wrong to feel this way?

Lastly, hear from someone who picks up packages at a locker or another place to avoid another delivery.

本文 / y_voice_3

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

I try to pick up packages at a delivery office or a locker so I don’t cause extra work with redelivery. It is handy to pick them up when it suits me. But I can’t choose a locker for some things. Even when I ask for a package to go to a delivery office, it may arrive late. So sometimes I end up getting it in person after all. Still, I take the time to think about how to get my packages. Honestly, I think it’s wrong that I pay the same fee as people who are always away and ask for redelivery. Redelivery takes up the delivery worker’s time, so charging for it is fine. I’d be happy if the first redelivery were free, and people who were away again and again had to pay. But maybe that’s just my view. The delivery office is nearby, and I can choose how to get my packages.

Y 導入/結び / y_closing

TTS未実行(このrunの対象外: SKIPPED_NOT_SELECTED)

A note saying delivery was missed was left at the home of someone who had chosen a morning time, too. For people who can choose how to get packages, a redelivery fee can be a sign that encourages them to make an effort. But making the person who gets the package pay will not fix late deliveries or the way people are told when packages will arrive. People who get packages cannot change the delivery cost. They also cannot decide how much of it goes to delivery workers. When you could not be home and had a problem, could you choose how to get your package? Did you know when it would arrive? Can charging the person who gets the package fix that problem?
