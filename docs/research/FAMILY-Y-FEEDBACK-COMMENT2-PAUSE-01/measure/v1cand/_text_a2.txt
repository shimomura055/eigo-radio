When I leave a note saying no one was home, I feel a little sad, too. But then I go to the next house. I really want fewer redeliveries. They are hard on us, too. Even so, I can’t simply say customers should pay the cost. I think the cost should be part of the general fee for carrying packages. That makes more sense than charging each person who was not home for their redelivery. I want the company to think about whether free delivery for online orders should stay as it is. If it changes delivery fees, I want it to look at how it sets them, too. Then the money can really reach the people who deliver packages.
