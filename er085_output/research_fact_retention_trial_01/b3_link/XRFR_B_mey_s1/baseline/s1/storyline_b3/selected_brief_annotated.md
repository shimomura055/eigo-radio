# Selected Fact Brief

## Storyline
国連特使はイエメンの全面戦争への逆戻りを報告し、中東の不安定化とイエメン情勢が相互に影響しているとの認識を示した。

## Selected Facts
- 【事実1】Hans Grundberg, the UN Secretary-General’s Special Envoy for Yemen, said Yemen had slid back into full-scale war over the preceding few weeks, with hostilities spreading across multiple frontlines.（範囲：Yemen; multiple frontlines。条件：【主体】Hans Grundberg, Special Envoy of the Secretary-General for Yemen）
- 【事実2】The Special Envoy said ongoing instability in the Middle East continued to affect dynamics in Yemen, while developments in Yemen in turn reverberated across the region.（範囲：Middle East and Yemen。条件：【主体】Hans Grundberg, Special Envoy of the Secretary-General for Yemen）
