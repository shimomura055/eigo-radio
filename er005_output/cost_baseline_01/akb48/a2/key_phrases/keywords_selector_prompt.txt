# AKB48’s New Single Keeps Its Center—and Opens the Door to a New Face

AKB48 is getting ready for a new release with two important stories in one song.

The group’s 68th single is called **“Suki-ish.”** It is scheduled for release on August 19, 2026.

Momoka Ito, a 19th-generation member, will stand at the center of the title song. She was also the sole center of the previous single, **“Nagori Sakura.”** That was her first time as the center of an AKB48 title song.

Now, Ito will take the same position for a second single in a row.

AKB48 says this has not happened for 12 years. The last member to be the sole center for two singles in a row was Mayu Watanabe in 2014.

So, the center stays the same. But a very young new face is also joining the main lineup.

Saki Kondo is a 20th-generation trainee. At 14, she is the youngest current member of AKB48. She has been chosen for an AKB48 title-song lineup for the first time. The lineup has 16 members.

There is one important difference to understand. Kondo is AKB48’s youngest member now, but she is not the youngest person ever chosen for a single in the group’s history.

Still, her first selection is a big step. One member is building on her role at the center. Another is entering the main lineup for the first time.

### The title is about a feeling that is not fully clear

The word “ish” means “kind of” or “maybe.” AKB48 explains the title as a feeling like, “I think I like you” or “Maybe I like you.” It gives the song a soft feeling of love that has not become a clear answer yet.

### The lineup connects two new generations

Ito comes from the 19th generation, while Kondo is a 20th-generation trainee. This is not simply a story about one person replacing another. Ito keeps the main role, and Kondo takes her first step into the title-song lineup. The group shows stability and change together.

## In one line…

**“Suki-ish” keeps Momoka Ito in the center while opening a new door for Saki Kondo—a single that looks steady and fresh at the same time.**
