# Package Redelivery: Different Voices

If you’re not home when a package comes, you can have it delivered again. What if the person getting it had to pay for this, as well as the usual delivery fee? Here are the views of three people with different lives and points of view. These are not stories about specific real people. They are voices made from several opinions shared online and in other places.

When I leave a delivery notice and head off, I do feel a little down. But then I go on to the next house. I wish there were fewer redeliveries, if possible. They really are hard on us. Even so, I can’t just say customers should pay for them. I think it makes more sense to put the cost into the overall fee for package delivery. If they look again at shipping fees, I want them to look at how the fees are set, too. I want the money to really go to the people delivering the packages.

It just doesn’t feel right to me to pay for redelivery. My husband and I are both out of the house for work. For packages where I can choose, I pick a day and time when I know I’ll be home. Many packages don’t let you choose a delivery time, either. So you don’t know ahead of time when they’ll arrive. Before they charge us, I want them to first do a better job of coming at the time we chose. I also want them to tell us when the package will arrive. I try to pick up packages at a delivery office or a pickup locker. That way, I don’t cause trouble with redelivery. It’s handy to pick them up when it works for me. Maybe that’s only my view, though, because the delivery office is nearby and I can choose how to get my packages.

They care about different things: money for package delivery workers, waiting for a promised morning delivery, and how the effort of going to a pickup locker is rewarded.
