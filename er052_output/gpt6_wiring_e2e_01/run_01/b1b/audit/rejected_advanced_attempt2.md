# Behind the Scenes of an AI Phone Assistant: Who Takes the Baton?

We live in a time when we can ask AI to make phone calls for us. But who makes those calls? Meta’s AI agent, Muse, can call businesses in the United States to book appointments at hair salons, check whether products are in stock, and ask for price estimates. But in internal tests, trained human contractors handled some calls instead. The lead role is not always played by AI. Still, humans did not handle every call.

This is not a story about humans taking over the AI phone feature completely. Meta admitted there was a problem with starting a test of how to pass calls to people without giving a proper explanation. The main point is not which one is better on the phone, AI or humans. It is how to explain that a call may be passed to a person.

Phone calls are not just for sharing requests; they are also a way to share information. Some Meta employees worried that sensitive information might be shared with contractors by mistake. However, it has not been confirmed that any information was actually leaked. A concern and something that has happened are two different things.

The Meta vice president in charge admitted that the lack of explanation was a problem and rolled back the phone feature involving human contractors for now. A company spokesperson said the test was meant to gather feedback on how to protect safety and privacy and improve the feature. It will not be made available to the public until a proper explanation is ready.

Having human helpers for AI may not be a problem in itself. But if a helper is involved, letting people know should be part of the service. When you ask a handy phone assistant to take care of something, you start to wonder about the people working behind the scenes. It is not enough to explain what AI can do; people also need to know who is actually doing the work. Only then can they feel comfortable leaving their tasks to it.

## In one line
Meta paused its AI phone feature after some calls were handled by human contractors without a clear explanation.
