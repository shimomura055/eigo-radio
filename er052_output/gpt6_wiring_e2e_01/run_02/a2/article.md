# The Mini Bag Comeback Trial: Micro Bags Are a Separate Case

Let’s hold our own trial about bag sizes. We have one question: “Can we say micro bags are back too, if mini bags are in the news?” When we look at the facts, the answer is not simple.

First, fashion magazine ELLE showed mini bags as a trend in its report on fall collections. The article showed that mini bags were getting attention. But it showed products and collections, not sales research. In court, an article and more people buying bags are different kinds of evidence.

Next, let’s hear what the runway showed. One report on fall collections showed small minaudières from several brands. It described them as small clutches, more about art than being useful. This point applies only to those bags. It does not mean every small bag is for show. The same report also listed large, roomy shapes as a major trend. So it does not show that small bags alone took over the runway.

About micro bags, an editor at another fashion magazine also gave a view. The editor called the micro-bag trend “short-lived.” The editor showed bags bigger than micro bags, but smaller than totes. Again, this was the editor’s view, not a result from sales research.

So there is evidence that “mini bags are getting attention.” But there is not enough evidence to say “micro bags are popular again.” We also cannot say people across the whole market want small bags again. Magazine stories about mini bags, runway clutches, and one editor’s view of micro bags may sound like one case. They may seem to be about similar sizes, but they are separate cases.

Bags are not only about how they look. They are also about what you can carry in them. It is too soon to think everyone is carrying less just because small bags appear in magazines. This time, the verdict is: “Mini bags are getting attention. But we cannot say micro bags, or the market as a whole, have made a comeback.” Words for bag sizes are more complicated than you might think.

## In one line
Mini bags are getting attention, but this does not prove micro bags are back or that people everywhere want small bags again.
