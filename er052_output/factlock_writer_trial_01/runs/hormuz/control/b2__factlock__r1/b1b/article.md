# The Toll Proposal Changes Its Costume. How Did Oil Prices Move?

It is the sort of turn that makes you want to call this a quick-change show in political news. First to appear was a proposal for a fee on cargo passing through the Strait of Hormuz. Trump said the United States would seek a 20 percent fee on all cargo to cover the cost of keeping the strait safe. But the important label here is not “already in place” but “proposed.” This does not mean that tolls have started.

But the next day, the sign on the stage changed. Trump posted that he would replace the fee, which was meant to pay back the United States, with trade and investment deals between the Gulf states and the United States. He said the decision was based on talks with Middle Eastern leaders. From a fee on cargo passing through the strait to trade and investment between the Gulf states and the United States. The story’s change of costume was quite striking.

Now, did the oil-market scene have a neat ending waiting for us: “The plan is withdrawn, and prices fall”? Brent futures briefly gave up some of their gains after the announcement, but soon returned to a high level close to where they had been before it. At the time of reporting, they were up about 2.6 percent, above $85 a barrel.

Around then, attacks between the United States and Iran, a sea blockade, and worries about tanker safety were still continuing. If the market had a line to say, it would be hard to say, “The fee plan was replaced, so we’re safe now.” The statement changed, but the worries at sea did not disappear offstage along with it. This price move also makes it hard to conclude that oil prices would fall just because the fee plan was withdrawn.

When we hear about a faraway strait as oil-price news, perhaps it is not completely unrelated to our own lives. The point to watch is not just the costume change in the fee plan. Even after the statement changed, prices soon returned to a high level. Looking at that sequence, political developments and worries at sea seem to be moving to different rhythms.

## In one line
Trump replaced a proposed Strait of Hormuz toll with trade deals, but oil prices soon returned to high levels.
