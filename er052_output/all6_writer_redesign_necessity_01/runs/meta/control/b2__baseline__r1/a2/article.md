# Who Is the Main Player in an AI Phone Call? Human Helpers

We are entering a time when AI can make phone calls for you. It can book a haircut. It can check if a store has something in stock. It can ask a business for an estimate. Muse is Meta’s AI agent for personal use. It can do these jobs by phone for users.

But the calls had another twist. In some calls through Muse, AI did not make the call. It did not finish the conversation either. Trained contract workers did those jobs instead. AI got the request and passed it to a human helper. A test of “human concierges” was happening.

The people who received the calls added another twist. Internal posts mentioned some cases. People hung up after learning Muse was AI. One employee also said an insurance company hung up again and again. It did this after noticing that the call came from AI.

Some tests had humans making the calls. They suggested success might reach 95 to 98 percent. However, people did not say how this was measured. They also did not say how many calls there were. This does not mean humans always succeed. There were cases where people hung up when the caller was AI. On the other hand, tests with human callers suggested success might be high. AI and humans almost seem to have changed roles.

However, there was a second twist. When contract workers handled calls, sensitive user information could be shared with them. They needed this information to move the call forward. It could be shared by accident at a call center. Meta employees raised privacy concerns. This does not mean that a large leak of information happened. Even so, users need clear information if humans help an AI service. They need to know who is speaking and what information is being shared.

The vice president of Meta’s Superintelligence Labs division admitted that starting these tests was a “mistake.” The tests let contract workers make calls without clearly telling people. An internal post said the human concierge feature had returned to its previous state for now. This did not mean all of Muse had been stopped.

The main player in AI phone calls was not AI alone. It was also the person on the other end. It was the human speaking behind the scenes. It was also how that person’s presence was explained. Muse’s tests showed that another person takes part behind convenient technology.

## In one line
Meta’s AI phone service sometimes depended on hidden human helpers, raising concerns about transparency and privacy.
