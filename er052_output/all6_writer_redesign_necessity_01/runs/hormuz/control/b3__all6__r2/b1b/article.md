# The Fee Proposal Changed Shape in a Day. The Curtain Has Not Fallen on the Oil Market Yet

In this news story, the policy proposal changed costumes remarkably fast. Just when a plan to charge for cargo passing through the Strait of Hormuz appeared, the story shifted to trade and investment the next day. But the oil market scene did not end with a neat final curtain.

First, on July 13, Trump posted a proposal calling for a 20 percent payment for all cargo passing through the strait. He said this would help cover the cost of the US keeping the strait safe. One important point is that no money had actually started being collected. The proposed rate was given, but the system was not explained: who would pay, how they would pay, or how the amount would be calculated.

Then, the next day, the story shifted. Trump posted that the payment would be replaced by trade and investment deals between Gulf countries and the United States. He said the decision was based on talks with Middle Eastern leaders. He also told reporters that ships passing through the strait should not be charged, and that he did not like the idea of a fee at all. The fee proposal lasted for only about a day.

You might think the market had settled down too, but it had not. After the replacement was announced, Brent futures narrowed their gains for a while. But before long, they returned to a high level close to where they had been before the announcement. At the time of reporting, they were up about 2.6 percent, above $85 a barrel. This was a figure from during trading, not the closing price that day.

Still, this move alone does not tell us for sure how withdrawing the fee proposal affected prices. During that time, concerns continued over attacks by the US and Iran, a sea blockade, and the safety of tankers.

The policy post changed greatly in just one day. But concerns about sea transport did not disappear. The news banner changed, but the tension in the background stayed on stage. The key point this time was that the quick shift from one scene to another came alongside market moves that did not end right away.

## In one line
Trump replaced his proposed Hormuz shipping fee with trade deals, but oil prices remained high as shipping concerns continued.
