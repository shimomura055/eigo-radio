# Who Is the Main Player in an AI Phone Call? Human Helpers

We are entering an age when you can ask AI to make phone calls for you. It can book a haircut, check whether a store has something in stock, or ask a business for an estimate. Muse, Meta's AI agent for personal use, is a system that can handle these tasks by phone for users.

There was another twist behind the calls. In some calls made through Muse, it was not AI that made the call and finished the conversation. Trained contract workers did that instead. AI received the request and handed it over to a human helper. A test of “human concierges” was under way.

The reaction of the people receiving the calls added another twist. Internal posts reported cases in which the other person hung up after realizing that Muse was AI. An employee also reported that an insurance company repeatedly hung up after noticing that the call was from AI.

Some tests in which humans handled the calls suggested that the success rate might reach 95 to 98 percent. However, the way it was measured and the number of calls were not made public. This does not mean that humans always succeed. There were cases in which people hung up when the caller was AI. On the other hand, tests handled by humans suggested a possible high success rate. In the world of phone calls, it almost looks as if the roles of AI and humans have been slightly reversed.

However, there was a second twist. When contract workers handled calls, the user's sensitive information needed to move the call forward could be shared unintentionally with contract workers at a call center. Meta employees raised privacy concerns. This does not mean that a large leak of information occurred. Even so, if humans are involved in a service that uses AI, users need to be clearly told who is speaking and what information is being shared.

The vice president of Meta's Superintelligence Labs division admitted that starting tests in which contract workers made calls without proper disclosure was a “mistake.” An internal post said that the human concierge feature had been returned to its previous state for now. This did not mean that all of Muse had been stopped.

The main player in AI phone calls was not AI alone. There was also the person on the other end, the human speaking behind the scenes, and the way that human's presence was explained. Muse's tests showed that behind convenient technology, there is another person taking part.

## In one line
Meta’s AI phone service sometimes relied on hidden human helpers, raising concerns about transparency and privacy.
