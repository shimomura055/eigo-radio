# An Unexpected Stand-In for AI Phone Calls: Some People Were Talking to Humans

If you let AI handle a phone call, you might expect the voice on the other end to be AI too. You can imagine a future like that, right?

But with Muse, Meta’s new AI agent, things took a slightly surprising turn. In some calls made through Muse, trained human contract workers handled the conversation with the other person all the way to the end instead of AI.

This did not mean that humans answered every call. It was only a test using some calls. Even so, whether the person on the other end was AI or human is a very big difference. You thought you were talking to AI, but a human was actually carrying on the conversation. It was almost as if the main actor in a play had been replaced halfway through.

Also, personal information comes up in phone calls. People may talk about plans, what they bought, or family matters—things they would rather not have strangers hear. Some Meta employees were concerned that sensitive user information needed to continue a call might be shared with contract workers against the user’s wishes.

The important point here is that this does not mean a large information leak actually happened. The problem was that the test began without fully explaining the possibility that human workers would handle the calls.

A Meta executive admitted that starting without proper disclosure was a mistake. Meta then rolled back the “human concierge” feature for the time being. It did not stop Muse as a whole. It rolled back the part in which human contract workers handled the calls.

In the future, Meta will expand the phone feature to the general public only when it is ready and can clearly explain who will handle the calls.

What people most want to know about AI phone calls is not only how smartly AI can talk. They also want to know who is on the other end of the call. That is not something that should be kept hidden.

## In one line
Some people who thought they were speaking with Meta’s AI were actually talking to human contract workers.
