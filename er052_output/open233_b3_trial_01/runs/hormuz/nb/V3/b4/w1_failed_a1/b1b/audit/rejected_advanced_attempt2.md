# Crude Oil Prices Hesitated. But They Soon Came Back

Seen as a short drama about crude oil prices, this story moves very quickly. A plan to ask for payment appeared, and the next day it was replaced by a different story. Normally, crude oil prices might be expected to fall along with that change. But Brent futures hesitated once, then quickly returned to a high level.

The first act was on July 13. President Trump of the United States proposed that the US seek reimbursement equal to 20 percent of all cargo passing through the Strait of Hormuz, to cover the cost of keeping the strait safe. Reimbursement means being paid back for costs that have been spent. What appeared here was a payment plan with a very large number attached to it.

Then, the next day, the story suddenly changed direction. Trump posted that the 20 percent reimbursement fee plan would be replaced by trade and investment deals between the Gulf states and the United States. The story of asking for payment changed into a story about trade and investment. Judging only by the headline, it would not have been surprising if the tension had eased a little at this point.

But from here, Brent futures went against expectations. After the replacement was announced, the rise temporarily became smaller. Up to that point, it looked as if prices had settled in response to the announcement. But soon after, they returned to a high level close to where they had been just before the announcement of the withdrawal and replacement. At the time this article was published, they were up about 2.6 percent and above $85 a barrel.

However, this recovery cannot be explained only by the replacement of the payment plan. At that time, concerns about attacks between the United States and Iran, a sea blockade, and the safety of tankers were continuing. In other words, the story about payment changed its form, but concerns about the sea did not disappear.

The main point this time is that Brent futures did not move in a straight line even after the policy headline changed. The rise became smaller once. But soon, prices returned to a high level. The sudden change in the payment plan and concerns about the sea appeared at the same time, so the drama of crude oil prices did not end at the moment of the announcement.

## In one line
Brent prices dipped after the payment plan changed, then quickly rebounded as concerns about the sea continued.
