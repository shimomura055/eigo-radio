# Crude Oil Prices Came Back After Trump Dropped the 20% Fee Plan

The stage was the Strait of Hormuz. Here, political news and crude oil price changes looked like separate scripts.

On July 13, President Trump appeared first. He posted a plan for all cargo passing through the strait. It would charge this cargo a 20% fee. The money would pay for the United States to keep the strait safe.

In other words, cargo would pay to protect the strait. But the plan did not say who would collect the fee. It did not say who would pay it. It also gave no way to calculate the amount. No details of the system were given.

The next day, the scene changed suddenly. President Trump posted that he was dropping the 20% plan. He would replace it with trade and investment deals between Gulf states and the United States. He also told reporters about ships passing through the strait. They should not pay a fee. He did not like the idea of a fee, either.

This was a small surprise in the crude oil market. After the announcement, Brent crude futures briefly lost some gains. Before long, they returned close to their earlier high level.

At the time of the report, Brent crude futures were higher. They were about 2.6% higher than the day before. They had risen above $85 a barrel. The settlement price after trading ended was $84.73 a barrel. It was up 1.7% from the day before.

If we only follow the headlines, prices may also seem likely to settle down. That is because the 20% fee plan was gone. But in fact, the gains became smaller after the announcement. Then they returned to a high level.

During that time, some news was still waiting in the wings. It included attacks by the United States and Iran. It also included a naval blockade and tanker safety worries. Even after the fee plan was replaced by investment deals, this news continued.

So this movement alone cannot show how the withdrawal affected prices. We can confirm one thing. Even after the political script changed, worries at sea stayed on the same stage.

The crude oil market is not a drama. It does not move only because of words in a post. Beyond the screen, there was also a question. Could tankers pass safely? That question was also part of the same scene.

## In one line
Crude oil prices came back after Trump withdrew the 20% fee plan, while worries at sea remained.
