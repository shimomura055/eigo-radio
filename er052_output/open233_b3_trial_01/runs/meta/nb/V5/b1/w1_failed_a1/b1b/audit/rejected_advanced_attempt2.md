# AI Was Supposed to Be the Star. A Human Made the Call

AI calls a store for you. It makes a haircut appointment, asks about items in stock, and gets a price estimate from a service provider. This is the convenient world imagined by Muse, Meta’s AI agent for personal use.

But then the story took a slightly unusual turn. In mid-September, in some calls made through Muse, it was not AI that actually made the call and finished the exchange with the other person. It was a trained human contract worker. Meta called this system “human concierges” and was testing it.

In other words, users asked AI to do a job, but in some cases, humans stood in for it. This was not a story about machines acting like humans. It was a story about humans taking on the role of machines.

What is more, reports said that who was really on the call sometimes affected how the conversation went. An internal post described cases in which the other person hung up after realizing that Muse was AI. In some tests handled by humans, the success rate was also reported as possibly reaching 95 to 98 percent. However, this was a report from some tests. It cannot be applied directly to the whole experiment.

Here, talk of a convenient feature turns into a slightly awkward problem. If a human makes the call, should the other person be told? Without proper disclosure, during a task that the user entrusted to AI, it becomes unclear who is speaking. The issue was not only whether AI could speak naturally, but also whether to say so when a human was speaking.

By September 22, the vice president of Meta’s Superintelligence Labs division admitted that starting the tests without proper disclosure had been a “mistake.” Meta then rolled back the human concierge feature.

Meta explained that it plans to make the phone feature available once it is ready and proper disclosure is in place.

In a future where people leave phone calls to AI, the real question is not only how natural its voice sounds. Who is on the other end of the phone? Only when that is made clear can a convenient stand-in become a service. Muse brought the challenge of the AI age into view with just one phone call. Before the voice, it needed a name tag.

## In one line
Meta paused its AI phone feature after humans handled some calls without properly disclosing who was speaking.
