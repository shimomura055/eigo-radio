# A Human Cast Member Appeared on an AI Call

This may look like a story from the near future, but it was actually a test that took place.

Meta’s AI agent, Muse, can call businesses and stores in the United States to make reservations, ask about stock, and request estimates. From the user’s point of view, they ask the AI to make a call and then wait for the result. You can imagine such a situation, right?

However, in some of the calls, it was not the AI making the call. Trained human contract workers took over requests from Muse and handled the rest of the conversation.

In other words, you ask the AI to run an errand, and then a human cast member appears during the phone call. This test had a slightly surprising cast.

However, the users, who were like the audience, were not properly told about this arrangement. Some Meta employees raised concerns that sensitive user information needed for the call might be passed to contract workers at a call center, without regard to the user’s wishes.

Also, in one case where the AI was asked to negotiate internet and cable fees, an employee reported that the call record contained inappropriate remarks about race made by a human contract worker. This was a report about one case. It does not show the nature of all the contract workers or of the experiment as a whole.

The vice president of the Meta department in charge admitted that starting the test without properly telling people was a mistake. The vice president explained that, for now, Meta had put back the feature involving human contract workers the way it was before. Meta did not stop Muse as a whole, or all of its phone features.

Meta explained that employees’ reactions were overwhelmingly positive, and that the test was meant to improve safety and privacy protection.

You thought you had asked an AI to make a phone call, but a human appeared on the other end. The problem is not that a human appears. If one does, they should say who they are first. This trouble added that obvious but important rule to the phone book of the AI era.

## In one line
Meta’s AI sometimes handed phone calls to undisclosed human contractors, raising concerns about privacy and transparency.
