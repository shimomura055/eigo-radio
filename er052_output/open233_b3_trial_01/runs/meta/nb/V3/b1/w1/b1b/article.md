# An Unexpected Replacement for AI Phone Calls: Human Staff Appeared

This is a somewhat unusual story about a phone call.

The show was called Muse. AI was supposed to be the star. But when the curtain went up, a human was making the calls. In one part of a phone test by Meta, trained contract workers were making the calls themselves instead of AI.

And the workers were not just watching from the side. They called the other person, talked with them, and handled the exchange all the way to the end. In other words, human replacements had appeared directly in the AI phone feature.

This is the main point.

There is nothing especially strange about humans helping. When an AI system cannot yet handle a situation well, humans help. This can be considered a way to test the technology.

But this time, the test began without fully telling people about that fact. Although the feature was presented as a phone call made by AI, human contract workers were actually speaking.

On the phone, you cannot see the other person's face. That is why it is important to know who you are talking to. If you think you are talking to AI, you may expect a somewhat mechanical reply. If you know it is a human, your expectations for the conversation, and your decision about whether to share personal information, may change.

What stands out this time is not that AI spoke just like a human. It is the opposite. A human came out from behind a phone call carrying the AI label as a replacement. And people were not properly told about this appearance.

A Meta vice president in charge admitted that it was a mistake to start a test in which contract workers made calls without a proper explanation. For now, Meta has put the feature back the way it was.

As we leave more reservations and questions to AI, there will be times even in our everyday phone calls when we want to know whether we are talking to AI or a human.

This incident showed us that there are humans behind the scenes of AI. It also brought another slightly plain but important rule into view: if the backstage is not going to be shown, then at least tell us who is speaking.

## In one line
In a Meta test presented as AI phone calls, human contractors actually made calls without users being told.
