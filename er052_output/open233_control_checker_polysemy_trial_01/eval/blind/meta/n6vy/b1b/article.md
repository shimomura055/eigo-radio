# Who Made the Call? The People Behind the Scenes of AI

This is a small mystery quiz.

You asked AI to make a phone call. You asked it to book a haircut. You asked it to check a store's stock. You asked it to get an estimate from a company. After hearing all this, you would think AI was the one making the calls.

But with Meta's AI agent Muse, some of those calls were actually made by humans.

Meta was testing a system called a human concierge. Muse passed the requests it received to trained human contract workers. The workers made the calls and finished their conversations with the other person. In other words, AI was the public face, but in some cases humans handled the calls behind the scenes.

The important point is that humans did not make all the calls. This was only a test involving some calls made through Muse.

Even so, this was quite unexpected. You thought you were asking AI to do a job, but a human worker was actually on the phone. It was like a driver suddenly stepping out of a self-driving car.

The problem was that Meta started the test without properly telling people about this behind-the-scenes setup. Meta employees pointed out that if users' sensitive information became necessary to continue a call, it could be shared by mistake with contract workers at a call center. This raised privacy concerns.

In other words, the question was not only whether AI could make calls well. Who was on the other end? Who would receive the user's information? It was also about whether it was right to offer the convenience first without telling people this.

A Meta vice president admitted that starting the test without proper disclosure was a “mistake.” Meta then rolled back the human concierge feature. The part that was rolled back was the part where humans handled the calls.

Meta said it will keep improving the phone feature and will launch it more widely only when it is ready and the company can give proper notice.

As we followed the progress of AI, what finally came into view was a human. The more convenient AI becomes, the more important it may be to introduce the people working behind the scenes first.

## In one line
Some calls made through Meta’s AI agent were actually handled by humans, without users being properly informed.
