# A Human Name Appeared on the Cast List for AI Phone Calls

Behind the scenes of AI phone calls, some unusual casting took place. AI was supposed to be the star. But on some calls, trained contract workers were the ones actually making the calls.

Meta tested calls made through its AI agent, Muse. Of course, humans did not handle every call. For some calls made through Muse, contract workers placed the calls and completed the conversations with the people on the other end.

The easiest story to imagine is that AI makes the call and hands it over to a human only when things become difficult. But in this test, humans handled some calls from beginning to end. Instead of AI calling a human when it got stuck, a human appeared on the AI agent’s stage and performed all the way to the end.

This feature was called “human concierge.” In Japanese, that means a human guide. It is a very elegant name, like someone at a hotel who can help with a special request. But the important point this time was not the fancy name. It was that the test began without fully telling people who was handling the calls.

Meta’s vice president admitted that starting a test in which contract workers handled calls without properly telling people was a “mistake.” In other words, the problem was not bringing in humans itself. If a human is going to appear on the stage, people should be told about it first.

This is easy for those of us who are used to automated voices and chat to understand. If we later learn that a human was actually involved in a situation we thought a machine was handling, we are surprised. There is nothing wrong with getting help from a human. What matters is clearly telling people who is playing each part.

For now, Meta will put this feature back the way it was. Even though this was news about AI phone calls, what drew attention in the end was not flashy technology. Was the speaker AI, or was it a human? It was the short message before the show began, telling us who the star was. Behind the scenes in the age of AI, that short message seems to be taking an unexpectedly important role.

## In one line
Meta’s AI phone test used human workers for entire calls without clearly telling people who was speaking.
