# Installing a Septic Tank Is Not the End: Three Water-Treatment Schedules

When you hear about Kitakata City's plan for wastewater treatment, you can see that it considers sewage treatment through not only large systems but also small systems in each community.

The city reclassified areas outside the group-treatment areas as individual-treatment areas using combined-treatment septic tanks. These are facilities installed for each house or similar unit. They treat not only toilet waste but also household wastewater from kitchens, baths, and washing. They are systems that treat water from daily life near the home.

The background is aging facilities and a falling population. As facilities grow old, the costs of renewal and other maintenance rise. As the population falls, revenue from user fees decreases. Taking these conditions into account, Kitakata City reviewed its wastewater treatment methods.

The Ministry of the Environment explains that in areas where homes are spread out, individual treatment with septic tanks may be more economical, while in areas where homes are close together, group treatment such as public sewers may be more economical. This does not mean that one choice is always correct. The choice is made to match local features, such as how homes are spread out.

Here, an unexpected side of septic tanks appears. Installing one does not end the matter. Septic tank managers are required to carry out maintenance checks and cleaning. As a general rule, this must be done at least once a year. However, if a ministerial ordinance sets a different number of times, it must be done at least that many times.

For small household septic tanks, maintenance checks are basically required at least once every four months, and cleaning at least once a year. The number of times differs depending on the treatment method and the number of people the tank is designed to serve. During cleaning, collected sludge and other material are pumped out.

There are also legally required inspections. These are separate from maintenance checks and cleaning. There is an Article 7 inspection, carried out after use begins, and an Article 11 inspection, which checks the function and maintenance condition. The Article 11 inspection is carried out once a year.

Kitakata City's review is about choosing a treatment method that suits each area. In individual-treatment areas, three schedules continue: maintenance checks, cleaning, and legally required inspections. Equipment that cleans water does not simply work quietly. A septic tank also includes taking care of it without forgetting.

## In one line
Installing a septic tank is only the beginning, because it requires regular maintenance, cleaning, and legal inspections.
