# AI Phone Relay: A Human Ran the Final Leg

Having AI make phone calls for you. Just hearing that, it sounds like a useful service from the future. But in Meta’s internal tests, an unexpected handoff was happening on some of those calls.

The main character is Meta’s AI assistant, Muse. Muse can call businesses in the United States. It can book a haircut, check whether a store has something in stock, or ask a contractor for an estimate. It takes care of bothersome calls for you.

But behind the scenes, Muse was not the only one working. Meta was testing a system called “human concierge” for its employees. It handed requests received by Muse to human contractors. Muse took the request, and a human agent made the call and moved the conversation forward.

In short, when you ask AI to make a call, the baton is passed to a human partway through. It is a phone relay.

This handoff also looked strong in the numbers. According to a Meta vice president, in some tests, the success rate when humans made the calls reached 95% to 98%. Calls made by AI had a lower success rate.

But what was meant to add a fast runner also meant carrying some unexpected baggage. At Meta, people raised concerns that when humans handled calls, sensitive information in the calls could be shared unintentionally with call-center contractors.

It is also important not to make this sound bigger than it is. There is no confirmation that information leaks happened on a wide scale. At the same time, one employee reported that after asking Muse to negotiate a bill with an internet and cable provider, the call record contained racist remarks made by a human contractor.

What Meta admitted was a problem was that it had started tests in which contractors made calls without clearly telling people. Meta’s vice president explained that the company had temporarily rolled back the human concierge feature.

What matters in an AI phone feature is not only its ability to get a task done. It also needs to tell people who is speaking on the other end of the call. Muse was the main character this time. But in the end, the one who drew attention was the human phone operator who appeared from behind the scenes.

## In one line
Meta’s AI phone assistant handed some calls to human contractors, raising privacy concerns despite their higher success rates.
