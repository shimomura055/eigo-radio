# We Thought AI Was Making the Calls, but Humans Were the Real Stars: An Unexpected Switch at Muse

Leave one phone call to AI. Then AI contacts the other person and gets everything settled. You can imagine such a convenient situation, right?

However, what was happening at Meta’s service, Muse, was a little different.

In some calls made through Muse, it was not AI that made the call and carried the conversation through to the end. It was trained human contract workers.

In other words, AI, which users thought was in the center of the stage, handed the main role to humans partway through. What is more, the test began without fully telling users about this change.

This was not simply a story about people helping behind the scenes. Humans themselves were handling the job of making the calls. Users thought they had asked AI to handle a task, but a person was actually on the phone. It was as if a real person had suddenly appeared from behind the scenes of a convenient future.

But at this point, the story can no longer end as a joke.

For a human to make a phone call, they may need to know information about the user that is needed for the conversation. This may include what is called sensitive information, which must be handled carefully. That information might also be shared unintentionally with contract workers at a call center. Within Meta, concerns about privacy like these were raised.

The important point is that there was no confirmation that a large-scale information leak had actually happened. The problem this time was that the explanation was not enough about who might be able to see the information behind a service that people use thinking it is AI.

A Meta vice president in charge admitted that starting a test in which contract workers made calls without proper notice was a “mistake.” The company then temporarily withdrew the concierge feature in which humans make phone calls.

However, it did not stop Muse as a whole. Just when we thought the curtain had risen on an AI service, humans appeared. This was a somewhat unexpected behind-the-scenes story that makes us think about who is there beyond convenience.

## In one line
Meta’s Muse appeared to have AI making calls, but undisclosed contract workers were actually handling some conversations.
