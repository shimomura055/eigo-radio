# Human Staff Played a Supporting Role in AI Phone Calls: Behind the Scenes of Muse

If we compare it to a movie, Meta’s AI agent Muse is the lead actor who makes phone calls. It calls stores and companies in the United States to make hair-cut appointments, ask about products in stock, and get price estimates from businesses. It takes on roles like these.

But during filming, an unexpected supporting actor appeared.

Some calls made through Muse were actually made by trained human contract workers, not by AI. The workers completed the conversations with the people on the other end. Meta was testing a system called “human concierges.”

Of course, humans did not take every call. This was only a test involving some calls. That is why the story becomes a little more complicated. Having humans step in to help with AI phone calls may look like a behind-the-scenes arrangement. But if users are not told clearly enough who the performer is, the audience can lose track of the script halfway through.

 Sensitive information from users that was needed to continue the calls might have been shared unintentionally with contract workers at call centers. What has been confirmed is not an actual large-scale information leak, but a privacy concern about the possibility of information being shared.

Also, the test in which contract workers handled the calls began without properly telling users about it. In other words, the notice that there were humans behind the scenes was not enough.

Meta executives admitted that this way of starting the test was a “mistake.” By September 22, they had rolled back the human concierge feature. They did not stop Muse itself. What was rolled back was the feature in which human staff handled the phone calls.

What is interesting this time is not only that humans joined AI phone calls. In AI services, telling people who is doing the work, as well as providing a useful system, becomes part of the work itself. Even if the lead actor is AI, a cast list is needed when the supporting actor is human. The Muse incident showed how the new role of explaining what is happening suddenly became much more important behind the scenes of AI.

## In one line
Meta tested having human workers handle some AI phone calls without clearly telling users, raising privacy concerns.
