# On the Earnings Stage, One Number After Another Appears

Broadcom announced its fiscal 2026 third-quarter earnings on September 2, 2026. The quarter ended on August 2. The first number on the earnings stage is total net revenue: $29.591 billion. Next comes a separate number: $16.7 billion in AI semiconductor sales. This $16.7 billion is for AI semiconductors, not the whole semiconductor segment.

AI semiconductor sales came with two comparisons. They were up 221 percent from the same quarter a year earlier. They were also up 54 percent from the quarter before. Put side by side, the numbers begin to sound more and more like live commentary. But numbers alone do not tell us what caused the increase.

Now the company's top leader speaks. The CEO said demand for custom AI accelerators and networking is still very strong. This is the CEO's view of demand. The key is to keep it separate from the sales figures.

Then the stage moves to the next quarter. Broadcom expects total revenue for the whole company to be about $34.8 billion. It said this would be 93 percent higher than the same period a year earlier. The CEO also said Broadcom expects $21.7 billion in AI semiconductor sales. That would be up 236 percent from the same quarter a year earlier. Neither figure is an actual result. Both are what the company expected when it announced its earnings. The company said actual results may be different from what it expects. The difference could be important.

The point to watch this time is that actual results and the CEO's view of demand appear together. The same announcement also gives a forecast for the next quarter. As the numbers grow, the story moves further and further ahead. But it is important to remember what each number means. That's a small tip for enjoying live coverage of an earnings report.

## In one line
Broadcom reported a sharp rise in AI-chip sales, but its next-quarter sales figures are forecasts, not actual results.
