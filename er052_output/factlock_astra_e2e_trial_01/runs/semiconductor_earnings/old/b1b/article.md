# On the Earnings Stage, One Number After Another Appears

Broadcom's fiscal 2026 third-quarter earnings report was announced on September 2, 2026. The quarter ended on August 2. The first figure to take the earnings stage is the company's total net revenue: $29.591 billion. Next, presented as a separate figure, is $16.7 billion in sales of AI semiconductors. This $16.7 billion figure is for AI semiconductors, not for the semiconductor segment as a whole.

The AI semiconductor figure came with two comparisons: up 221 percent from the same quarter a year earlier and up 54 percent from the previous quarter. When the two comparisons are placed side by side, even just presenting the numbers starts to sound more and more like live commentary. Still, the list of numbers alone is not enough to decide what caused the increase.

Now the comment from the company's top executive enters the scene. The CEO said that demand for custom AI accelerators and networking remains very strong. This is the CEO's view of demand. The key is to treat it as information separate from the sales figures.

Then the stage moves to the next quarter. Broadcom expects consolidated revenue for the company as a whole to be about $34.8 billion, which it said would represent a 93 percent increase from the same period a year earlier. The CEO also said the company expects AI semiconductor sales of $21.7 billion, up 236 percent from the same quarter a year earlier. Neither figure is an actual result; both are the company's outlook at the time of the earnings announcement. The company noted that actual results may differ from its outlook, and that the difference could be important.

The point to watch this time is that actual results, the CEO's assessment of demand, and the outlook for the next quarter all appear in one announcement. As the numbers get bigger, the story moves further and further ahead. But it is important not to lose sight of what each number represents. That's a small tip for enjoying the live coverage of an earnings report.

## In one line
Broadcom reported soaring AI-chip sales, but its next-quarter sales figures are forecasts, not actual results.
