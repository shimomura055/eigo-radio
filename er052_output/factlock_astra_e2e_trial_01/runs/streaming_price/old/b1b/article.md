# Disney+ Price Hike: Bills Start at Different Times

The starting gun for the price increase is about to sound. But bills will not all change at once. In the US, Disney+ will start charging the new prices on different dates for new subscribers and people who already have a subscription.

New subscribers will be first. They will pay the new prices starting September 23, 2026. For people who subscribed before then, the change will begin with billing cycles on or after October 21, 2026. But that does not mean everyone’s bill will change on October 21. From then on, the new price will take effect according to each person’s billing cycle. In other words, there is a time gap between the day you see the news about the price increase and the day your own payment changes.

So, how much will prices go up? The monthly price of the standalone plan with ads will rise from $11.99 to $12.49. The monthly price of the standalone Premium plan will go up from $18.99 to $21.49. The annual plan will also rise, from $189.99 to $214.99. That means the monthly price of the plan with ads will go up by 50 cents, the monthly Premium price by $2.50, and the annual price by $25.

To find out which group you are in for this price increase, check when you subscribed and your billing cycle. Looking not only at the monthly price but also at when it will take effect may help you avoid being surprised when you see your bill.

The sources we checked do not confirm that Disney gave a reason for this price increase. Also, if you subscribe through a third-party payment service, the price or the terms that apply may be different.

## In one line
Disney+’s US price hike begins earlier for new subscribers, while existing customers’ bills change with their billing cycles.
