# Disney+ Prices Rise: Bills Change at Different Times

The starting gun for the price rise is about to fire. But bills will not all change at once. In the US, Disney+ will charge new prices on different dates. The dates differ for new subscribers and people who already subscribe.

New subscribers will be first. They will pay the new prices from September 23, 2026. For people who signed up before then, the change starts later. It starts with billing cycles on or after October 21, 2026. But this does not mean everyone’s bill will change on October 21. After that, the new price will start with each person’s billing cycle. There is a gap between hearing about the rise and your payment changing.

So, how much will prices go up? The standalone plan with ads will cost $11.99 a month. Its price will rise to $12.49. The standalone Premium plan will cost $18.99 a month. Its price will rise to $21.49. The annual price will also rise from $189.99 to $214.99. So, the plan with ads will cost 50 cents more each month. The monthly Premium price will rise by $2.50. The annual price will rise by $25.

To see which group you are in, check when you subscribed. Also check your billing cycle. Do not look only at the monthly price. Check when the new price will start, too. This may help you avoid a surprise when you see your bill.

The sources we checked do not confirm that Disney gave a reason for the rise. Also, if you subscribe through another company’s payment service, your price or terms may differ.

## In one line
Disney+’s US price rise starts earlier for new subscribers, while current customers’ bills change with their billing cycles.
