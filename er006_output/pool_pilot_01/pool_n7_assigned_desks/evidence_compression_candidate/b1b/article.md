# Assigned Desks Are Quietly Coming Back to Some Offices

The office desk lottery is losing some of its appeal.

In some workplaces, companies are bringing back assigned desks and reducing their use of hot-desking. Under this system, workers do not have a regular desk. They choose any open desk when they arrive at the office.

Hot-desking became more common after the pandemic. It gave companies more flexibility and allowed them to share office space. But many employees have not welcomed the change. Some say they want a workplace that feels more stable and personal.

Now, several companies are quietly moving in the other direction. They are giving workers their own desks again, at least in some offices or departments.

This change does not mean that every office is returning to the old system. For now, it is a selective move. Some companies are testing whether assigned seating can make the office feel more useful and more comfortable.

The bigger question is simple:

“Does a flexible office work equally well for everyone?”

### A desk can be more than a place to sit

One workplace survey found that employees with assigned desks were more likely to say they felt a sense of belonging at work. They were also more likely to say they could focus effectively. The results show a connection, not proof that assigned desks caused these feelings. Still, they suggest that a stable place may affect how people experience the office.

### The wider office is still sharing

At the same time, the broader trend still points toward fewer desks per employee. In a survey of companies in the Americas, the share with one desk per employee or less fell from 56 percent in 2023 to 40 percent in 2024. Respondents expected that figure to fall to about one third by 2026. So the return of assigned desks is not a full reversal. It is a targeted response in some workplaces.

## In one line…

Assigned desks are returning because some offices are questioning the human cost of desk sharing. But the shared workplace is not disappearing—the personal desk is simply finding its way back into selected corners.
