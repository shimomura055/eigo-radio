# The Bill Changed. But People Still Worry About Tankers

“If the extra charge goes away, it should get a little cheaper, right?”

Your wallet would hope so. But the oil market does not always respond as we expect. The payment plan changed, but prices stayed high. This time, money was not the only concern. People also worried about tankers at sea: “Could they get through safely?”

It began with a post by President Trump. He said he wanted a 20% charge on all cargo passing through the Strait of Hormuz. The idea was to get paid back for the cost of keeping the strait safe. For the companies carrying the cargo, it felt like a huge bill had suddenly appeared halfway through the trip.

That same day, Brent oil futures rose 9.59%. That was a very sharp rise. You might say, “So the new charge came as a shock.” But we should not blame only one thing.

Reuters linked the rise to the US plan to block Iran by sea. It also linked it to fears about energy shipments going through the Strait of Hormuz. So the question was not only, “How much is the bill?” There was a more basic worry too: “Can the ships get through at all?”

The next day, things changed fast. Trump said he would replace the 20% charge with plans for Gulf countries to trade with the US and invest there. The cargo bill had become talk of a different deal.

Would the market breathe a sigh of relief now?

In fact, Brent futures briefly lost some of their gains after the announcement. But be careful. A smaller rise does not mean prices fell everywhere. Soon, prices were near the high level from before the announcement. Oil did not get cheaper as people had hoped.

The answer is at sea. Even after the announcement, people still worried about attacks by the US and Iran, the sea blockade, and tanker safety. Changing the way the bill worked did not remove worries about the route.

Think about home delivery. If you hear, “The extra delivery fee is being handled separately now,” you feel better for a moment. But what if you are then told, “We are still worried about whether your package will arrive safely”? Even if your face had just started to brighten, it would freeze halfway. The delivery fee and delivery safety are separate issues.

The same thing was true of oil this time. The question of how much to pay changed, but worries about moving oil safely remained. A bill can be rewritten. But that alone cannot calm the sea. The worry about tankers was still there, beyond the price tag.

## In one line
Trump replaced his proposed 20% charge on cargo crossing the Strait of Hormuz with plans for Gulf countries to trade with and invest in the US. Brent futures briefly lost some gains, then neared earlier highs as worries about the route and tanker safety continued.
