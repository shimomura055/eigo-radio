# Pickles in a Lemon Tart? When AI Joins the Convenience-Store Kitchen

What would you do if a computer program suggested putting pickles in a lemon tart?

That surprising idea came from AI at Lawson, one of Japan’s major convenience-store chains. It became the starting point for a real product called “Lemon Tart (Pickle Flavor).”

At first, this sounds like a computer making a food decision. But that is not what happened.

Lawson’s human developers made samples, tried them, and tested them repeatedly. They adjusted the taste and appearance before the product was finished. The AI offered an idea. People decided what to do with it.

Lawson scheduled the product for September 29, 2026. It was planned for about 4,700 stores in Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama, Chiba, Ibaraki, Tochigi, Gunma, Niigata, Yamanashi, and Nagano. Some areas of Niigata and Nagano were not included. Natural Lawson was also excluded. The price was 270 yen, including tax.

So this was a regional launch, not a nationwide or permanent product. But it gives us a clear picture of one way some major Japanese convenience-store chains are using AI around food: a computer can suggest a starting idea, while people decide whether it belongs on the shelf.

### Surprise was the goal

At Lawson, the first search was not about finding a sure hit. The company did not use past sales or market data at that stage. Instead, it asked AI for combinations and flavors that might make people say, “What is this?” That made AI useful for opening the door to an idea the team might not have considered.

### A different question: What might sell?

FamilyMart took a different path. It used sales data with AI while developing “Oimo no Canele — with Caramel Sauce.” The product launched across Japan on September 22, 2026, but it was quantity-limited. This search started with what people had bought before, not with a wish to surprise.

## In one line…

AI can help find a strange idea or suggest a product with a better chance of selling. People still decide what actually reaches the shelf.
