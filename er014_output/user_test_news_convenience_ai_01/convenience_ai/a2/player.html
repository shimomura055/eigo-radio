Full Story Part 1
voice=Aoede(英語・A2 6%減速)		What would you do if a computer program suggested putting pickles in a lemon tart? That surprising idea came from AI at Lawson, one of Japan's major convenience-store chains. It became the starting point for a real product called Lemon Tart (Pickle Flavor). At first, this sounds like a computer making a food decision. But that is not what happened. Lawson's human developers made samples, tried them, and tested them repeatedly. They adjusted the taste and appearance before finishing the product. The AI offered an idea. People decided what to do with it.

voice=Aoede(日本語)		前半で大切なのは、AIの案をそのまま使わず、人が試して商品にしたことです。では、この商品はどのように販売される予定だったのでしょうか。
Full Story Part 2
voice=Aoede(英語・A2 6%減速)		Lawson is scheduled to sell the product from September 29, 2026. It will be sold at about 4,700 stores in Tokyo and nearby prefectures. Some areas of Niigata and Nagano are excluded. Natural Lawson stores are also excluded. The price will be 270 yen, including tax. So this will be a regional launch, not a nationwide product. But it shows one way some major Japanese convenience-store chains are using AI around food. A computer can suggest a starting idea. Then people decide whether it belongs on the shelf.
