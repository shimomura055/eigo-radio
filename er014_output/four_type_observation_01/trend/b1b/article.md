# The Screen Is Still Here, but It Is No Longer Alone

Your phone is still in your hand. But it may no longer be where every digital task begins and ends.

Some companies are moving digital help into the spaces around us. Google says Gemini on Android will begin with selected Pixel and Samsung Galaxy phones, then extend to watches, cars, glasses, and laptops later in 2026. Google has also shown Android XR glasses that could handle messages, directions, photos, and translation without the user reaching for a phone.

Amazon is moving in a similar direction. Alexa+ is available in the United States through Alexa-enabled devices, the Alexa app, and Alexa.com. Amazon says it can help with tasks such as ordering takeout, booking rides, making restaurant reservations, and scheduling home repairs.

The message is not that smartphones are disappearing. It is that the touchscreen is becoming just one doorway into digital services.

Voice, audio, cars, homes, and glasses are beginning to share that job. Meta says its Ray-Ban Meta glasses have sold millions of units since launch. Google also offers hands-free Gemini through Pixel Buds, while Gemini is being rolled out to Wear OS watches from several manufacturers.

Taken together, these signals point to an early but broad change. Digital interaction is spreading across more places, even though some products are still only planned or being tested.

### From opening apps to stating an outcome

The deeper change is not simply moving an assistant from a phone to a watch or speaker. It is moving from doing each step to stating the result you want. Google describes tools that can research or manage complex tasks with little oversight. Alexa+ can handle several real-world requests, and Google Home can create automations through natural language. The screen may remain, but it may no longer be the main workspace.

### A gradual change, not one replacement gadget

The shift is likely to be uneven and tied to specific situations. Amazon reports 600 million active Alexa endpoints, though that figure does not mean 600 million people. Google says Android Auto supports more than 250 million cars. These figures show reach in homes, cars, voice, and audio. At the same time, Google’s XR glasses remain a development-stage demonstration, even as Meta reports millions of glasses sold. Progress will be easier to see in repeated daily moments than in one dramatic product launch.

## In one line…

The smartphone is not being replaced; it is becoming one interface among many, with the strongest evidence pointing to a gradual and uneven shift beyond the touchscreen.
