One voice: A feed that saves her time

On the morning train, she opens Google News’s “For you” page. Stories about topics she follows are waiting, along with sources that matter to her. Past activity on Google services and YouTube can shape the page too. She does not search through everything before work. If a story misses the mark, she can ask for less, hide a source, or change what she follows. She is not seeing every headline, but the feed gives her a useful feeling: she is still keeping up. Personalization helps her reach relevant news before the day gets away.
