# Coffee Bean Prices Fall. Are Price Tags Still on a Journey?

“Beans have gotten cheaper!” At that news, your wallet pumps its fist. In your mind, the price of your usual cup has already come down, too. But the price tag in a shop may not change right away. When you see a price tag that makes you want to say, “Haven’t you heard the news?”, it seems there is a time lag.

The price measure reported by the International Coffee Organization fell 8.7% from the previous month. The reason, it said, was that more beans harvested in Brazil had entered the market, and a good supply was expected to continue.

The US Department of Agriculture said that earlier high prices came from several factors happening at once: bad weather in Brazil and Vietnam, fewer beans available for sale, and strong demand.

Meanwhile, US consumer price data showed that the price of coffee was 6.1% higher than in the same month a year earlier. It may leave you wondering, “Did prices go down or up?” But the data covers a different month from the bean price measure. And it does not show the price of a cup at a cafe. Hold on before putting the two figures in the ring.

So, how long does it take for the price of beans to reach a price tag?

According to estimates from market insiders and experts interviewed by Reuters, it takes at least about nine months for the price of raw beans to show up in US retail prices. The road to a price tag is quite a long journey.

The article says that companies in the US that roast coffee keep raw beans in stock. Roasting and packing the beans also take time. They also often negotiate prices with retailers on a regular basis. The price tag in a shop does not flash to a new price the moment the market moves.

However, the “about nine months” figure is an estimate based on interviews. It is not a rule that applies to every company, and it is not a promise that “prices will fall in nine months.” It is too soon to mark the date on your calendar.

And a cup at a cafe comes with costs besides the beans. One cafe operator in Chicago said that the cost of cups and labor, as well as the cost of buying beans, had been behind price increases. Even if bean prices fall, other costs do not always fall along with them.

If you are waiting for the price of your usual cup to come down, think not only, “How much do beans cost now?” but also, “When does the price of the beans reach here?” A coffee price tag may have an unseen clock of its own, separate from the latest news about the market.

## In one line
Falling coffee bean prices may take at least nine months to affect US retail prices.

# If You Didn’t Write with AI, You Can’t Apply

What if a literary prize had a rule like this for entry? Not “You may use AI,” but “You must write with AI.” Wait, is that really required? I thought AI was a helper working behind the scenes. Now it is openly the ticket to enter.

The Prix Pascal was announced in France. It is for French stories that have not been published, written by a person and AI together. Using AI is not an extra benefit or a special case. If you have not used it, you cannot apply.

It may be too soon to say, “At last, we can compete to see how well AI can write.” What I want to know is not about the writers. It is about the people giving the prize. Why did they create a prize with this special rule for entry?

The people behind the prize say they want to ask other literary prizes a question. Those prizes are trying to decide what to do about writers using AI. They create one prize and ask the others, “So, what would you do?” You could call it a question shaped like a trophy.

Imagine you are reading a story late at night. The last line moves you. Then you hear, “Actually, I wrote it with AI.” Would a line that seemed beautiful suddenly feel far away? Or would you say, “I enjoyed it, so I have no complaint”? A special meeting to judge the story might start inside your mind.

Of course, the label “co-written with AI” does not mean the story is interesting. I would not want to read it just because of that, either. I want to know what kind of story it is and how it is written. Before the label, I want to see the first page.

But when I think about being the writer, things become much harder. I might ask AI to fix a phrase or help me think about what happens in the story. But how much can I use it and still proudly say, “I wrote this”? Where is the line between a helpful partner I can talk with and someone who writes with me?

So it would be a shame to make this prize only a simple question: “Is AI good or bad?” A prize that chooses stories also shows what the people choosing them value. The question of whether AI was used does not end the discussion. Instead, it becomes the starting point for reading. It seems writers are not the only people being tested at this new entrance. So, how will we read that book?

## In one line
France has announced a prize for unpublished French stories written by a person and AI. Its founders ask other prizes to think about AI, and readers to think about how AI changes reading.

# Looking for a “Money-Making Personality”? The Main Character Was Your Past Self

“If you take things more seriously, will you earn more?”
“Or do you start taking things more seriously after you earn more?”

When you hear about studies on income and personality, you may expect to learn the “secret of people who earn a lot.” But this time, the people in the story are not rich people. They are “your past self” and “your later self.”

The research brought together 11 large studies. They looked at people who had jobs and shared information about their personality and work income. The number of people was not the same in every study. The researchers used data from up to 134,515 people. Each study measured the same people’s income and personality at least three times.

The important thing was how the researchers compared people. They looked separately at differences between people that usually stay the same. They also looked at changes inside each person.

Not, “What’s different between me and that person who earns a lot?”
But, “How are my past self and my later self different?”

In a way, the researchers stopped comparing people with others. Instead, they followed each person over time. It is like putting down binoculars you use to look at your neighbor’s greener grass. Then you turn the pages of your own records.

Then came “diligence.” This means taking things seriously and working hard. When a person’s income went up, their diligence tended to rise later too. This link was small. The link also went the other way. When diligence rose, income tended to rise later. That link was small too.

It was not a link in only one direction. It went both ways. But this is important, so let me say it clearly: the links were “small.”

“So, earning more makes me more serious!”
Wait before you jump to that conclusion.

This study cannot show that higher income changed a person’s personality. It also cannot show that their personality made their income rise. Something else may have played a part in both. Even when researchers follow the same people and compare them carefully, this alone cannot show what caused what.

In other words, this is not a how-to guide for a “personality that earns more.” This study can give you a reason to open again a book you were about to close. You may have thought, “This is just the kind of person I am.”

Instead of ending with, “That person is the kind who earns a lot,” you can look back and ask, “How have I changed?” You may plan to flip through a book about successful people. Then you may realize you are reading the next part of your own story. That is what makes this study interesting.

## In one line
Over time, income changes and later diligence changes had small links both ways. The study cannot show what caused what, but it can make us ask how we changed.
