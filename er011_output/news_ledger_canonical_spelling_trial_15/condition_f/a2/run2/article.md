# Hanshin Take Control Early and Finish with an 8–1 Win

On August 16, at MAZDA Zoom-Zoom Stadium in Hiroshima, the Hanshin Tigers gave the Hiroshima Toyo Carp a difficult night.

Hanshin moved first in the opening inning. Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field, giving the Tigers a quick 2–0 lead.

That early start was supported by a steady performance from starting pitcher Takato Ihara. He pitched five innings, gave up only two hits, and allowed one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. But the Carp could not add to it.

Hanshin stayed in control. The Tigers scored again in the seventh and eighth innings. Seishiro Sakamoto had important hits, and Torai Fushimi also drove in a run.

The result was clear:

Hiroshima Toyo Carp 1–8 Hanshin Tigers

Hanshin scored early, got strong starting pitching, and added more runs later. Hiroshima had one answer, but not enough to change the game.

### One pitch, one milestone for Sato

There was a clear detail behind Sato’s home run. Shohei Mori had a 1–1 count, and Sato hit his third pitch, a two-seam fastball.

The home run was Sato’s 28th of the season. That left him just one behind his teammate Shota Morishita, who had 29.

So the hit was more than one strong swing. It was also a specific win in one matchup and moved Sato closer in Hanshin’s home-run race.

### A win with value beyond one night

This result also changed the teams’ short-term situation. Hanshin stopped a two-game losing streak and stayed in first place.

The Tigers also kept a two-game lead over Yomiuri. Hiroshima, meanwhile, saw its own two-game winning streak end and missed a chance to win three straight.

That made the victory important in two ways. Hanshin stopped its slide, while Hiroshima lost the chance to build more momentum.

## In one line…

Hanshin took control early, trusted Takato Ihara, and kept adding pressure until the end. It was a complete 8–1 win that also kept the Tigers on top.
