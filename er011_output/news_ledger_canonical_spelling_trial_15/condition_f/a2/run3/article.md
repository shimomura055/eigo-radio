# Hanshin Takes Control Early and Beats Hiroshima 8-1

On August 16, 2026, the Hanshin Tigers beat the Hiroshima Toyo Carp 8-1 at MAZDA Zoom-Zoom Stadium Hiroshima.

The game was simple: Hanshin moved first and kept control.

Hanshin took a 2-0 lead with a two-run home run in the first inning. That gave the team an early advantage and put pressure on Hiroshima.

Takato Ihara gave that lead a strong base. The Hanshin starter worked five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. The score became 2-1, but Hiroshima could not score again.

Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. Seishiro Sakamoto and Torai Fushimi delivered important hits, and Sakamoto added another run with a double.

The lead grew, and Hiroshima had no answer late in the game.

The final score was:

Hanshin Tigers 8, Hiroshima Toyo Carp 1.

This was not a close escape. It was a win built in steps: an early strike, steady pitching, and more runs late.

### Sato’s big swing had a deeper story

With two outs and a runner on first, Teruaki Sato faced Shohei Mori. The count was one ball and one strike. Sato hit Mori’s third pitch, a two-seam fastball, for his 28th home run. He moved within one home run of teammate Shota Morishita. One swing won that moment and brought Sato closer in the team’s home-run race.

### A win that stopped two slides

This victory changed the short-term picture. It stopped Hanshin’s two-game losing streak and kept the Tigers in first place, two games ahead of the Giants. Hiroshima’s own two-game winning streak ended before it could become three. The result held Hanshin’s position and stopped Hiroshima from building more momentum.

## In one line…

A quick home run gave Hanshin the lead, but Ihara’s steady work and late runs turned it into a complete 8-1 victory.
