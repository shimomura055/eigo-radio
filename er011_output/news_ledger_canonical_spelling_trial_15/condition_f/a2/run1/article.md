# Hanshin Turn One Early Swing into a Complete 8-1 Win

One big swing started the night, but it did not decide the game alone.

On August 16, 2026, the Hanshin Tigers faced the Hiroshima Toyo Carp at MAZDA Zoom-Zoom Stadium Hiroshima.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run, putting Hanshin ahead at once.

Then Takato Ihara gave that lead a strong base. The starting pitcher worked five innings, allowing only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima's only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

That was the only crack in Hanshin's night. The Tigers added runs in the seventh and eighth innings, moving farther ahead and making a comeback harder.

By the end, the scoreboard read:

Hanshin Tigers 8, Hiroshima Toyo Carp 1.

The story was simple but complete. Hanshin struck first, received steady pitching, and kept adding to its lead. This was a win built across the whole game, not only at the start.

### Sato's swing also changed the home-run race

The opening swing also carried a small power-race story. With the count at one ball and one strike, Shohei Mori threw his third pitch, a two-seam fastball. Teruaki Sato hit it for his 28th home run. That left him one behind teammate Shota Morishita, who had 29. The at-bat mattered beyond this game.

### The late attack had more than one source

The late push came from several players. Seishiro Sakamoto brought home a run in the seventh and hit a run-scoring double in the eighth. Torai Fushimi, batting as a substitute, also brought home a run. Different players kept the attack moving late.

## In one line…

An early home run opened the door, but steady pitching and late contributions turned it into a complete 8-1 win. Hiroshima scored only once.
