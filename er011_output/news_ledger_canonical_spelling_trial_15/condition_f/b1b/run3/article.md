# A Fast Start and a Growing Lead: Hanshin Beat Hiroshima 8-1

Some games are won with one big swing. This one began that way, then became something more.

On August 16, 2026, in Hiroshima, the Hanshin Tigers beat the Hiroshima Toyo Carp 8-1.

Hanshin moved in front in the first inning. Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run, giving his team an early lead and setting the tone for the night.

Then Takato Ihara made that lead hold up. The Hanshin starter worked five innings, allowing only two hits and one run. Hiroshima’s only score came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

That was the only real answer Hiroshima could produce.

Hanshin did not leave the result to its opening swing. The Tigers added runs in the seventh and eighth innings, widening the gap and taking control of the finish.

The early two-run lead became an 8-1 win. Hanshin scored first, kept Hiroshima’s offense under control, and continued to add to the scoreboard late in the game.

### Sato’s swing had a little more behind it

With two outs and a runner on first, Sato met Shohei Mori’s third pitch, a two-seam pitch on a 1-1 count. He sent it to the back screen in center field for a two-run homer. It was Sato’s 28th, leaving him one behind Shota Morishita.

### The late lead had several contributors

In the seventh, Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi had timely hits. In the eighth, Sakamoto added a timely double. Several at-bats helped build the late lead, including one from a player brought in to hit.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned Teruaki Sato’s early two-run shot into an 8-1 win, with Takato Ihara limiting Hiroshima to one run and the late offense finishing the job.
