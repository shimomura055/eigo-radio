# A Quick Start, Then Control: Hanshin Beat Hiroshima 8–1

On August 16, 2026, Hanshin played Hiroshima at Mazda Stadium.

The game changed early.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly moved ahead, 2–0.

Hiroshima answered in the fifth inning and cut into the lead. But Hanshin did not let the game turn into a close finish.

In the seventh and eighth innings, Hanshin added more runs. The gap grew again, and Hiroshima could not find another answer.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

This was more than a game decided by one early swing. Hanshin scored first, stayed steady when Hiroshima pushed back, and then made its lead bigger late in the game.

The key was not only the first score. Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara gave Hiroshima very little room to build pressure. He pitched five innings, allowed just two hits, and gave up one run. ### More than one answer at the plate

Hanshin’s late advantage came from several players. In the seventh inning, Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Taira Fushimi each hit the ball to bring in a run. In the eighth, Sakamoto added another run with a double. The finish showed the value of different players handling important moments.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned a quick lead into a clear win by limiting Hiroshima’s attack and adding important runs later. At Mazda Stadium, the 8–1 result was built step by step.
