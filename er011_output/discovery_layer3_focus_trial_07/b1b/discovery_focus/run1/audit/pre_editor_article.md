# The Small Refrigerator Setting That Changes How Produce Lasts

A refrigerator crisper drawer can look like a simple box for fresh food. But many refrigerators offer two settings: low humidity and high humidity. These settings create different conditions inside the drawer.

The low-humidity setting allows more air to pass through. The high-humidity setting is more nearly sealed. That small difference matters because fresh food can lose quality in different ways.

Some foods lose water quickly and become wilted. A high-humidity setting helps them hold on to that water. Fruits also release a gas called ethylene as they mature. If other fruits or vegetables stay exposed to this gas, they may ripen too quickly and spoil sooner.

A low-humidity drawer allows more of that gas to escape. So it can suit foods that release a lot of ethylene. A high-humidity drawer can suit foods that need more protection from moisture loss.

This means the useful question is not simply, “Is this a fruit or a vegetable?” The better question is, “What kind of change is most likely to damage this food?” The drawer setting is a small control, but it can change the air around the food and help it stay fresh longer.

### Fruit is not always a low-humidity food

The familiar shortcut—fruit in low humidity and vegetables in high humidity—can lead to the wrong choice. Strawberries and citrus fruits, including oranges, can favor high humidity. Their best humidity is about 90 to 95 percent. The lesson is to check the food’s needs, not only its category.

### Sometimes the drawer is not the first choice

Before choosing a humidity setting, ask whether the food belongs in the refrigerator at all. Tomatoes and bananas are recommended for counter storage. Potatoes, sweet potatoes, onions, and garlic do not need refrigeration; they need a cool, dry place. In these cases, the most useful change may be taking the food out of the drawer.

## In one line…

The crisper drawer works best when you look past simple labels: protect the food’s real needs, and first make sure it belongs in the refrigerator.
