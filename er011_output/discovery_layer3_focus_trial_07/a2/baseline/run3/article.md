# The Small Refrigerator Setting That Can Protect Fresh Food

Open your refrigerator and look at the crisper drawer. It may have two settings: low humidity and high humidity.

They are more than small labels. They are two ways to deal with food that is losing freshness.

The low-humidity setting lets more air move through the drawer. This is useful for foods that give off ethylene as they ripen. Ethylene is a natural gas. If other fruits and vegetables stay around too much of it, they may ripen too fast and spoil sooner. With more air movement, the gas can escape more easily.

The high-humidity setting is more closed off. It helps food hold on to its water. That is useful for leafy vegetables and other produce that can lose water quickly and become wilted.

So the drawer is not a magic box. The best choice is not based simply on where an item sits in the store. It depends on the kind of freshness problem the food may have.

One setting helps move a ripening gas away. The other helps reduce water loss. With this in mind, the drawer can help food stay fresh longer.

### First, ask if it belongs in the fridge

Before choosing either drawer, first check: does this food belong in the refrigerator at all? Tomatoes and bananas are recommended for counter storage. Potatoes, sweet potatoes, onions, and garlic do not need refrigeration; they are recommended for a cool, dry place. In practice, storage becomes a three-way choice: counter, cool and dry place, or crisper drawer.

### Fruit is not a perfect shortcut

The word “fruit” can send shoppers in the wrong direction. Strawberries and oranges are fruits, but they prefer high humidity. So the useful test is not the grocery-store section. Ask what the food needs: protection from quick water loss, or a setting suited to a food that gives off ethylene. This is more useful than memorizing “fruit means low.”

## In one line…

A crisper drawer is less about food labels and more about matching a food with its freshness problem. First choose the right place; then choose the humidity setting.
