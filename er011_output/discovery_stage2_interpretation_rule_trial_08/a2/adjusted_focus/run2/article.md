# The Small Refrigerator Setting That Can Keep Produce Fresh Longer

A crisper drawer may look like just another part of the refrigerator. But its humidity setting can affect how well some produce keeps its quality.

Many refrigerators offer two choices: low humidity and high humidity. The low-humidity setting lets more air move through the drawer. The high-humidity setting keeps more moisture in.

The important question is not simply, “Is this a fruit or a vegetable?”

Instead, ask two simple questions.

Does the food release a lot of ethylene, a natural gas produced as some foods ripen? Or does it lose water quickly and become wilted?

Apples and pears release more ethylene. In a low-humidity drawer, that gas can escape more easily. This can help protect other produce nearby from ripening too fast.

Leafy greens like kale and vegetables like broccoli can lose water quickly. A high-humidity drawer helps keep moisture around them, which can help them stay fresh and firm.

So the crisper is not divided into a simple “fruit side” and “vegetable side.” The better choice depends on what the food is doing.

### Check the storage place first

Before choosing low or high humidity, make a different choice: should this food be in the refrigerator? Iowa State Extension recommends room temperature for tomatoes and bananas. Potatoes, sweet potatoes, onions, and garlic need a cool, dry place, but they are not simply counter foods. Choose the storage place first, then choose the drawer setting. That keeps the drawer rule from being used on the wrong foods.

### When the label is not the whole answer

Numbers can mislead. One large-scale storage guide gives strawberries and oranges a target humidity of about 90 to 95 percent after harvest. That does not automatically mean the high-humidity drawer at home. One refrigerator guide puts both in its low-humidity fruit bin, while another puts strawberries on the high side. When guidance is unclear, follow your refrigerator’s own guide—not a universal fruit rule.

## In one line…

The crisper is not simply a fruit drawer and a vegetable drawer. Choose the right storage place first, then match the drawer to the food’s water loss or gas, and check the home guide when the answer is unclear.
