# Hanshin Turned an Early Spark into a Complete Win in Hiroshima

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, the Hanshin Tigers took control early and never gave the Hiroshima Carp a real chance to change the game.

The key moment came in the first inning. Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin had the lead before the game had fully settled.

Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara then gave the team a steady foundation. Hiroshima got one run in the fifth inning, but Hanshin kept the game in its hands.

The Tigers added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. What began with one big swing became a comfortable finish.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win for Hanshin, with an early lead, reliable pitching, and more offense later in the game.

### Hiroshima never built a full rally

The numbers show more than simple protection of a lead. Ihara allowed only two hits and one run in five innings. Hiroshima’s only run came from Montero’s solo home run in the fifth. The Carp never turned their limited scoring into a multi-run attack. That is why Hanshin’s early advantage stayed safe without a shutout.

### The late push had many contributors

The late scoring had a different feel from Sato’s opening blast. In the seventh, Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Tora Fushimi each delivered an RBI hit. In the eighth, Sakamoto added an RBI double. Hanshin’s finish came from repeated timely hits and more than one contributor, not from one power play alone.

## In one line…

An early home run opened the door, but Hanshin won by keeping Hiroshima quiet and steadily widening the gap. It was an 8–1 victory built on control from start to finish.
