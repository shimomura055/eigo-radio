# Hanshin Starts Fast, Then Pulls Away in Hiroshima

On August 16, 2026, Hanshin played Hiroshima at Mazda Stadium.

The game began with a big swing.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly moved in front, 2–0.

That early lead then got strong support from the mound. Hanshin’s starting pitcher, Ryoto Ihara, worked five innings. He allowed only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came from Elehuris Montero’s solo home run in the fifth inning.

But Hanshin did not stop there.

The Tigers added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead grew, and Hiroshima could not answer again.

By the end, the scoreboard read:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win for Hanshin. The team started with power, received steady pitching, and kept building its lead late in the game.

### The game was closer for a moment

The game was briefly much tighter than the finish suggests. In the fifth, Montero’s solo homer brought Hiroshima within one run. That moment mattered because the final gap had not yet arrived; Hanshin still had to protect a small lead before the later innings created real distance.

### A win built by more than one hitter

The late attack had more than one face. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in runs in both the seventh and eighth innings, and pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi added an RBI hit. The big finish came from repeated contributions, not only from Sato’s first swing.

## In one line…

Hanshin started fast, faced a brief test in the middle, and turned the game into a comfortable win with steady pitching and late scoring.
