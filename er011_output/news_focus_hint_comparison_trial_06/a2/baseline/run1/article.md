# Hanshin Starts Fast, Then Pulls Away in Hiroshima

On August 16, 2026, Hanshin played Hiroshima at Mazda Stadium.

The game began with a big swing.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took a 2–0 lead.

Hanshin’s starting pitcher, Ryoto Ihara, then gave the team strong support. He worked five innings, allowing only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

But Hanshin kept going.

The Tigers added runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead grew, and Hiroshima could not answer.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win for Hanshin. The team started with power, got steady pitching, and kept building its lead late.

### The game was closer for a moment

The game was briefly much tighter than the final score suggests. In the fifth, Montero’s solo homer brought Hiroshima within one run. Hanshin still had only a small lead before the later innings created real distance.

### A win built by more than one hitter

The late attack had more than one contributor. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in runs in both the seventh and eighth innings, and pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi added an RBI hit. The big finish came from repeated contributions, not only from Sato’s first swing.

## In one line…

Hanshin started fast, faced a brief test in the middle, and turned the game into a comfortable win with steady pitching and late scoring.
