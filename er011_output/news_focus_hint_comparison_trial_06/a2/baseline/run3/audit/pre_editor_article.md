# A Fast Start, Then a Clear Finish: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8–1

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin took control early and finished the game strongly against Hiroshima.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly moved ahead, 2–0.

Hanshin’s starting pitcher, Rihito Ihara, then gave the team a steady base. He allowed only two hits and one run over five innings.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, making the score 2–1.

The game was still close at that point. But Hanshin did not let the lead disappear. The Tigers added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings.

When the game ended, Hanshin had scored eight runs. Hiroshima had scored only one.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win, but it was built step by step.

### A win built in two stages

The 8–1 score may look like a game Hanshin owned from the start. The actual shape was different. Hanshin led only 2–0 before Hiroshima came within one run in the fifth. The game became one-sided later. The final margin shows how Hanshin turned a close game into a clear win.

### Why the first lead held

Ihara’s value was more than a good line on the scorecard. He kept Hiroshima from building steady pressure. Over five innings, he gave up two hits and one run. The only damage was Montero’s solo home run, so Hiroshima never erased Hanshin’s opening advantage with a bigger attack.

## In one line…

Hanshin made the first move, held firm through the middle of the game, and turned a narrow lead into an 8–1 finish.
