# Hanshin Turned One Big Swing into a Complete Win

On August 16 at Mazda Stadium, the Hanshin Tigers took control early and never gave it back.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly took a 2-0 lead.

Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara gave the team a strong foundation. He pitched five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. That made the game closer for a moment, but Hiroshima could not turn another scoring chance into a bigger attack.

Hanshin added runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead kept growing, and the game moved away from Hiroshima.

The final score was:

Hanshin 8, Hiroshima 1.

It was a complete win built on an early lead, steady pitching, and more scoring later in the game.

Hiroshima had one scoring event in this game, not a long run of offense. That helps explain the wide gap on this night. But the game record does not tell us why Hiroshima could not score more. It does not confirm a certain strategy, a player problem, or a wider team weakness. The safe takeaway is smaller: Hiroshima could not build a lasting attack in this game.
