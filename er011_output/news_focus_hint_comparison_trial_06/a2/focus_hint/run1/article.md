# Hanshin’s Early Lead Grows Into an 8–1 Win

On August 16, the Hanshin Tigers faced the Hiroshima Carp at Mazda Stadium.

Hanshin began to take control in the first inning.

Hanshin struck first. Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field, putting the Tigers ahead before Hiroshima could settle into the game.

Hanshin’s starting pitcher, Rihito Ihara, gave the team a strong base. He pitched five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima could not build a big attack, and Hanshin kept its lead.

The Tigers scored again in the seventh inning and added more in the eighth. The gap grew, and Hiroshima could not close it.

At the end, the scoreboard showed:

Hanshin 8–1 Hiroshima

Hanshin started fast, received steady pitching from Ihara, and continued to add to its advantage.

### Hiroshima’s answer never became a wave

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. After that, there was no second scoring wave from the Carp. Hanshin added runs in the seventh and eighth, so Hiroshima’s answer stayed isolated instead of becoming a turning point.

### More than one role kept the attack moving

The late offense came from different parts of the lineup. Seishiro Sakamoto brought in a run in the seventh and hit a double that brought in another in the eighth. Torae Fushimi, sent in as a pinch-hitter, also drove in a run. The win was built by several contributions, not one late swing.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned an early lead into an 8–1 win by stopping Hiroshima from building a second attack and finding help from different parts of the lineup. It was a game of control, followed by separation.
