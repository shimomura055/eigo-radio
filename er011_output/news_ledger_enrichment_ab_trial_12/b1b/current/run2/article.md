# Hanshin Turned an Early Strike into a Complete Win

## Main Story

Some wins are decided by one big moment. This one was decided by what came after.

On August 16 at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin took control early against Hiroshima. In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin was ahead before the game had time to settle.

Starter Rihito Ihara gave the team a steady foundation. He worked five innings, allowed two hits, and gave up one run. That kept Hanshin in front while Hiroshima looked for a way back.

Hanshin did not stop there. The team added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings, turning its early advantage into a much larger one.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

It was a complete win. Hanshin had early power, a strong start from its pitcher, and enough offense later to finish the job.

### One run, but no lasting push

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. Ihara had allowed just two hits and one run over five innings. Hiroshima changed the score once, but never built a longer scoring attack. The home team’s pressure stopped there.

### More than one way to score

Sato’s home run created the first gap, but Hanshin did not depend on that one swing. In the seventh and eighth innings, Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Tora Fushimi added timely hits. The win had two offensive phases: early power, followed by runs created by different hitters.

## In one line…

Hanshin struck first, stayed steady, and kept adding until an early lead became an 8-1 statement in Hiroshima.
