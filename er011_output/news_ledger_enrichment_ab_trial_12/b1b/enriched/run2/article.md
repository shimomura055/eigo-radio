# A Fast Start and a Firm Finish: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8-1

On August 16, 2026, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin turned one big swing into a complete victory over Hiroshima.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took the lead before the game had time to settle.

That early advantage was supported by a steady start from Rihito Ihara. The Hanshin pitcher allowed only two hits and one run over five innings, giving Hiroshima little room to build an attack.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. But it did not change the shape of the game. Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings, extending its lead.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

This was not simply a game won by one early home run. Hanshin struck first, got a strong start from Ihara, and kept applying pressure until the finish.

### Ihara contributed with his bat, too

There was another side to Ihara’s night. The starting pitcher also went 2-for-2 at the plate, with singles in the third and fifth innings. It was the first multi-hit game of his professional career. His contribution reached both sides of the game.

### A clean handoff in the late innings

After Ihara left, four relievers each worked one inning. Together, they allowed one hit, gave up no walks, struck out five, and surrendered no runs. That control removed any late chance for Hiroshima to make the game competitive and completed Hanshin’s team effort.

## In one line…

Hanshin made the first move, protected its advantage, and finished the night with power, balance, and control.
