# One Early Swing, Then a Full Hanshin Victory

On August 16, 2026, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin took control before Hiroshima had time to settle.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato sent a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin led 2-0, and the game changed at once.

Rihito Ihara gave that lead a solid base. The Hanshin starter worked five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only score came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, but Hiroshima could add no more.

Hanshin scored again in the seventh and eighth innings, widening the gap late. The early lead became a comfortable finish.

Hanshin beat Hiroshima 8-1.

It was a game built in three steps: a quick opening punch, steady work from the starter, and more runs later.

### Ihara added his own bat to the story

Rihito Ihara added an unexpected extra layer. Away from the mound, he went 2-for-2, collecting singles in the third and fifth innings. It was the first multi-hit game of his professional career. His contribution was not limited to pitching; he also joined Hanshin’s attack.

### A personal milestone in a one-sided game

Montero’s home run carried its own milestone. It was his 10th of the season, giving him his first season in Japan with 10 or more home runs. That personal achievement came in a difficult team result, as Hiroshima could not build any more scoring around it.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned Sato’s early swing into an 8-1 win, with Ihara and the rest of the team carrying that advantage to the end.
