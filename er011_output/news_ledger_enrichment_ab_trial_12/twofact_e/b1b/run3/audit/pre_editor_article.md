# A Fast Start, a Steady Mound, and an 8-1 Finish

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, the Hanshin Tigers turned an early lead into a clear 8-1 win over the Hiroshima Carp.

Hanshin did not wait long to take control. In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. The Tigers quickly moved in front, 2-0.

But an early lead is only useful if a team can protect it. Starting pitcher Ryoto Ihara gave Hanshin the time it needed. He pitched five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, but the Carp could not build another scoring chance into a comeback.

Hanshin then added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead grew, and the game moved away from a close finish.

By the end, the story was simple: one early strike, steady work from the starting pitcher, and more points added later. The Tigers completed an 8-1 victory.

### One swing inside a team home-run race

Sato’s opening shot also had a more specific detail. It was his 28th home run, hit on the third pitch after one ball and one strike. The pitch was a two-seamer. That left Sato one home run behind teammate Shota Morishita, who had 29 at the end of the game.

### A win that changed the race for one night

This result mattered beyond the single score. Hanshin ended a two-game losing streak, stayed in first place, and kept a two-game lead over the Giants. Hiroshima’s own two-game winning streak ended, and the Carp missed a chance to win three straight for the first time since the opening series.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned one early swing and five steady innings into an 8-1 win that stopped a slide and protected its place at the top.
