# Hanshin Builds an 8-1 Win, One Step at a Time

## Main Story

Some wins are decided by one big moment. This one grew in stages.

On August 16, 2026, the Hanshin Tigers faced the Hiroshima Carp at Mazda Stadium. Hanshin made the first move in the opening inning.

Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field, giving Hanshin a 2-0 lead.

The lead got steady support from the mound. Hanshin starting pitcher Rihito Ihara worked five innings, allowing only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. Hiroshima could not add another run.

Hanshin scored again in the seventh and eighth innings. The extra runs widened the gap and gave Hiroshima little chance to come back.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

It was a clear Hanshin win built in a simple order: score first, keep the other team quiet, and add more later.

### A home run with a second story

Sato’s home run meant more than its two runs. It was his 28th home run, just one behind teammate Shota Morishita when the game ended. The swing came on Shohei Mori’s third pitch, a two-seam pitch, after a one-ball, one-strike count.

### A stop to the short slide

The win also changed the teams’ immediate direction. Hanshin stopped a two-game losing streak. Hiroshima’s two-game winning streak ended, and the Carp missed a chance to win three straight for the first time since the opening series. The result mattered to both teams in the short run.

## In one line…

Hanshin won by taking an early lead, trusting a steady start from Ihara, and adding pressure late. It was a complete 8-1 victory that stopped Hanshin’s slide and ended Hiroshima’s run.
