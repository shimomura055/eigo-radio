# An Early Blast, Then a Calm Finish: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8–1

On August 16, Hanshin faced Hiroshima at Mazda Stadium.

The game changed very early.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly took a 2–0 lead.

That early lead gave Hanshin control. Starting pitcher Rito Ihara kept Hiroshima from building a strong reply. He pitched five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, but Hiroshima could not add to it.

Hanshin did not let the game get close again. The team scored more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch hitter Tori Fushimi helped widen the gap.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

Hanshin controlled the game from the start. Sato created the first big turn, Ihara protected the lead, and the offense finished the job late.

### Sato’s big swing had a small window

Sato’s home run came with two outs and a runner on first. He hit a two-seam fastball on the third pitch, after a 1–1 count. It was also his 28th home run of the season, putting him just one behind teammate Shota Morishita. Hanshin turned one early chance into two important runs.

### A win that reset the race

This victory stopped Hanshin’s two-game losing streak. The team stayed in first place and kept a two-game lead over second-place Yomiuri at the end of the day. Hiroshima’s own two-game winning streak also ended. So, the result changed the mood around both teams, even though the standings at the top stayed the same.

## In one line…

Hanshin struck early, stayed steady, and finished strongly. The 8–1 win brought a clear victory and a needed return to winning form.
