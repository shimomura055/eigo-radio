# Hanshin Turns an Early Swing into an 8–1 Win

On August 16 at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin did not wait for the game to open up. The Tigers opened it themselves.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin led before Hiroshima could settle into the game.

Starting pitcher Ryoto Ihara gave that lead a strong base. He worked five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run. Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

Hanshin kept the game moving in its own direction. The Tigers added runs in the seventh and eighth innings, making the gap too large for a late comeback.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

Hanshin struck first, got a steady start from Ihara, and kept adding to its lead until the final out.

### Ihara’s night had a second side

Away from the mound, Ihara created another story. He went 2-for-2, with singles in the third and fifth innings. It was the first multi-hit game of his professional career. He stood out with both his arm and his bat.

### A milestone inside a losing night

Montero’s home run also had personal meaning. It was his 10th of the season, giving him his first season with double-digit home runs since he came to Japan. He had finished with nine home runs in 2025. Hiroshima lost, but Montero reached a clear mark in his second year in Japan.

## In one line…

One early blast set the direction, and steady work turned it into a complete Hanshin win—with personal milestones inside the 8–1 score.
