# One Big Swing, Then a Complete Win for Hanshin

On August 16, 2026, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin quickly took control against the Hiroshima Carp.

The first blow came in the opening inning, when Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin led 2-0 before the game had time to settle.

Starter Rihito Ihara protected that early lead. He pitched five innings, allowed two hits, and gave up one run. Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

Hiroshima had an answer, but not a second one. Hanshin added runs in the seventh and eighth innings, continuing to build its lead.

By the end, the gap was too large for Hiroshima to close. Hanshin beat Hiroshima 8-1. The game followed a clear path: an early home run created the advantage, Ihara held the middle, and the offense finished the job late.

For Hanshin, this was a win built step by step. For Hiroshima, one home run was all the offense could produce. Hanshin kept moving forward, while Hiroshima never found a way to build a real reply.

### Ihara made an impact in two ways

Ihara also made an impact at the plate. He singled in the third and fifth innings, going 2-for-2. It was his first multi-hit game as a professional. His contribution went beyond the mound and into Hanshin’s attack.

### A milestone inside a difficult result

Montero’s swing carried a different meaning for him. It was his first time reaching double-digit home runs in a season since coming to Japan. He had finished 2025 with nine. The moment did not change Hiroshima’s result, but it marked a clear season milestone for the hitter.

## In one line…

A fast start became an 8-1 finish: Hanshin controlled the game, while Ihara and Montero each reached a personal milestone in different ways.
