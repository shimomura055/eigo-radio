# A Fast Start, a Steady Hand, and an 8-1 Hanshin Win

On August 16, 2026, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin turned one big swing into a convincing 8-1 win over Hiroshima.

The Tigers struck first in the opening inning. Teruaki Sato sent a two-run home run to center field, giving Hanshin an early lead before the game had time to settle.

That lead was supported by starter Rihito Ihara. Over five innings, he allowed only two hits and one run. Hiroshima’s only score came in the fifth, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

But that one swing did not start a comeback.

Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. Timely hits from Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Torae Fushimi helped the Tigers pull further ahead.

By the end, the game was a clear Hanshin victory: an early home run, steady pitching, and enough late offense to close the door.

### Ihara made his mark at the plate

Ihara’s performance was not only about his work on the mound. He also went 2-for-2, with two singles. It was the first multi-hit game of his professional career.

That gave his night an unusual second side. The pitcher who helped keep Hiroshima quiet also contributed directly as a hitter.

### A milestone inside Hiroshima’s difficult night

Montero’s fifth-inning home run could not change the result, but it was still an important moment for him. It was his 10th home run of the season, giving him his first season with double-digit home runs since coming to Japan.

So, even in an 8-1 loss, the game included a personal milestone that the final score alone would not show.

## In one line…

Hanshin started fast, stayed steady, and finished strongly. The Tigers earned a complete win, while Ihara and Montero each added a personal milestone to the night.
