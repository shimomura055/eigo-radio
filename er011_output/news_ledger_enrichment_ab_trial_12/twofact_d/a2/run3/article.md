# A Fast Start, Then Full Control: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8–1

On August 16, 2026, the Hanshin Tigers took control early at Mazda Stadium.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly led 2–0.

The Tigers also got a strong start from Rihito Ihara. He worked five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run. His steady performance helped Hanshin hold its early lead.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, but the Carp could not build on it.

Hanshin added runs in the seventh inning and again in the eighth. The lead grew, and Hiroshima could not come back.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

A home run gave Hanshin the early lead. Ihara kept the game under control, and late runs made the result safe.

### Ihara made an impact at the plate, too

Ihara also contributed at the plate. He came to bat twice and got two hits, one in the third inning and one in the fifth. It was his first professional game with more than one hit. The starting pitcher also reached a rare personal mark as a hitter.

### Montero found a milestone in a difficult night

Montero’s fifth-inning home run had meaning beyond the game. It was his 10th home run of the season. In his second year in Japan, it was his first time reaching double figures in a season. Hiroshima lost, but Montero reached an important personal milestone.

## In one line

Hanshin turned one early swing into a complete victory: a quick lead, steady work from Ihara, and more runs late gave the Tigers an 8–1 win.
