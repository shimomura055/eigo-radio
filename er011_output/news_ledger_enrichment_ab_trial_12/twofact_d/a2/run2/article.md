# A Fast Start, a Strong Arm, and an 8-1 Hanshin Win

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin took control early.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin led 2-0 before Hiroshima could settle into the game.

The early lead had support from the mound. Hanshin starter Ryohto Ihara pitched five innings, allowing only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. But that was the Carp’s only score of the night.

Hanshin kept adding to its lead in the seventh and eighth innings. Hiroshima had no answer.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1-8 Hanshin

It was a complete win for Hanshin. The team struck first, received steady pitching, and kept adding to its lead late in the game.

### Ihara helped in two different ways

Ihara was strong as a pitcher, but he also made an unusual contribution at the plate. He hit singles in the third and fifth innings, finishing 2-for-2.

It was his first professional game with more than one hit. So, he not only pitched well but also helped Hanshin’s offense directly.

### A personal milestone in a difficult loss

Montero’s home run did not change the result, but it marked an important moment for him. It was his 10th home run of the 2026 season.

He finished 2025 with nine home runs. This was his first season in Japan with ten or more. Hiroshima lost the game, but Montero reached a new personal mark.

## In one line…

Hanshin took control early, protected its lead with steady play, and finished with an 8-1 win. Behind the final score were two extra stories: Ihara’s work with both his arm and his bat, and Montero’s first double-digit home run season in Japan.
