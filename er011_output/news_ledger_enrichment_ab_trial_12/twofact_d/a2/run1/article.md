# A Fast Start, Then Full Control for Hanshin

On August 16, Hanshin faced Hiroshima at Mazda Stadium. The game changed quickly in the first inning.

Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took a 2–0 lead before Hiroshima could settle.

That early lead gave starter Rihito Ihara room to work. Ihara pitched five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run. Hiroshima replied in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

But Hanshin did not lose control.

The Tigers added more runs in the seventh inning and scored again in the eighth. Each time, the lead grew, giving Hiroshima less chance to change the game.

This was a win built in stages. Sato created the early advantage. Ihara kept the game in Hanshin’s hands. Then the offense added more support late.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

Hanshin won clearly. The team did not depend on one moment alone. It started fast, stayed steady, and finished strongly.

### The pitcher had a bat story too

Ihara’s night had another achievement. He also collected singles in the third and fifth innings, going 2-for-2 as a hitter. It was his first multi-hit game as a professional. His performance stood out on both sides of the game.

### A milestone inside a difficult night

Montero’s home run carried personal meaning. It was his 10th of the season, his first season with at least 10 home runs in Japan. In his second year there, he had hit nine in 2025. The swing marked a clear step for him.

## In one line

Hanshin took control early, protected the lead, and pulled away late. It was a complete 8–1 win, with memorable moments for players on both teams.
