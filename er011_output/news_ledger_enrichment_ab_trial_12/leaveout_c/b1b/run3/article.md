# Hanshin Turns a Fast Start into an 8-1 Win

This looked like a game that might be decided by one swing. Instead, Hanshin kept building its lead.

At Mazda Stadium on August 16, the Hanshin Tigers took control early against the Hiroshima Toyo Carp. In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin led 2-0, and the game quickly took shape.

But the win was not built on one swing alone. Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara worked five innings, allowing only two hits and one run. Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

Hanshin added more offense later. The Tigers scored in the seventh and eighth innings. Seishiro Sakamoto delivered important hits, and pinch-hitter Tora Fushimi also drove in a run. The lead kept growing without coming under serious pressure.

By the end, Hiroshima had one run and Hanshin had eight. It was a convincing win built in stages: an early lead, a steady start from Ihara, and more offense late in the game.

### A team win in the numbers

The team numbers tell a wider story. Hanshin finished with 13 hits and no errors. Hiroshima had three hits and one error. That shows a strong performance at the plate and in the field, not just one moment that changed the game.

### A clean handoff to the finish

After Ihara left, Rito Kinoshita, Rafael Dolis, Yū Iwasaki, and Masataka Oikawa each pitched one inning. Together, they allowed one hit, issued no walks, struck out five, and gave up no runs. It was a clean handoff through the final four innings.

## In one line...

Hanshin started fast, kept adding to its lead, and stayed in control throughout the game. The 8-1 result was a complete team win, from Sato’s first-inning swing to the final out.
