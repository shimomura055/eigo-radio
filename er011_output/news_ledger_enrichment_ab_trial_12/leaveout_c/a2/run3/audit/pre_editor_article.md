# Hanshin Turns an Early Home Run into an 8-1 Win in Hiroshima

At Mazda Stadium on August 16, Hanshin did not wait for the game to become complicated.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took the lead before Hiroshima had time to settle in.

But an early lead is only a beginning. Hanshin’s starting pitcher, Rihito Ihara, gave that lead a strong base. He pitched five innings, allowed two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, but Hiroshima could not score again after that.

Hanshin kept adding pressure. The team scored more runs in the seventh and eighth innings, and the lead grew larger. Hiroshima never found a second big moment.

This was not a close finish. Hanshin made the first move, limited Hiroshima’s scoring, and kept moving forward late in the game.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

### The game stayed under control after Ihara

The quiet strength of the win came after the starting pitcher left. Kinoshita, Dolis, Iwasaki, and Oikawa each pitched one inning. Together, they allowed one hit, gave no walks, struck out five, and gave up no runs. Hiroshima had no late opening for a comeback.

### A power race inside Hanshin

Sato’s home run also changed the shape of Hanshin’s home-run race. It was his 28th of the season. Shota Morishita had 29 at the end of the game. That left Sato only one behind his teammate, giving the opening blast meaning beyond the two runs.

## In one line…

Hanshin won with a fast start, a steady middle, and a controlled finish. The 8-1 result was a complete performance, not just an early punch.
