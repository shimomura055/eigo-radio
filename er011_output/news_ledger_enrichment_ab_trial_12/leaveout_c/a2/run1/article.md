# Hanshin Turns a Fast Start into a Complete Win in Hiroshima

## Main Story

Some baseball games turn on one big moment. This one started that way, but Hanshin did not let it stay close.

On August 16 at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin quickly took control against the Hiroshima Toyo Carp.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took the lead before the game had time to settle.

Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara protected that lead. He worked five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, but Hiroshima could not score again.

Hanshin added to its lead in the seventh and eighth innings. The game slowly moved away from Hiroshima. The Carp needed a big response, but it never came.

When the game ended, Hanshin had won 8-1.

This was more than a win built on one early home run. Hanshin started strongly, got steady pitching from Ihara, and added more runs later. That made the game easier to control from beginning to end.

### The box score tells a bigger story

Hanshin controlled more than the final score. The team had 13 hits and made no errors. Hiroshima had only 3 hits and made 1 error. Hanshin kept pressure on at the plate and avoided the defensive mistake that Hiroshima made.

### A clean handoff at the end

After Ihara left, four Hanshin relief pitchers shared the final four innings. Together, they allowed only 1 hit, gave up no walks, struck out 5, and allowed no runs. Hiroshima had no late chance to change the game.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned an early lead into a complete team win: strong hitting, steady pitching, and a clean finish.
