# Hanshin Turns a Fast Start into a Complete 8-1 Win

On August 16, Hanshin faced Hiroshima at Mazda Stadium.

The game changed with one swing in the first inning.

With a runner on base, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly took a 2-0 lead.

That early lead gave starter Rihito Ihara room to work. Ihara held Hiroshima to two hits and one run over five innings.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, cutting the lead to 2-1.

For a moment, the home team had a chance to come back.

But Hanshin did not let the game turn into a close finish. The team added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead grew, and Hiroshima’s chance became smaller.

By the end, Hanshin had won 8-1.

This was a game built in stages. First came the big early swing. Then came steady pitching. Finally, Hanshin added enough runs to remove the tension.

### A pitcher who helped in two ways

Ihara did more than control the game from the mound. He also went 2-for-2 at the plate, with singles in the third and fifth innings. It was his first professional game with two hits. Hanshin received an unusual offensive contribution from its starting pitcher.

### A clean finish from the bullpen

After Ihara left, four relief pitchers each covered one inning: Rito Kinoshita, Rafael Dolis, Yū Iwasaki, and Masataka Oikawa. Together, they allowed only one hit, gave up no walks, struck out five, and allowed no runs. Hiroshima had no late opening.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned one early home run into a full-game performance: a strong start, help from an unexpected bat, and a quiet finish. It was an 8-1 win with very little room left for Hiroshima to fight back.
