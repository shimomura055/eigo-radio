# Hanshin Strikes First and Controls the Night

On August 16, 2026, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin took control early and never gave it back.

The first big moment came in the opening inning. With one runner on base, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field.

Hanshin quickly moved in front, 2-0.

But a fast start is not enough. Hiroshima still had many chances to answer.

Hanshin starter Rihito Ihara kept those chances small. He pitched five innings and allowed two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, cutting the gap to one run.

That was as close as Hiroshima came.

Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The game slowly moved away from the home team, and Hiroshima could not start another comeback.

The final score was:

Hanshin 8, Hiroshima 1.

It was a complete win built in stages: a strong first swing, steady work from the starting pitcher, and more pressure late in the game.

### Ihara helped with both his arm and his bat

Rihito Ihara gave Hanshin more than a solid start. He also went 2-for-2 at the plate, earning the first multi-hit game of his professional career. Hanshin finished with 13 hits, so the victory was supported by wider team offense, not only Sato’s early home run.

### A win that changed the week

This victory stopped Hanshin’s two-game losing streak. The team stayed in first place and kept a two-game lead over Yomiuri. Hiroshima’s own two-game winning streak ended, and the Carp missed their first three-game winning streak since the opening series.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned one early swing into a full-team win, with Ihara helping on both sides of the game.
