# Hanshin Turned an Early Lead into a Clear 8–1 Win

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, the Hanshin Tigers beat the Hiroshima Toyo Carp 8–1.

The game had two important moments: one early blow, and a much bigger finish.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin moved in front before the game had fully settled.

Hanshin starter Rihito Ihara then gave the team a strong middle. He pitched five innings, allowing only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

That gave Hiroshima a chance, but the Carp could not build a larger attack. Hanshin kept its advantage and added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings.

Seishiro Sakamoto brought runners home with his hits. Pinch-hitter Tai Fushimi also drove in a run.

The Tigers turned a good start into a wide gap. The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

### The big gap came late

The final 8–1 score may make the game sound easy from the beginning. But after Montero’s fifth-inning solo home run, Hanshin led only 2–1. The big difference came in the seventh and eighth. A close game became a one-sided finish near the end.

### More than one way to score

Hanshin did not depend on Sato’s one big swing. It used power early and hits that brought runners home later. Ihara also kept Hiroshima’s chances small, allowing two hits and one run in five innings. Hiroshima got its only run from Montero’s solo homer. In this game, Hanshin had several ways to score; Hiroshima had one.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned a quick start into a late breakaway, winning 8–1 with both strong pitching and more than one way to score.
