# The Early Blow That Became a Complete Hanshin Win

One swing gave Hanshin the lead. The rest of the game showed whether that advantage could last.

At Mazda Stadium on Sunday, August 16, Teruaki Sato answered that question in the first inning. He hit a two-run home run to center field, putting Hanshin ahead 2–0.

Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara then gave the team a steady foundation. He worked through five innings and kept Hanshin in control of the game.

Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The early lead grew larger, and Hiroshima could not match the pace.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win, built first by one big swing and then by a team effort that kept adding to the lead.

### More than one hitter finished the job

The late attack came from several contributors. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in a run in the seventh inning. Pinch-hitter Taii Fushimi added another in the same inning. Sakamoto then hit an RBI double in the eighth. Hanshin did not leave the result resting on one swing.

### Hiroshima’s response stayed narrow

Elehuris Montero’s solo home run in the fifth inning was Hiroshima’s only run. Ihara had allowed just two hits in his five innings. The record shows one scoring play, not a sustained rally, while stopping short of saying what the players felt.

## In one line…

Hanshin struck first, strengthened its lead late, and turned a close beginning into an 8–1 statement at Mazda Stadium.
