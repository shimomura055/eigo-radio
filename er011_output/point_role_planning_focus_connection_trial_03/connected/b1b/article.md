# The Early Blow That Became a Complete Hanshin Win

One swing gave Hanshin the lead. The rest of the game showed whether it would last.

At Mazda Stadium on Sunday, August 16, Teruaki Sato answered that question in the first inning with a two-run home run to center field, putting Hanshin ahead 2–0.

Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara gave the team a steady foundation. He worked through five innings and kept Hanshin in control.

Hanshin added runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead grew, and Hiroshima could not match the pace.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win, built by one big swing and a team effort that kept adding to the lead.

### More than one hitter finished the job

Several players contributed to the late attack. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in a run in the seventh inning, and pinch-hitter Taii Fushimi added another. Sakamoto then hit an RBI double in the eighth. Hanshin did not leave the result resting on one swing.

### Hiroshima’s response stayed narrow

Elehuris Montero’s solo home run in the fifth inning was Hiroshima’s only run. Ihara allowed just two hits in his five innings. The record shows one scoring play, not a sustained rally, while stopping short of saying what the players felt.

## In one line…

Hanshin struck first, strengthened its lead late, and turned a close beginning into an 8–1 statement at Mazda Stadium.
