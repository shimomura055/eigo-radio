# One Big Start, Then Steady Pressure: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8-1

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin took control early against Hiroshima.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field, putting Hanshin ahead 2-0.

Starting pitcher Rito Ihara gave the team a steady foundation. He worked five innings, allowed two hits, and gave up one run. Hiroshima’s only score came in the fifth, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run.

That cut Hanshin’s lead, but Hiroshima could not build a larger attack. Hanshin added runs in the seventh and eighth innings, and Hiroshima could not close the gap.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win built on an early advantage, steady pitching, and more runs later in the game.

### More than one big swing

Sato’s home run gave Hanshin its first push, but the offense did not depend on that moment. In the seventh inning, Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Tora Fushimi each delivered an RBI hit. Sakamoto added another RBI with a double in the eighth.

That variety mattered. Hanshin widened the gap through several contributors and different kinds of hits, rather than waiting for another huge home run.

### Hiroshima’s answer stayed small

Ihara did not have a perfect, scoreless outing. His contribution was damage control. Montero’s home run gave Hiroshima one run, but it was a solo shot, and the team did not build a rally from it.

Ihara allowed only two hits over five innings, while Hiroshima finished with just that one scoring play. Its only counterattack was real, but it stayed isolated. That helped Hanshin hold its early lead before adding more runs later.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned Sato’s early two-run spark into a complete 8-1 victory, while Hiroshima’s only reply was a single solo home run.
