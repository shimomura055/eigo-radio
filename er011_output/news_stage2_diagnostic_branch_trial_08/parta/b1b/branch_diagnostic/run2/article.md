Starting pitcher Ryoto Ihara gave that lead a strong base. He worked five innings, allowing only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, making the score 2-1. But that was Hiroshima’s only run all night.

Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The advantage grew, and the game moved away from a close finish.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.
