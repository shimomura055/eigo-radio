# A Fast Start, Then Full Control for Hanshin

On August 16, 2026, at Mazda Stadium, the Hanshin Tigers made their move before Hiroshima could settle into the game.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly took the lead.

Then starter Rito Ihara kept the game in their hands. He worked through five innings, allowing only two hits and one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. It gave the home team one score, but it did not start a larger comeback.

Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The early lead grew wider, and Hiroshima never found a way back.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

This was a win built in two steps: an early blow, followed by steady control.

### The late attack had more than one hero

The late scoring was not carried by one player. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in a run in the seventh and hit another RBI double in the eighth. In the seventh, pinch-hitter Tairiku Fushimi also delivered. That spread the work across the lineup and turned an early lead into a much safer advantage.

### No big rally for Hiroshima

The shape of Hiroshima’s scoring tells us something important about this game. It never became a long, connected rally. Ihara finished five innings after allowing only two hits and one run, and Hiroshima had no other scoring play beyond Montero’s solo shot. This shows Hanshin’s control in this game, but not a proven season-long change.

## In one line

Hanshin struck first, kept Hiroshima quiet, and added enough late pressure to turn a quick lead into a complete 8-1 victory.
