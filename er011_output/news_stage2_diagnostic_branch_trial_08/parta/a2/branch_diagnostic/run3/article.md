# Hanshin Strikes Early and Pulls Away in Hiroshima

On August 16 at Mazda Stadium, the Hanshin Tigers started fast against the Hiroshima Carp.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin led 2-0.

Hanshin’s starting pitcher, Rito Ihara, then gave his team five steady innings. Hiroshima answered once in the middle of the game, but Hanshin stayed ahead.

The Tigers added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The early lead became a wide gap, and Hiroshima could not bring the game back to a close contest.

When the final out came, Hanshin had won 8-1.

It was a clear win built across the game: an early lead, steady pitching, and more scoring later.

### More than one big swing

The first home run was the trigger, not the whole attack. Later, Seishiro Sakamoto and Tora Fushimi, who entered as a replacement hitter, brought in runs with timely hits. Hanshin used power first and different hitters later. The lead was extended by the team, not by one swing alone.

### One answer, but no long attack

Hiroshima was not held scoreless. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run in the fifth inning. But it was the home team’s only run, while Ihara allowed just two hits and one run in five innings. One good swing appeared, but no continuing attack followed.

## In one line…

Hanshin opened the game with power, then finished it with steady pitching and support from the whole lineup.
