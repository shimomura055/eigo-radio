# A Quick Start, Then Complete Control: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8-1

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin turned an early advantage into an 8-1 win over Hiroshima.

The first signal came immediately. In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took the lead before the game had time to settle.

Then starter Ryoto Ihara gave that lead a steady base. He worked five innings, and Hiroshima scored only once during his outing. Hanshin did not allow the game to swing back after that.

The Tigers added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. Each extra run made the gap wider and reduced the danger of a late fight.

When the final out arrived, Hanshin had won 8-1. The shape of the game was clear: an early strike, steady control, and a stronger finish.

### More than one bat carried the offense

Sato supplied the opening burst, but the later scoring came from different places. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in runs in the seventh and eighth, while pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi added an RBI in the seventh. The first blow had support after the spotlight moved on.

### One home run, but no lasting attack

Hiroshima’s one scoring play was Elehuris Montero’s solo home run in the fifth. Ihara allowed only two hits and one run over five innings. That meant Hiroshima could not turn one successful swing into continued pressure. The 8-1 score shows containment, not a shutout.

## In one line…

Hanshin struck first, kept Hiroshima’s offense from growing, and finished with an 8-1 win built on both control and continued scoring.
