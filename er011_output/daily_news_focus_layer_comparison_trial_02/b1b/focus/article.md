# A Quick Start, Then Complete Control: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8-1

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin turned an early lead into an 8-1 win over Hiroshima.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took the lead before the game had time to settle.

Starter Ryoto Ihara kept that lead steady. He worked five innings, and Hiroshima scored only once. Hanshin did not let the game swing back.

The Tigers added runs in the seventh and eighth innings. Each one widened the gap and reduced the danger of a late fight.

When the final out arrived, Hanshin had won 8-1. The game had a clear shape: an early strike, steady control, and a stronger finish.

### More than one bat carried the offense

Sato supplied the opening burst, but the later scoring came from elsewhere. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in runs in the seventh and eighth, while pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi added an RBI in the seventh. The first blow had support after the spotlight moved on.

### One home run, but no lasting attack

Hiroshima’s only scoring play was Elehuris Montero’s solo home run in the fifth. Ihara allowed only two hits and one run over five innings. Hiroshima could not turn one successful swing into continued pressure. The 8-1 score shows containment, not a shutout.

## In one line…

Hanshin struck first, kept Hiroshima’s offense from growing, and finished with an 8-1 win built on control and continued scoring.
