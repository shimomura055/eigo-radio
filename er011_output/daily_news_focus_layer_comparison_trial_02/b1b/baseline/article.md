# The Early Lead Was Only the Beginning: Hanshin Beat Hiroshima 8–1

At Mazda Stadium on August 16, 2026, Hanshin took the lead right away.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin went ahead 2–0, and the Tigers quickly took control.

But a two-run lead is not a finished story.

Hiroshima answered in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run, cutting the score to 2–1. It was the Carp’s only run of the night.

Hanshin starter Rihito Ihara gave his team a steady foundation. He pitched five innings, allowing two hits and one run.

After that, Hanshin added runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead grew, and Hiroshima could not close the gap.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

The game began with one big swing. It ended with Hanshin creating a much wider difference.

### The moment the game could have changed

Hiroshima’s fifth-inning home run was not just a run on the board. It was the one moment when the Carp pulled within one. That was the game’s pressure point. Hanshin did not allow that narrow gap to become a longer rally, and later scoring turned a close 2–1 game into the final 8–1 margin.

### More than one answer at the plate

The early home run may be the easy image to remember, but the late margin had several contributors. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in a run in the seventh and added an RBI double in the eighth. Pinch-hitter Taii Fushimi also delivered an RBI hit. This was not one swing alone.

## In one line…

Hanshin turned a first-inning spark into a complete 8–1 victory: Hiroshima found one opening, but the Tigers finished the game with steady pitching and contributions from different roles.
