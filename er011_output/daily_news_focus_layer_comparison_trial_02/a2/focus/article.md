# A Fast Start, Then No Way Back: Hanshin Beats Hiroshima 8-1

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, the Hanshin Tigers beat the Hiroshima Toyo Carp 8-1.

The game changed very early.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly led 2-0.

Starter Ryoto Ihara gave the Tigers a strong start. He pitched five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. The score became 2-1, but Hiroshima could not score again.

Hanshin kept building its lead. The Tigers scored again in the seventh and eighth innings. The game moved away from Hiroshima one step at a time.

By the end, Hanshin had turned an early lead into an 8-1 win. It was not just a game decided by one big hit. Hanshin kept control in different ways, and Hiroshima never found a way to start a real comeback.

### The win came from more than one hitter

The late scoring showed the depth of Hanshin’s lineup. Seishiro Sakamoto brought a run home in both the seventh and eighth innings. Taii Fushimi also brought in a run after entering as a hitter. Hiroshima had to face several contributors, not just Sato.

### One home run, but no rally

Ihara did not need a shutout to make his start valuable. He allowed one run in five innings, and it came from Montero’s solo home run. After that, Hiroshima scored no more. One strong swing created a moment, but it never became lasting pressure.

## In one line…

Hanshin won with an early blast, several late contributors, and pitching that kept one Hiroshima home run from becoming a rally. A quick 2-0 start became a complete 8-1 victory.
