# The Small Refrigerator Slider That Helps Fresh Food Last Longer

A tiny slider in a refrigerator can look like a minor detail. It may say “low humidity” on one side and “high humidity” on the other. But that small choice changes the kind of space fresh food gets.

Many crisper drawers have these two settings, or a slider that moves between them. The low-humidity setting allows more air to move through. The high-humidity setting is more closed.

This is not simply a choice between fruit and vegetables. Fresh produce behaves in different ways while it is stored. Some foods release a gas that can affect nearby food. Others lose water quickly and begin to wilt.

Guidance from Iowa State University Extension shows why the setting matters. Used with the right food, the drawer can help produce stay in better condition for longer. But there is an important step before anyone moves the slider: ask whether that food belongs in the refrigerator at all.

### Sort food by what it does in storage

A useful test is to look at the food’s behavior, not just its name. Apples and pears release ethylene, a gas that can make nearby produce ripen and spoil sooner. They are examples of foods that fit the low-humidity side, where air can move more freely.

Leafy greens, such as kale, and vegetables such as broccoli can lose water quickly. They are better matches for the high-humidity side, which helps them hold on to moisture. This gives the drawer a practical sorting rule: think about what the food releases or loses.

### The slider is not the first question

The humidity setting cannot make every food suitable for refrigeration. Tomatoes and bananas are recommended for counter storage, rather than a crisper drawer.

Potatoes, sweet potatoes, onions, and garlic do not need to be refrigerated. They are recommended for a cool, dry environment. That does not automatically mean the same place as a kitchen counter.

So before choosing a drawer setting, first check whether the food belongs in the refrigerator. Only then is it useful to decide which humidity setting fits its storage needs.

## In one line

The best use of a crisper drawer starts with two questions: what does this food do in storage, and should it be in the refrigerator at all?
