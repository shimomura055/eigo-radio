以下は、同じ記事から選ばれた5件のKey Phrase(英語学習ポッドキャストの
リスニング教材)です。5件から作れる全てのペア(10組)について、
それぞれ以下4つの観点で意味重複を判定してください(それぞれPASS=重複なし/
FAIL=重複あり)。

- meaning_overlap: 2つのkey_phraseが表す意味内容が、実質的に同じか(例:
  「注意を引く」と「注意の一部を奪う」は表現は違っても同じ「注意を奪われる」
  という概念を指しており、meaning_overlap=FAILとすべきです)
- usage_context_overlap: 2つの表現が使われる典型的な場面・文脈が、実質的に
  同じか
- grammatical_teaching_value_overlap: 2つの表現が学習者に教える文法・構文上の
  価値(例: 句動詞の型、比喩の型、コロケーションの型)が、実質的に同じか
- conceptual_role_overlap: 記事の中でこの2つの表現が担っている概念・役割が、
  実質的に同じか

is_near_duplicateは、上記4観点のうち2つ以上がFAILの場合、または1つでも
明確にFAILで「単なる言い換え、または同じ内容を別の比喩・表現で表しただけ」
と判断できる場合にtrueにしてください。reasoningには判定理由を1〜2文の
日本語で書いてください。

単に同じ記事・同じ話題について書かれているために表面的な語彙(記事固有の
固有名詞やトピック語)が共通することは、重複とはみなさないでください。
判定対象はあくまで、Key Phraseそのものが教える意味・用法・学習価値です。

pairsには、必ず10組全ての組み合わせを含めてください(rank_a < rank_b)。

# The Strait’s Numbers Tell Two Different Stories

This conflict now has an unusual shape. The route is not simply open or closed. Oil can move on some days, while the wider economic system remains under pressure.

The United States and Israel began airstrikes on Iran on February 28. A ceasefire memorandum was agreed on June 14, and the blockade was lifted four days later. But after Iran attacked three merchant ships outside the approved route, the blockade resumed on July 14.

Since then, pressure has appeared in several places at once. Shipping has remained difficult. Energy production has also been hit. Diplomacy has not produced a new agreement. On August 18, President Trump said that no talks with Iran were scheduled. The 60-day negotiation period then passed without an agreement.

A private political-risk firm withdrew its earlier expectation of peace by September and moved its forecast for easing tensions to the end of the year. In early September, one report said that a month-long lull had ended and that the region was moving back toward a dangerous and unsustainable military stalemate.

Taken together, these signals point toward a conflict becoming a long economic war of exhaustion. But the numbers do not all tell the same story.

### A record day is not a recovery

The headline number is hard to read. The energy secretary said more than 17 million barrels a day passed through Hormuz on August 31, calling it a wartime record. But the official quarterly average fell from roughly 20 to 22 million barrels before the war to about 5 million in the second quarter of 2026. Another tracking report put the figure at about 9.1 million barrels for the same day across the Arabian Sea, using a different area and counting method. One peak cannot by itself prove recovery.

### Open water is not normal commerce

The key distinction is between access and normal commerce. The US Navy said it cleared the main route, but did not publish a mine count. An anonymous US official cited more than 200 “mine-like objects,” with only 11 confirmed mines; the maritime threat level stayed SEVERE. An opposition media group reported an Arvand petrochemical shutdown and about 230 million cubic meters a day of lost gas capacity since the war began. Traffic can return without restoring normal commerce.

## In one line…

The signs point toward a longer economic war of attrition, but the mixed shipping evidence makes this a direction of travel, not a settled result.
