# Hanshin Turns an Early Lead into a Complete Win in Hiroshima

At Mazda Stadium on August 16, 2026, Hanshin took control before Hiroshima could settle in.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly took the lead.

Hanshin also had a strong foundation on the mound. Starting pitcher Rihito Ihara worked five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

That run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run for Hiroshima. It was the Carp’s only score.

Hanshin did not let that moment change the result. The Tigers added runs in the seventh and eighth innings, and the gap continued to grow.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

It was a complete Hanshin victory. Sato created the early advantage, Ihara limited Hiroshima’s scoring, and the team finished by making its lead even bigger.

The late cushion had more than one source. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in a run in the seventh, then hit a run-scoring double in the eighth. Tora Fushimi also drove in a run as a pinch-hitter. The final margin came from several contributions, not just one early swing.
