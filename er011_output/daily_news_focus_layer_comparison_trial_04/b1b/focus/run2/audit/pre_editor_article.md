# One Early Swing, Then a Complete Hanshin Win

On August 16 at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin did not wait long to take control.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato sent a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly moved in front, 2–0.

But the win was not built on one swing alone.

Starter Ryoto Ihara gave Hanshin a steady base. Hiroshima found one answer during the game, but Hanshin stayed in control. Later, Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings.

The early lead had become a wide gap.

Hiroshima’s only run came in the fifth inning, when Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. After that, Hiroshima could not create another serious threat.

The final score was:

Hiroshima 1–8 Hanshin

It was a complete win for Hanshin, from the first big hit to the final score.

### The lead survived one clear challenge

The important contrast was not simply how many runs Hanshin scored. Hiroshima never built a long rally. Its only run came on Montero’s solo shot, while Ihara allowed just two hits and one run over five innings. The early lead faced one clear challenge, not repeated waves of pressure.

### More than one hitter finished the job

Late in the game, the scoring had more than one source. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in a run in the seventh and added an RBI double in the eighth. Pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi also produced in the seventh. Hanshin’s win was therefore not carried by Sato’s opening home run alone.

## In one line…

Hanshin struck first, kept Hiroshima’s offense under control, and finished the night with a powerful 8–1 victory.
