# A Two-Run Start Becomes a Complete Win for Hanshin

At Mazda Stadium on August 16, the Hanshin Tigers took control early.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly led, 2-0.

That early lead was supported by a strong start from pitcher Rinto Iharu. He worked five innings and allowed only two hits and one run. Hiroshima had few chances while he was on the mound.

Hanshin added to its lead in the later innings. More runs came in the seventh and eighth. The team did not need a dramatic finish. It built a safe gap and kept Hiroshima out of the game.

The final score was Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

For Hanshin, the story was simple but powerful: one quick swing started the game, steady pitching protected the lead, and more hitting finished the job.

### The win came from more than one hitter

Sato opened the game with the big blow, but Hanshin did not depend on that one swing. Seishiro Sakamoto drove in runs in both the seventh and eighth innings. Substitute hitter Torai Fushimi also drove in a run. The win became a team effort, with help from the bench and the starting lineup.

### Hiroshima had one answer, but no steady attack

The scoring pattern tells us something important. Hiroshima’s only run came from Elehuris Montero’s solo home run in the fifth inning. The verified record shows no multi-run scoring sequence from Hiroshima. It does not explain why more runs did not follow, but it does show that Hanshin was not escaping repeated rallies.

## In one line…

One early swing started the night, but steady pitching and contributions from several hitters turned it into a complete Hanshin victory.
