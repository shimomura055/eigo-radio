# Hanshin Turns One Big Swing into an 8-1 Win

On August 16, at Mazda Stadium, Hanshin got off to the right start and never gave Hiroshima a real chance to catch up.

In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin quickly moved ahead, 2-0.

Starter Ryoto Ihara helped protect that lead. He pitched five innings, allowed only two hits, and gave up one run.

Hiroshima’s run came in the fifth inning. Elehuris Montero hit a solo home run. Hiroshima was on the scoreboard, but Hanshin stayed in front.

Hanshin added more runs in the seventh and eighth innings. The lead grew, and the game moved further away from Hiroshima.

The final result was:

Hiroshima 1, Hanshin 8.

### Hiroshima’s answer was only one blow

Hiroshima did get on the scoreboard, but its reply had a clear limit. Montero’s fifth-inning home run was a solo shot and Hiroshima’s only run of the game. It was one strong answer, not a long scoring attack. That helped keep Hanshin’s early advantage safe.

### More than one hitter finished the job

Hanshin’s late attack came from different parts of the lineup. Seishiro Sakamoto brought in a run in the seventh and hit another run-scoring double in the eighth. Substitute batter Tairiku Fushimi also brought in a run in the seventh. The win was not built on Sato’s swing alone.

## In one line…

One early home run opened the door, but steady pitching and help from several hitters carried Hanshin to a complete 8-1 victory.
