# An Early Lead Becomes a Complete Win in Hiroshima

On August 16, 2026, the Hanshin Tigers faced the Hiroshima Carp at Mazda Stadium.

The game began quickly. In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin took a 2-0 lead.

But that early lead did not make the game easy. Hiroshima scored once in the middle of the game, and Hanshin needed steady pitching to stay ahead.

Rihito Ihara did that over the first five innings. He kept Hiroshima’s offense quiet and gave his team a chance to hold the lead.

Then Hanshin pulled away. The Tigers scored again in the seventh and eighth innings. Hiroshima could not answer, and the early lead grew wide.

Hanshin won 8-1. It was a victory built in stages: a strong start, steady work in the middle, and more runs late.

### The close middle mattered

After five innings, the score was only 2-1, not a runaway. Hiroshima had come within one run after Elehuris Montero’s solo home run. But its attack stopped there. Ihara’s five innings, two hits, and one run allowed kept Hanshin in front until the team scored more late.

### Two different ways to score

The two teams scored in very different ways. Hiroshima’s only run came from one solo home run. Hanshin used Sato’s early two-run shot, then added runs through hits by Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi. Hanshin had more than one way to build its lead.

## In one line…

Hanshin did more than strike first. It held a narrow lead through the middle of the game, then widened it with more scoring to complete an 8-1 win.
