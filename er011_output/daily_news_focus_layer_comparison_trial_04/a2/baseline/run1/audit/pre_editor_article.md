# An Early Lead Becomes a Complete Win in Hiroshima

On August 16, 2026, the Hanshin Tigers faced the Hiroshima Carp at Mazda Stadium.

The game began with a quick attack. In the first inning, Teruaki Sato hit a two-run home run to center field. Hanshin moved in front, 2-0.

But the early lead did not make the game easy right away. Hiroshima answered once in the middle of the game. Hanshin needed steady pitching to keep control.

Rihito Ihara did that over the first five innings. He kept Hiroshima’s attack small and gave his team a chance to stay ahead.

Then Hanshin pulled away. The Tigers scored again in the seventh and eighth innings. Hiroshima could not answer, and the early lead became a wide one.

Hanshin won 8-1. It was a victory built in stages: a strong start, calm work in the middle, and more runs late.

### The close middle mattered

After five innings, the score was only 2-1, not a runaway. Hiroshima had come within one run after Elehuris Montero’s solo home run. But its attack stopped there. Ihara’s five innings, two hits, and one run allowed kept Hanshin in front until the team added more late.

### Two different ways to score

The two teams scored in very different ways. Hiroshima’s only run came from one solo home run. Hanshin used Sato’s early two-run shot, then added runs through hits by Seishiro Sakamoto and pinch-hitter Torai Fushimi. Hanshin had more than one way to build its lead.

## In one line…

Hanshin did more than strike first. It held a narrow lead through the middle of the game, then widened it with more scoring to complete an 8-1 win.
