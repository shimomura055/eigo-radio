AUse natural, expressive speech with variations in pace and intonation that fit the character's emotions. Avoid unnaturally stretching words or adding excessively long pauses for dramatic effect. Keep the dialogue flowing at a believable conversational pace.
BPerform the dialogue with the character's emotions clearly expressed through tone, emphasis, and intonation. Let the pace change naturally when the scene calls for it, but do not rely on prolonged syllables or exaggerated pauses to create emotion. Keep the speech lively, fluid, and natural.
CSpeak as if the character is talking to another person in a real conversation, not performing a dramatic speech for an audience. React naturally to the meaning of each line, with spontaneous changes in pace and emphasis. Avoid theatrical pauses, drawn-out words, or overly deliberate delivery.
