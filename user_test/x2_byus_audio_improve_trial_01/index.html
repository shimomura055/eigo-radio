Standardの英文本文（Part 1〜3）

Part 1

Imagine this. You are reading a love story on your phone. Your favorite character is about to say something important.

Then the episode ends.

“No! Just say you like her!”

Next week feels very far away.

But what if you could enter the story and speak first?

That is the idea behind byUs, an AI story chat service. It came to LINE Manga in Japan in October 2026.

You become the main character. You type a message, and an AI version of a manga character answers. Together, you make a new story.

So, back to our love story. This time, you type:

“I like you.”

Now it is his turn.

But what do you really want? A sweet answer—or an answer that feels like him?

Part 2

The exciting part is not just getting an answer. It is feeling that the character knows you.

A Korean writer who tried byUs said the AI remembered earlier conversations. It used those details later in the story. That made the chat feel more real.

Think about talking to someone who remembers your favorite drink. A small detail can make you smile.

But the same review also described a funny problem.

The characters were talking in a café. Then the story described the place as an office.

Wait. An office?

Where did the coffee go? Are we on a date, or do we have a meeting?

One strange detail can break the feeling.

And a strange place is not the only possible problem.

What if the character’s words feel wrong, too?

Part 3

Let’s return to our imagined love story.

Your favorite character is quiet. He finds it hard to talk about his feelings. That is part of why you like him.

Now imagine he suddenly sends you ten sweet messages.

“You are my whole world!”

At first, you smile.

Then you think: “Would he really say that?”

byUs uses works with the original authors’ permission, according to LINE Manga. But permission to use a character does not promise that every AI reply will feel right.

That is the tricky part.

You want to change the story. But you may not want to change the person you love inside it.

So your phone lights up. His answer is here.

Do you want him to say exactly what you hoped?

Or would you rather hear something less perfect—and think, “Yes. That sounds like him”?

Advancedの英文本文（Part 1〜3）

Part 1

You’re reading a romance manga. Your favorite character walks the heroine home. They stop under a streetlight. He takes a breath…

And the chapter ends.

“Oh, come ON.”

Now imagine opening a chat window and typing the words nobody in the manga seems able to say:

“I like you.”

That impatience is the perfect invitation to byUs, an AI story-chat service launched on LINE Manga in Japan in October 2026.

Instead of watching the romance from outside, you step into the main character’s place and talk to the characters yourself. In fan-created stories, you can set up new situations and relationships, and share your stories with other users.

The characters are used with their original creators’ permission.

So, finally, you can stop shouting at the page.

You have the keyboard.

Part 2

And sometimes, the conversation can feel surprisingly convincing.

A Korean journalist testing byUs reported that it remembered earlier conversations and used them later in the story.

Think about why that matters. A character saying, “How was your day?” is pleasant. Remembering something you mentioned earlier can make it feel as though they were actually listening.

But the same review described a problem: a conversation taking place in a café suddenly had an office as its setting.

Imagine looking up from your imaginary coffee.

Wait. Why is there a printer beside our table?

That mistake is funny because it is obvious. You know the scene has gone wrong.

But another reaction points to something trickier. One Japanese blogger described feeling irritated by the character’s smooth talk rather than won over.

Sometimes, getting a charming reply isn’t the same as getting the reply you wanted.

Part 3

Go back to that streetlight.

Imagine your favorite character is shy. Saying hello is practically an athletic event for him.

Now imagine the AI gives him a flawless romantic speech.

You finally got your confession.

But would *he* really say that?

That is the interesting tension here. You can change the situation, start the conversation, and push the romance forward. Yet push too far, and you might lose the awkward, frustrating person you liked in the first place.

The café turning into an office is easy to notice.

A beloved character turning into someone who always says exactly what you want? That might take longer.

Your finger is hovering over “Send.”

Do you want him to confess?

Or do you want him to be himself?
