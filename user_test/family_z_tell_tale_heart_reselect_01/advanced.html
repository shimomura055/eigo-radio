I stood motionless. For an hour he sat in bed, listening. At last he groaned softly. Not pain—terror. I knew that sound. I uncovered the lantern. Its narrow beam found the eye. Wide open. A low, quick beating reached me, like a watch wrapped in cotton. His heart! Faster it went, and louder. What if a neighbour heard? I rushed forward. He shrieked once. I dragged him down and pulled the heavy bed over him. Finally, the beating stopped. I checked his pulse. Nothing. I hid his body beneath three floorboards and fitted them back exactly. Not a mark! You could have searched all night. At four in the morning, three police officers knocked. A neighbour had heard a shriek.
