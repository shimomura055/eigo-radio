The number of people varied by analysis, but the researchers used data from as many as 134,515 people. In every study, the same people’s income and personality were measured at least three times. The key was how the researchers compared people. They analyzed separately the differences between people that tend not to change and changes that happen within each person. Not, “What’s different between me and that person who earns a lot?” But, “How are my former self and my later self different?” In a way, the researchers shifted from comparing yourself with others to keeping track of yourself over time.
