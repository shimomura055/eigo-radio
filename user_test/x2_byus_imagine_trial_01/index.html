When Manga Characters Text Back

Part 1: “Just Say It!”

Imagine this. You are reading a love story on your phone. Your favorite character is about to say something important.

Then the episode ends.

“No! Just say you like her!”

Next week feels very far away.

But what if you could enter the story and speak first?

That is the idea behind byUs, an AI story chat service. It came to LINE Manga in Japan in October 2026.

You become the main character. You type a message, and an AI version of a manga character answers. Together, you make a new story.

So, back to our love story. This time, you type:

“I like you.”

Now it is his turn.

But what do you really want? A sweet answer—or an answer that feels like him?

Part 2: A Date… but Where?

The exciting part is not just getting an answer. It is feeling that the character knows you.

A Korean writer who tried byUs said the AI remembered earlier conversations. It used those details later in the story. That made the chat feel more real.

Think about talking to someone who remembers your favorite drink. A small detail can make you smile.

But the same review also described a funny problem.

The characters were talking in a café. Then the story described the place as an office.

Wait. An office?

Where did the coffee go? Are we on a date, or do we have a meeting?

One strange detail can break the feeling.

And a strange place is not the only possible problem.

What if the character’s words feel wrong, too?

Part 3: Too Perfect to Be Him?

Let’s return to our imagined love story.

Your favorite character is quiet. He finds it hard to talk about his feelings. That is part of why you like him.

Now imagine he suddenly sends you ten sweet messages.

“You are my whole world!”

At first, you smile.

Then you think: “Would he really say that?”

byUs uses works with the original authors’ permission, according to LINE Manga. But permission to use a character does not promise that every AI reply will feel right.

That is the tricky part.

You want to change the story. But you may not want to change the person you love inside it.

So your phone lights up. His answer is here.

Do you want him to say exactly what you hoped?

Or would you rather hear something less perfect—and think, “Yes. That sounds like him”?
