Then the episode ends.

“No! Just say you like her!”

But what if you could enter the story and speak first?

That is the idea behind byUs, an AI story chat service. It came to LINE Manga in Japan in October 2026.

You become the main character. You type a message, and an AI version of a manga character answers. Together, you make a new story.

So, back to our love story. This time, you type:

“I like you.”

Now it is his turn.

But what do you really want? A sweet answer—or an answer that feels like him?

英文（Part 1）

You’re reading a romance manga. Your favorite character walks the heroine home. They stop under a streetlight. He takes a breath…

And the chapter ends.

“Oh, come ON.”

Now imagine opening a chat window and typing the words nobody in the manga seems able to say:

“I like you.”

That impatience is the perfect invitation to byUs, an AI story-chat service launched on LINE Manga in Japan in October 2026.

Instead of watching the romance from outside, you step into the main character’s place and talk to the characters yourself. In fan-created stories, you can set up new situations and relationships, and share your stories with other users.

The characters are used with their original creators’ permission.

So, finally, you can stop shouting at the page.

You have the keyboard.
