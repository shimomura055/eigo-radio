### 1. The Friend Who Saves You Money
An AI that tells you NOT to spend more? Now that’s a shopping buddy.

One Mumsnet user asked about more luxurious options. The advice they reported was simple: don’t bother.

Imagine that. A shopping assistant that helps keep your wallet closed.

### 2. Wait—Was That a Sales Pitch?
But then that helpful voice mentions a brand. And suddenly, you wonder: “Whose side are you on?”

In December 2025, some ChatGPT users saw suggestions involving brands like Target and Peloton. They felt like ads. OpenAI said they were unpaid app suggestions.

That distinction matters. A brand name isn’t proof of advertising. But once you suspect a sales pitch, even friendly advice can sound different.

### 3. The Checkout Test
So, don’t ask only, “What should I buy?”

Ask, “Why does this fit my needs? Is there a cheaper option? Do I need it at all?”

Then check the price and seller elsewhere.

The real test isn’t whether your AI can find something to buy.

It’s whether it can still help you buy nothing.
