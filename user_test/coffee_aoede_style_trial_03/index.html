Beans have gotten cheaper! At that news, your wallet pumps its fist. In your mind, the price of your usual cup has already come down, too. But the price tag in a shop may not change right away. When you see a price tag that makes you want to say, Haven't you heard the news?, it seems there is a time lag. The price measure reported by the International Coffee Organization fell 8.7% from the previous month. The reason, it said, was that more beans harvested in Brazil had entered the market, and a good supply was expected to continue. The US Department of Agriculture said that earlier high prices came from several factors happening at once. bad weather in Brazil and Vietnam, fewer beans available for sale, and strong demand.
