# Today’s Diary: Letting My Pet Take Over as Narrator

Whenever I try to write in my diary, my language arts teacher somehow appears in my head. “What did you learn today?” No, I was just tired today. On a night like that, what if my pet started talking for me while I sat frozen in front of a blank page?

TechCrunch reported that Meta’s app Waddle World was released in the Philippines. The app store description says that when you tell it about your day by voice or text, your pet creates a diary in its own voice, along with doodles that look hand-drawn.

In other words, I provide the day’s material, and my pet takes the role of narrator. I’m still the main character, as always. Only the narrator changes. It’s not a major change to my life. It’s more like changing the soundtrack to everyday life a little.

It’s surprisingly appealing. Even on a day when I might just end with, “I got up. I worked. I’m tired. That’s all,” a different voice might make it sound different. It doesn’t have to be a great day. What I’m curious about is, “So how would you tell that ordinary little story?”

When I open my diary, the thought “I have to write this properly” shows up. But if I’m telling my pet about my day, I might be able to start with, “So, today...” It’s a move from the exam room for a writing test to a chat at home. At least, it seems easier than struggling with a blank page.

Do I want good writing? Or do I want time for someone to listen to me talk about my day? They may seem similar, but they’re two different things. What matters to me more than my writing grade is the voice that answers after I finish talking. And how today sounds in that voice.

Of course, this is all based on reading the app store description. Will it be fun to hear, or just awkward? Will I say, “I’ll ask you again tomorrow,” or, “I’ll write it myself today”? I don’t want to give it a star rating based on the description alone. I’ll leave that question open for now.

TechCrunch also mentions that the app could be an early sign of future pet-like devices from Meta. But it does not say that it confirmed any specific connection between the app and a device. It’s too soon to get excited and say, “Next, a buddy in the palm of your hand!”

Let’s leave aside for now whether it will be the next craze after Tamagotchi. What sounds fun to me first is simply changing the diary’s narrator. “You worked hard today.” If that one line comes back in my pet’s voice, I might look forward a little to telling it about tomorrow.

## In one line
Meta’s Waddle World, released in the Philippines, is described as turning a user’s account of the day into a diary in their pet’s voice. That shift could make ordinary days easier to share, though the writer has only read the app store description.
