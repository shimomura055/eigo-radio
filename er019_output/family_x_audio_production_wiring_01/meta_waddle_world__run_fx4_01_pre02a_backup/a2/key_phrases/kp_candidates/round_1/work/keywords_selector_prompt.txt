# Today's Diary Is Written by a Pet

I opened my diary and picked up a pen. But today’s me has not sent in a first draft. “Did anything happen today that is worth writing about?” The editors’ meeting has already reached a dead end before it starts. What if you could give the writing job to your pet?

Meta’s Waddle World is an app for AI pets in the Philippines. Its official app-store page says you can tell it what happened that day by speaking or typing. It then makes a diary from the pet’s point of view, with doodles that look hand-drawn. You give it the story, and the pet does the writing. It is an interesting way to divide the jobs.

Our job is to tell it what happened today. Then the pet, which was listening just moments before, becomes the storyteller. Some days feel too plain for us to write about. But they can change when another storyteller tells them. Suddenly, we want to read on: “So, how did you write it?”

If I said, for example, “I was tired today,” would it become a sad little story? Or would it focus on something I had missed? We do not know what it will actually make. But it is fun to wait and see what kind of diary your day becomes. It is like opening a package.

The hardest part of keeping a diary comes before you start writing. “Is this worth keeping?” “Should I write it up properly?” Even though no one asked it to, the editor in my head starts judging my day. But when you talk to your pet, you can simply start: “Well, about today...” You can begin with a chat, not a finished piece of writing.

The app also adds doodles that look hand-drawn. You might see them as extras beside the writing. But imagine your pet showing you “today’s diary,” and you start wondering about the pictures too. It feels less like checking a record. It feels more like opening a package when you do not know what is inside.

Of course, the official description alone cannot tell us if the writing or pictures are good. There is no promise that every result will be just what you hoped for. The interesting part is not a promise that every result will be good. It is the idea of changing who tells the diary.

You may feel a day is not worth writing about yourself. But you may want to read about it when your pet tells it. The events stay the same, but the reason to open the page changes. You are the main character, and your pet does the writing. For now, the human only has to bring in the story of the day.

## In one line
Meta’s Waddle World is an app offered in the Philippines. It turns a person’s account of the day into an AI pet’s diary, with hand-drawn-style doodles. A day too ordinary to write about yourself may become worth reading when your pet tells it.
