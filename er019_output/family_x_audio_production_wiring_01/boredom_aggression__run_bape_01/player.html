Full Story Part 2 (Aoede)
voice=Charon		Participants who were strongly inclined to enjoy seeing others suffer were more likely to reduce the other person’s reward after watching the stone video than after watching the magician video. Among those with a weaker tendency, there was no clear difference. You might want to dismiss this by saying, “So it really is a matter of personality.” But there’s more to the story. In the next experiment, participants were again randomly assigned to groups. But this time, there was no option to increase the reward. The only choice was to reduce it.
