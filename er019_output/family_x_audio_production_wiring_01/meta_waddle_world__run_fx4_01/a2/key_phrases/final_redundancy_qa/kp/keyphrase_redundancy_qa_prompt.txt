以下は、同じ記事から選ばれた3件のKey Phrase(英語学習ポッドキャストの
リスニング教材)です。3件から作れる全てのペア(3組)について、
それぞれ以下4つの観点で意味重複を判定してください(それぞれPASS=重複なし/
FAIL=重複あり)。

- meaning_overlap: 2つのkey_phraseが表す意味内容が、実質的に同じか(例:
  「注意を引く」と「注意の一部を奪う」は表現は違っても同じ「注意を奪われる」
  という概念を指しており、meaning_overlap=FAILとすべきです)
- usage_context_overlap: 2つの表現が使われる典型的な場面・文脈が、実質的に
  同じか
- grammatical_teaching_value_overlap: 2つの表現が学習者に教える文法・構文上の
  価値(例: 句動詞の型、比喩の型、コロケーションの型)が、実質的に同じか
- conceptual_role_overlap: 記事の中でこの2つの表現が担っている概念・役割が、
  実質的に同じか

is_near_duplicateは、上記4観点のうち2つ以上がFAILの場合、または1つでも
明確にFAILで「単なる言い換え、または同じ内容を別の比喩・表現で表しただけ」
と判断できる場合にtrueにしてください。reasoningには判定理由を1〜2文の
日本語で書いてください。

単に同じ記事・同じ話題について書かれているために表面的な語彙(記事固有の
固有名詞やトピック語)が共通することは、重複とはみなさないでください。
判定対象はあくまで、Key Phraseそのものが教える意味・用法・学習価値です。

pairsには、必ず3組全ての組み合わせを含めてください(rank_a < rank_b)。

# AI Pets Are Free. But Their Owners Still Have Only 24 Hours a Day

Work messages, news, videos, and that app you open without thinking. Your smartphone is already like a packed train at rush hour. What if someone said, “An AI pet is getting on, too”? And it is free. Your wallet nods. But your calendar looks away.

Meta’s “Waddle World” is an AI pet app in the Philippines. It is free in app stores, and its official website says there is no subscription. The stamps you earn in the app cannot be bought with money. It is nice not to have to check a price list.

If you are thinking, “I’ll let my child play with it,” stop here. Both app stores say users must be 18 or older. This time, adults are asked if they want to welcome a pet.

The age labels are a little confusing. Apple says 18+, while Google Play gives it a 3+ content rating. But that rating does not say who can use the app. It does not mean, “OK from age three!” A content rating and the age needed to use an app are different things. Do not skip this just because the pet has a cute face.

If it costs nothing, you might next check how much storage space your phone has. Or maybe how much free time you have. The question is not, “Would I want it if I had to pay?” It is, “Do I want to spend a few minutes with this pet today, too?” Even though it is free, the test for letting it move in is surprisingly hard.

When it comes to usefulness, you can compare features side by side. But “I kind of want to see it” is hard to put in a chart. An AI pet may matter less to adults for what it can do. It may matter more if they want to make room for one during the gaps in a busy day.

Still, it is too soon to get excited about future predictions. At the time of writing, no one had said when it would be available in Japan or other places. TechCrunch has suggested it may be an early step toward future devices like pets. But it does not say that any specific link has been confirmed.

We still do not know if it will become a craze like Tamagotchi. Will “It’s free, so I’ll try it” turn into “I want to see this pet for a little while today, too”? A place to try it out and a seat kept for it every day are different. What is missing from an adult’s smartphone? A new resident? Or a little room in the day to care about someone?

## In one line
Meta’s Waddle World AI pet app is free in the Philippines, but its app stores say users must be 18 or older. Adults may care less about its features than about whether they want to make time for it.
