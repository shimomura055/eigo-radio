# Could Musk’s “Good Idea” Slow Merger Talks?

“If the boss wants it, things move fast.” It is a familiar hope in meetings and business talks. But talks about joining Tesla and SpaceX may bring the opposite result. You think you have pressed the gas, but the brake lights come on. People are starting to worry about this kind of change.

Musk supported joining the two companies. He called it “a good idea.” But he says the choice should be left to special committees at both companies. Do not decide too soon. There are still steps between “he supports it” and “the merger is decided.”

According to Reuters, a person who knows about the talks is worried. They worry that Musk’s support and the public announcement of the talks may make shareholders look more closely. This could slow the talks. Support from a big name does not always make things move ahead quickly. This is not a report that the talks have already slowed.

The louder the support, the more attention it gets. When the star of the show steps into the spotlight, the audience watches more closely. Will people think, “Musk supports it, so we can relax,” or, “Wait, let’s check the details”? What matters is not how loud the support is. It is how others respond.

To begin, special committees at both companies are there to review the merger. Setting them up does not mean the merger is approved. In other words, they have only put out chairs for a meeting to review it. It is too early to set off the party poppers.

Shareholders must also follow certain steps to approve it. For some mergers and other matters, SpaceX’s articles of incorporation set rules. Holders of “Class B” shares must vote as a separate group and approve it. Other required approvals are needed too. Musk’s support is not a pass that opens every gate.

We do not know if talks will really be delayed this time. So watch not only, “Does Musk support it?” but also, “How will others react?” Will cheers help things move forward, or will closer checks make people drive carefully? A big name’s words can fire the starting gun or give people a reason to stop. Since someone has spoken in favor, we must watch others’ next moves in these talks.
