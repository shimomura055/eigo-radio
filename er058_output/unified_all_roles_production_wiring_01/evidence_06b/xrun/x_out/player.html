Hold on before putting the two figures in the ring. So, how long does it take for the price of beans to reach a price tag? The road to a price tag is quite a long journey. The article says that companies in the US that roast coffee keep raw beans in stock. Roasting and packing the beans also take time. They also often negotiate prices with retailers on a regular basis. The price tag in a shop does not flash to a new price the moment the market moves.

voice=Charon		However, the “about nine months” figure is an estimate based on interviews.
