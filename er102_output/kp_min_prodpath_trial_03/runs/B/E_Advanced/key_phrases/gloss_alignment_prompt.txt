各項目について、次を行ってください。
1. current_japanese_glossが、最終的なkey_phrase全体(目的語・補語・修飾語を含むすべての語)の意味と過不足なく一致しているかを判定し(current_gloss_consistent)てください。「～」「〜」を含むかどうかは判定の基準ではありません。key_phrase全体の意味が日本語に表れているかどうかだけで判定してください。
2. 一致している場合は、japanese_glossにcurrent_japanese_glossをそのまま返してください。一致していない場合は、最終key_phrase全体に一致する日本語グロスを返してください。current_japanese_glossが最終key_phrase全体の意味を既に表している場合は、より自然・より適切と思える言い換えがあっても書き換えず、current_japanese_glossをそのまま返してください。書き換えるのは、key_phraseの語の意味が欠けている・誤っている場合だけです。
3. added_content_wordsの各語について、japanese_glossの中でその語の意味を表している部分を、japanese_glossからそのまま切り出してadded_word_mappingsに書いてください(english_wordには対応させる英語の語、複数語をまとめた句でもかまいません。japanese_spanにはjapanese_glossに含まれない文字列や、「～」などの記号だけを書かないでください)。複数の英語語が一つの日本語表現でまとめて表されている場合は、同じ部分を複数の語に対応させてかまいません。added_content_wordsが空の項目は、added_word_mappingsを空配列にしてください。

# A Human Joined an AI Phone Call. The Problem Was the “Cast Change” Notice

I was impressed: “AI can even make phone calls. The future is here!” But the person handling the request on the other end of the line was human. Wait—what a sudden change of genre, from science fiction to a workplace drama. Still, the heart of this story is not “So it was a human after all!” It is whether people were properly told about the change in who was taking part.

Meta was testing some calls made through Muse in which trained contract workers made the calls and completed the conversations with the other person. They tested a setup where Muse handed the work over to a human agent. Not all calls were handled by humans. It’s too soon to put up a caption saying, “Actually, it was all done by people!”

But one important notice was missing from this test. The vice president of Meta’s Superintelligence Labs division admitted in an internal post that starting a test in which contract workers made calls without proper disclosure was a “mistake.” The vice president also said that Meta had rolled back its human concierge feature for now.

Let’s check the breaking-news caption once more. What they pulled back was the human concierge feature. They did not stop all calls through Muse. Canceling an entire show and pausing one segment are two different things.

So, is it bad in itself to bring in a human helper? There may be times when handing things to a person helps move the conversation forward. But there is a big difference between telling someone, “A person will take over from here,” and quietly changing the cast backstage. In a movie, you might clap and say, “Ah, so that’s who it is!” when a mysterious person is revealed. But you may not want a surprise reveal in a conversation you are part of.

Some people may choose their words or feel differently when they think they are talking to AI than when they know they are talking to a person. Even if the request is handled without a problem, that does not mean the disclosure issue can be brushed aside with “it all worked out in the end.” That was exactly the point Meta itself admitted it had got wrong.

Convenience matters. But for some people, knowing who they are talking to matters just as much. If AI hands the baton to a person, that one line saying so is part of the service, too. The person taking over does not have to be a secret guest.
