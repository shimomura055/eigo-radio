# B3Z Story Brief

Storyline: Melos must return to face death in time to save his friend.

Write a 300–350-word story in simple, mainly third-person English for A2 learners. Keep the kingdom setting. Use Melos as the only personal name; refer to others by their roles.

Begin with Melos openly criticizing the ruler for killing suspected enemies without fair trials. The ruler sentences him to death but gives him three days to attend his sister's wedding. His friend stays as a hostage and will die if Melos does not return in time.

After the wedding, Melos races back. Dangerous water and then attackers delay him and leave him weak. Make the time pressure grow through these obstacles. At his lowest point, he nearly collapses and is tempted to stop, even though his friend would die. Keep this difficult choice: trust helps him recover his resolve and continue.

The climax is his arrival just before the execution begins. His friend is freed, and the ruler is changed by their loyalty and mutual trust. End with relief and a sense of the strength of trust. Wedding details and descriptions of the obstacles may be brief, but keep the promise, the temptation, and the renewed effort.
