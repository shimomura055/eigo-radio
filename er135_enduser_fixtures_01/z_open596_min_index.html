"I killed him fairly,"
Tajomaru said. "The wind lifted her face covering, and I wanted her. I promised him buried treasure and led him into the bushes. There I tied him to a cedar tree. She fought with her dagger.
"She held my arm and begged for one of us to die. She would leave with the man who lived. I freed him for a fair sword fight. Twenty-three blows—no other man had lasted twenty! She was gone when he fell. I took his weapons and left."
"I crawled towards my husband,"
Masago said at the temple. "The bandit had left him tied up. I begged him to die with me. His cold eyes said I was nothing, even though I was his wife. "He moved his lips while I held my dagger. 'Kill me,' he said, so I stabbed him. I could not stand those eyes.
"I later tried drowning myself, but I failed. Where can I go after that?"
