## 選定: CP-01

**Core Provocation**: If a workplace can detect mental hesitation before it becomes speech, does refusing to share that signal become a form of professional dishonesty?

**Premise**: In a future workplace, employees use implanted BCIs to control software, speak silently, and receive rapid feedback during meetings. A new kind of manager begins evaluating not only completed work but also neural signals that appear to show hesitation, distraction, or unexpressed disagreement.

**Safety boundary note**: This is a clearly imagined future scenario extrapolating from experimental systems that currently restore speech or computer control for people with severe motor impairments, not a claim about present workplace technology.

**選定理由(LLM自己申告)**: CP-01 is the strongest choice because it turns the demonstrated movement from neural signals to speech and computer control into a vivid social conflict: whether a person owes access to mental hesitation before it becomes communication. It has the greatest combination of excitement, ethical tension, and everyday consequences, while remaining clearly hypothetical and anchored to the current evidence without treating present research as proof that such workplace surveillance already exists.
