[Theme]
the future of language

[Inspiration note -- a starting angle, not a rule to follow literally]
Real-time translation does not just remove a barrier between languages. It can quietly remove the small, deliberate effort of choosing how to say something in someone else's language -- and that effort was often where real connection happened.
