# The House That Learns to Wait

## The Robot at the Edge of the Room

(この段落は想像した未来の場面のため、Fact Check対象から除外されています。)

At first, home help stays close to the easy center of the house: floors, open paths, and clear instructions. If mobile robots became easier to provide, they could move toward laundry, bed making, and tidying. The machine would not simply do more; it would enter rooms filled with habits, memories, and private choices.

## No Two Homes Are Alike

(この段落は想像した未来の場面のため、Fact Check対象から除外されています。)

If this change continued, housework could become less about constant movement and more about checking, correcting, teaching, and choosing what deserves human attention. The most exciting promise is not a silent home with no chores. It is a home where people decide which chores to give away—and which ones still belong to them.
