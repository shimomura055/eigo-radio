## A Home Designed Around Cooperation

The first change is not a spotless house at every moment. It is a new division of attention. Residents prepare the floor, choose where objects belong, and decide when a remote helper should step in. The robot takes the routine route; the person remains the final judge when a home contains something unexpected. Furniture, floor space, and daily plans begin to adjust around a moving helper.

## When Every Home Uses It Differently

(この段落は想像した未来の場面のため、Fact Check対象から除外されています。)

If this spread continued, home robots could make routine care less physical without making housework vanish. Hands-on cleaning might give way to checking routes, moving obstacles, repeating missed tasks, and handling stairs, ceilings, deep dirt, and rooms that machines cannot manage well. A quieter floor would not decide sleep or work on its own; those choices would still depend on the people and routines around the machine. The central tension would be between the hope of a home that quietly maintains itself and the worry that its helper creates a new layer of supervision.

When the robot stops, the quiet house will still be waiting for a human choice.
