## 選定: CP-05

**Core Provocation**: If a home’s memory lives inside its robot, what happens to family knowledge, responsibility, and identity when the machine is sold, hacked, or simply upgraded?

**Premise**: A household robot becomes the keeper of the family’s practical memory: it knows where every item belongs, which clothes are clean, which foods are expiring, and how each person prefers tasks to be done. When the robot is replaced, the family realizes that much of its shared knowledge was stored in the machine rather than in the people.

**Safety boundary note**: This is an imagined future based on robots learning household tasks and operating through persistent perception and planning, not a statement that current home robots possess family memory.

**選定理由(LLM自己申告)**: CP-05 is the single strongest choice because it moves beyond the familiar promise that robots save time and asks what domestic life becomes when the machine stores the household’s accumulated knowledge. It creates personal, emotional, and practical tension at once: dependence, privacy, continuity, replacement, and ownership. It also offers a genuine future leap while remaining defensible as an extrapolation from robots that already learn tasks, recognize objects, and are beginning to operate as general-purpose household systems. It is deliberately not the safest or most cautious option; its power comes from making an ordinary upgrade feel like a possible loss of part of the home itself.
