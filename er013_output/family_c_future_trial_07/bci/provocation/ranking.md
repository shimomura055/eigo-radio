## 代表案選定(決定的計算、ランキング結果のみから算出)

- **A_intimate** 採用: CP-A1(rank 5)
  - core_provocation: If a BCI can speak your intentions before you have chosen your words, who is responsible for what it says?
  - 順位理由: The machine becoming socially authoritative by guessing correctly gives the family conflict sharper immediate drama than CP-B3's more conventional courtroom contest.

- **B_societal** 採用: CP-B3(rank 6)
  - core_provocation: If neural data can support a legal story about what someone intended, who gets to define the difference between an intention and an action?
  - 順位理由: It turns neural ambiguity into a consequential legal question, but its expert-versus-expert courtroom structure is less surprising than CP-A1's intrusion into intimate communication.

- **C_radical** 採用: CP-C3(rank 1)
  - core_provocation: If a person can operate through several authenticated cognitive profiles, which one is the real self?
  - 順位理由: It has the strongest collision of identity, agency, and legal consequence, making it more narratively volatile than the agency crisis in CP-C2.

- 追加レンズ: 採用なし(理由: Neither CP-D1 nor CP-E1 is clearly stronger than the best intimate, societal, and radical candidates; CP-E1 is a close contender, but not strong enough to justify a fourth article.)
