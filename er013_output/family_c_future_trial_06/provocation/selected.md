## 選定: CP-01

**Core Provocation**: When a robot quietly defines what an efficient and well-run home looks like, are the people living in the home—or adapting themselves to the robot’s idea of a good life?

**Premise**: Imagine that a general-purpose home robot becomes the household’s operating system: it schedules meals, cleaning, shopping, room temperatures, and even the timing of family activities. Over time, the home is organized around what the robot can measure, predict, and optimize.

**Safety boundary note**: This is explicitly an imagined future extrapolated from current cleaning automation, household robots, and research into open-ended task planning, not a claim about present-day homes.

**選定理由(LLM自己申告)**: CP-01 is the strongest choice because it turns individual household tasks into a larger question about who sets the standards of everyday life. It offers vivid scenes—schedules, meals, rooms, and family routines—while creating tension without relying on a malfunctioning or hostile robot. The premise can grow plausibly from current cleaning automation, emerging manipulation abilities, and research into robots that learn varied household tasks, yet the central leap remains clearly speculative. It is selected for its excitement and philosophical tension, not because it is the safest or most cautious option.
