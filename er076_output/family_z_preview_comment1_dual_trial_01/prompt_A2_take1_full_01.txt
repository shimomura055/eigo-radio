【これから聞く部分】
Melos called across the hall, “You kill men from fear. You never give them a chance to speak.”

Silence came, and guards grabbed the young shepherd. The ruler ordered his death.

“Give me three days to see my sister’s wedding,” Melos said. “Then I will return to you.”

The ruler smiled and said, “Your friend must stay here. Return late, and he will die in your place.”

Melos turned to his friend, who offered his hands for the chains. Melos had no time to speak.

“Go now, because I know you will come back.”

At the wedding, his sister’s laugh rose above everyone’s voices. Melos listened closely to remember its exact sound.

He left for the city before daylight on day three.
