Tension: Where the Difference Comes From (Aoede, English, A2 slowdown, no heading)
voice=Aoede(EN, A2 slowdown)		The disagreement is not really about moving. It is about the source of stability. One worker finds stability in a desk that stays in one place. Papers and a familiar start help this person. The other finds stability in the power to change who is nearby. The kind of space this person needs can also change each day. A shared-desk system can even lead people to use the same desk often. So a rule may look flexible on paper but feel difficult to one person. The same system may feel useful to another.

Closing: What the Seat Really Means (Aoede, English, A2 slowdown, no heading, In One Line)
voice=Aoede(EN, A2 slowdown)		The real question is not fixed desks or shared desks. It is what makes a place work. One person needs a steady point. It removes the need to search every day. Another needs enough control to change the people nearby and the work space. The seat is only the visible part of a deeper question: What makes a worker feel, “This is my place at work”?
