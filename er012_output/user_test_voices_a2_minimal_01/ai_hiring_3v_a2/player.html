voice=Aoede(EN, A2 slowdown)		All three want the right person in the right job. But each person faces a different problem. The applicant may lose a fair chance. The recruiter must keep work moving and explain the system. The owner must keep the company running and answer for the choice. The applicant cannot choose the system. The recruiter runs it and may help with the decision to use it. The owner may make the final choice. Rules outside the company limit all three roles. In New York City, an employer must have a recent fairness check. It must also give the public a simple summary and give notice before using an automated hiring tool. The recruiter cannot use it in secret. The owner cannot rely on speed alone. The applicant still cannot control the score. One company stopped a tool after it gave women lower scores. These different views do not give one easy answer.
